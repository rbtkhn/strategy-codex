# Priority Printing Press CLIs

Printing Press is a candidate Tier-1 tooling layer. The order below reflects Strategy-Codex fit, governance risk, and expected local proof value.

## Pilot sequence

| Rank | CLI | Status | Primary use | Safety note |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `scrape-creators` | Implemented pilot | Public YouTube transcript/video metadata intake for the cognition stream | Public YouTube only in v1; no comments, cookies, DMs, or social graph harvesting |
| 2 | `company-goat` | Next proof point | Company, competitor, market, and strategy intelligence | Keep as research input; corroborate before strong claims |

Tier-1 language becomes appropriate only after both pilots produce useful local receipts.

## Near candidates

| CLI | Primary use | Safety note |
|---|---|---|
| `hackernews` | Weak-signal and tooling trend scans | Low credential risk; useful for context-efficiency comparison |
| `nvd` | Vulnerability and resilience monitoring | Read-only security source; good for work-dev risk posture |
| `archive-is` | Web snapshot and source preservation | Check legal/ToS posture and avoid private pages |
| `firecrawl` | Web acquisition and page extraction | Treat as scraping; require source and rate-limit discipline |

## Higher-friction candidates

| CLI | Primary use | Extra review |
|---|---|---|
| `agent-capture` | Evidence capture, screenshots, local proof artifacts | May touch local UI/private context; require capture-boundary checklist |
| `linear` | Issue tracking and blocker analysis | Requires account auth and may mutate workflow state |
| `ahrefs` | SEO/source visibility research | Likely credentialed; keep outside v1 unless account policy is clear |
| `yahoo-finance` | Market data scans | Good read-only candidate, but financial outputs need timestamp/source clarity |

## Admission rule

Every new PP CLI starts as a pilot dossier plus a portable skill draft. Do not treat generated skills, MCPs, or binaries as admitted just because Printing Press can generate them.
