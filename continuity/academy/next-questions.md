## Current Questions

**Theology:** When does sacred language reveal real moral pressure, and when does it excuse command, violence, or institutional self-protection?

**Military:** When does operational explanation clarify responsibility, and when does it make violence look cleaner than it is?

**Politics:** How do regimes convert legitimacy, party organization, media, and institutional veto points into durable power?

**Statecraft:** How should decision-makers act when external constraint, alliance pressure, intelligence uncertainty, and domestic politics pull against each other?

**Singularity:** What happens to agency, alignment, and human meaning when technical systems begin acting faster than institutions can understand?
