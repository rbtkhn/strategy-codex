# Empire And Sacred Order
<!-- word_count: 251 -->


## Purpose

Study how empire borrows, carries, restrains, or corrupts sacred order. The central problem is not whether theology enters politics; it is what political power does once it speaks sacred language.

## Working Questions

- What is being sacralized: ruler, law, people, land, church, mission, sacrifice, victory?
- Does sacred order restrain power or decorate it?
- How do New Rome and Holy Roman Europe differ as Christian-imperial forms?
- Where does continuity become a claim of innocence?
- What happens to dissenters, outsiders, and defeated enemies?

## Counter-Reading

Secular anti-empire can miss the ordering function sacred institutions actually performed. The test is not "religion bad, empire bad"; the test is whether sacred form limits power, legitimates power, or does both at once.
