## Working Questions

- What is the providential claim?
- What historical pressures make the claim effective?
- Who gains authority when events are described as providence?
- What suffering is explained too quickly?
- What responsibility remains after pressure is named?

## Counter-Reading

Anti-providence can become its own simplification. Refusing divine authorization does not mean sacred belief is epiphenomenal or fake. It may be one of the strongest pressures in the field.
