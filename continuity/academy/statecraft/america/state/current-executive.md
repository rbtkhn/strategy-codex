## Role In Current State Form

The current U.S. executive is the first visible settlement carrier for crisis diplomacy, sanctions, military command, emergency declarations, and public legitimacy. The office is powerful but not sufficient by itself: Congress, courts, military command, states, allies, and successor legitimacy can determine whether an instrument survives.

## Authority Base

- Formal authority: executive power, commander-in-chief role, sanctions administration, diplomacy, emergency powers.
- Informal authority: party control, media command, donor networks, public mandate, crisis narrative.
- Security / military authority: operational command through lawful military channels.
- Coalition authority: congressional majority, allied confidence, bureaucratic compliance.
- Symbolic authority: claim to defend the republic, allies, maritime order, and national dignity.

## Institutional Carrier

Durable instruments need more than presidential signature. Prefer treaty organs, congressional notice or authorization, allied burden-sharing mechanisms, statutory review, and military deconfliction channels.

## Domestic Coalition

The executive must manage Congress, military leadership, allied governments, security hawks, war-fatigue voters, markets, and any successor-authority coalition if federal legitimacy degrades.

## Strategic Temperament

America-facing instruments should expect pressure for visible resolve. The drafting problem is to make limited restraint appear as bounded command rather than weakness.

## Red Lines

Terms that look like rewarding coercion, abandoning allies, conceding hostile veto power, or bypassing constitutional authority can break domestic carrying capacity.

## Settlement Credibility

- Can sign: executive agreements, sanctions waivers, emergency coordination, diplomatic frameworks.
- Can implement: military posture, sanctions administration, allied coordination, backchannels.
- Needs support for durability: Congress, allies, courts, military command, and successor-stable institutional design.

## Succession / Continuity Risk

High. America lane instruments should ask whether the compact survives election, impeachment, civil conflict, emergency drift, or reconstitution of authority.

## Binding Instrument Test

Can this executive express durable American state interest, or is the instrument only a personal or factional move? If the latter, add congressional, allied, judicial, or institutional carriers before treating it as statecraft.
