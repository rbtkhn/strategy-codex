## Family Claim

A settlement becomes real when it changes who can carry order after the initiating crisis passes.

Across all four lanes, the common test is not moral aspiration, rhetorical closure, or momentary de-escalation. The test is whether the bargain creates:

- a recognizable political end
- a bounded enforcement or guarantor carrier
- an implementation path that later institutions can still own
- a public or civilizational grammar that does not recode the bargain as humiliation, tutelage, or fraud

A settlement remains theatrical when it borrows the language of peace while leaving the real carrying burden unresolved.

## Cross-Lane Matrix

| Lane | What makes settlement real | What makes settlement theatrical |
| --- | --- | --- |
| America | Lawful domestic carry, allied burden-sharing, review clocks, and a successor-stable ownership map | Executive theater, permission-based relief, endless supervisory discretion, or peace that later institutions never truly own |
| Russia | Recognized equilibrium, reciprocal consultation, bounded security geometry, and protection of third-party sovereignty | Symbolic recognition without real parity, buffer logic without limits, ambiguity with no review path, or peace that feels like managed humiliation |
| China | Face-preserving calm, routable implementation, quiet ownership discipline, and material continuity through ordinary carriers | Harmony language with hidden burdens, visible tutelage, public ownership of another actor's disorder, or peace that only works while summit attention is high |
| Iran | Dignity-preserving recognition, visible relief before irreversible surrender of leverage, reciprocal verification, and successor-stable sovereign carry | Honor without relief, relief without protection, inspection as abasement, or a bargain that later carriers can only describe as dependency in softer language |

## Shared Structural Rule

The four lanes converge on one structural rule:

- settlement is real only when **recognition** and **carry** meet

Recognition alone is not enough. Every lane has a way to falsify symbolic inclusion:

- America rejects peace that sounds lawful but leaves ownership hidden in executive or alliance management
- Russia rejects peace that sounds consultative but leaves real interpretation under another power's custodianship
- China rejects peace that sounds harmonious but quietly assigns Beijing visible responsibility for a disorder it cannot fully control
- Iran rejects peace that sounds respectful but leaves relief discretionary and review humiliating

Carry alone is not enough either. Pure management can still be theatrical if the political end remains unreadable:

- America needs the republic to understand why it owns the peace
- Russia needs the settlement to read as equilibrium rather than tutelage
- China needs calm to preserve face and anti-disorder legitimacy
- Iran needs restraint to preserve dignity and sovereign room

That means the family-level synthesis is sharper than "peace needs institutions." It is:

- peace needs **recognized institutions**
- and recognition needs to be **institutionally survivable**

## Enforcement / Guarantor Comparison

The family also clarifies that enforcement is not one thing across the set.

- **America** wants an enforcement carrier that is lawful, reviewable, and shareable with allies.
- **Russia** wants a guarantor structure that preserves reciprocal status and does not collapse into one-sided interpretation.
- **China** wants implementation that works through dispersed quiet carriers rather than through visible public custodianship.
- **Iran** wants guarantees, relief, and verification that do not read as foreign management of Iranian political life.

So the set-wide rule is:

- a guarantor makes settlement real only when the guarantor's role can be described by the lane as protection rather than domination

That is the cross-lane hinge connecting civilization and empire. The empire-side carrier makes the settlement governable; the civilization-side grammar decides whether that governability still counts as peace.

## Successor-Carry Comparison

All four lanes also converge on successor stability, but each means something slightly different:

- **America:** later Congresses, allies, courts, and administrations can keep owning the peace without theatrically rediscovering its legality.
- **Russia:** later carriers can describe the peace as ordinary equilibrium rather than as delayed surrender or frozen domination.
- **China:** ministries, ports, firms, provinces, banks, and diplomatic carriers can keep administering calm without fresh summit theater.
- **Iran:** supreme, elected, diplomatic, parliamentary, clerical, and security carriers can all keep calling the peace sovereign rather than humiliating.

The family lesson is that successor carry is not an optional refinement. It is the point at which settlement crosses from event to order.

## Strongest Shared Falsifiers

Across the set, a settlement is still theatrical when one or more of these conditions hold:

1. the public language says peace, but the ownership map is hidden
2. the other side is asked to trust enforcement or relief that remains discretionary
3. later carriers would have to improvise the meaning of the bargain after the initiating coalition is gone
4. the room design leaves one side symbolically included but materially managed
5. the guarantor or enforcement carrier cannot be described as legitimate by the civilization-side grammar that must inherit it

These falsifiers travel well across the lanes because they are really different forms of the same failure:

- the settlement speaks before it can carry

## Drafting Implications

For the helix-first corpus, this family now gives a reusable drafting test:

1. What is the recognizable political end?
2. Who carries enforcement, review, or implementation?
3. Can those carriers still own the bargain after the initiating crisis passes?
4. Can the lane's civilization-side grammar describe that ownership as legitimate?
5. What would make the same bargain look like tutelage, humiliation, fraud, or empty choreography instead?

If a draft cannot answer those questions, it may still pause violence, but it has not yet become a real settlement in the stronger cross-lane sense.
