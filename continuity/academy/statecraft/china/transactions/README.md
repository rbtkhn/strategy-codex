# China Statecraft Transactions
<!-- word_count: 215 -->

This surface is non-authoritative and subject to revision.

This bench spends compact speaker-state into China-lane instruments. Use the canonical [Statecraft Transaction Template](../../templates/statecraft-transaction.md) for any reusable transaction.

## Transaction Bias

China transactions should prioritize anti-disorder compacts, energy and supply-chain stability, quiet leverage, infrastructure or finance mechanisms, and diplomatic credit without imperial policing burdens.

Each transaction should separate:

- current PRC party-state policy;
- deeper Chinese state capacity and civilizational continuity;
- industrial geography and maritime exposure;
- successor-stable interests in energy, trade routes, and technological continuity.

## Spendable Inputs

- Pape for shock clocks, coercive leverage, and systemic spillover.
- Mearsheimer for balancing pressure and regional-hegemon constraints.
- Jiang for China-facing strategic framing.
- Sachs for economic system effects.
- Crooke / Diesen for order transition.
- Parsi for regional architecture and off-ramps.

## Preferred Transaction Families

- Gulf transit compacts;
- LNG and energy-stability arrangements;
- yuan settlement or insurance alternatives;
- infrastructure guarantees;
- regional security forums;
- quiet pressure and face-saving de-escalation channels.

## Validity Questions

- Does the instrument reduce energy disorder without restoring uncontested U.S. regional control?
- Does it preserve Iran's usefulness without owning Iran's escalation?
- Does it accidentally make India the more responsible stabilizer?
- What mechanism lets China stabilize without demanding public credit?
