# Military Workshop Method
<!-- word_count: 303 -->

The method is practical: use campaign literature, theory, and writer lanes to produce disciplined military judgment without confusing narrative with evidence.

## Pattern

**Campaign before vibes.** Start with the actual problem: theater, front, objective, forces, logistics, terrain, time, and political purpose.

**Logistics before drama.** A vivid narrative is not a supply line. Ask what can move, what can be replaced, what breaks, and what rate of loss is sustainable.

**Command before abstraction.** Do not let "the system" erase agency. Identify commanders, institutions, orders, permissions, refusals, and alternatives.

**Primary before interpretation.** Separate official claims, wire reports, imagery, maps, ORBAT work, writer essays, and operator synthesis.

**Politics before victory language.** Tactical destruction is not strategic success unless it produces a political result.

**Faces inside maps.** Units, fronts, populations, and states are necessary abstractions, but they can become blades. Restore the concrete human reality before turning analysis into recommendation.

## Sheet Template

Use this structure for new sheets:

1. Purpose
2. Bookshelf anchors
3. Writer-lane companions
4. Working questions
5. Counter-reading
6. Output prompts

## Note Template

Use this compact form when the workshop produces a note:

- **Claim:** the campaign or strategy claim being tested.
- **Evidence lane:** primary source, official claim, imagery/map, ORBAT, writer interpretation, or operator inference.
- **Constraint:** logistics, time, terrain, politics, industry, morale, or command.
- **Responsibility:** who chose, ordered, enabled, resisted, or suffered.
- **Bridge check:** face/category, sacred legitimacy, or pressure and agency when needed.
- **Reusable output:** warning, instrument amendment, source question, or Bookshelf pairing.

## Output Rule

Every workshop output should answer at least one of these:

- What is the campaign problem?
- What is the logistics constraint?
- What is the political purpose?
- What command responsibility remains?
- What evidence lane supports the claim?
- What abstraction is hiding a face?
