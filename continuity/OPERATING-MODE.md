# continuity/ — operating mode
<!-- word_count: 260 -->

End-of-day strategy session and daily continuity workflow.

## Default day (EOD compose)

1. Capture in [daily-strategy-inbox.md](daily-strategy-inbox.md) and [`source-archive/statecraft/`](../source-archive/statecraft/README.md) as needed.
2. Choose **one** thesis (or explicit carry).
3. In the **end-of-day strategy session**, compose **one** `chapters/YYYY-MM/days.md` day block (minimum sections per [NOTEBOOK-PREFERENCES](NOTEBOOK-PREFERENCES.md)).
4. Add **zero or one** new or revised `strategy-page` in time-scoped thread files under `continuity/years/2026/<channel>/`.
5. **Stop.**

Full `strategy` pass: [DEFAULT-PATH.md](../docs/skill-work/work-strategy/DEFAULT-PATH.md).

## Normal use

Capture through the day; once per day (default) run **`strategy page`** or **`strategy page compose`**. Read-only orient: **`strategy page read`** — no `days.md` / `strategy-page` edits.

**Strategy Console (derived):** [strategy-console/](strategy-console/README.md) — `python3 scripts/strategy_console.py --mode eod` for orientation only.

## Date hygiene

`Accumulator for:` and inbox batch labels **do not** mandate new `days.md` headings. See [STRATEGY-NOTEBOOK-ARCHITECTURE.md § days.md date keys](STRATEGY-NOTEBOOK-ARCHITECTURE.md#days-md-date-semantics) and [strategy-notebook-days-date-semantics.mdc](../.cursor/rules/strategy-notebook-days-date-semantics.mdc).

## Primary written unit

**`strategy-page`** — marker-fenced analysis in time-scoped thread files under `continuity/years/2026/<channel>/`. Profiles at `continuity/profiles/<channel>-profile.md`. `chapters/YYYY-MM/days.md` holds time and continuity; threads hold substance.
