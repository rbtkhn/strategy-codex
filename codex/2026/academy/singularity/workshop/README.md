# Singularity Workshop

WORK only; not Record.

The Singularity Workshop is the active-use layer of academy singularity. It begins with agency under acceleration, not a 25-book canon.

## Workflow

1. **Name the acceleration.** Identify what is becoming faster, cheaper, more autonomous, more scalable, or more recursive.
2. **Name the agent.** Distinguish human, model, tool, institution, workflow, market, and hybrid system.
3. **Test alignment.** Ask what objective is being optimized, who set it, who can inspect it, and who can stop it.
4. **Test substrate.** Ask where memory, authority, identity, permissions, receipts, and rollback live.
5. **Test displacement.** Ask what skill, role, institution, market, or meaning-structure is being weakened or transformed.
6. **Produce reuse.** End with an agency map, alignment warning, substrate boundary, human-meaning note, or bridge question.

## First Instruments To Build

- **Agency Under Acceleration Map:** What can now act faster or at larger scale?
- **Alignment Surface Check:** What objective controls behavior, and who audits it?
- **Substrate Boundary Test:** Where does authority live, and what must not be absorbed?
- **Recursive Improvement Watch:** What feedback loop improves the system without enough human sensemaking?
- **Human Meaning Check:** What role, dignity, attention, or relationship is being transformed?

## Boundary

This workshop may generate WORK notes. It does not replace AI news capture, model benchmarks, product-release tracking, raw-input, speaker folders, technical specs, or Record surfaces.
