## Boundaries

Do not use this speaker arc as:

- a substitute for the broader `thread:wilkerson` continuity record
- a wire-grade verifier for exact battle damage, shipping losses, or strike-count arithmetic
- a replacement for the Diesen Wilkerson arc when the question is primarily long-horizon order consequence
- a claim that Wilkerson's host-stream rhetoric equals a settled institutional position

This arc is strongest as a **live-pressure imperial warning form**: Alkorshid pushes the crisis forward, Wilkerson translates it into statecraft collapse, force-limit warning, and hegemonic self-destruction.
