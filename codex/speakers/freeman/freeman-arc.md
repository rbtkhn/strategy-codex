## What makes this an arc

Freeman now clearly has more than a helix. The continuity is broad enough that the notebook keeps encountering the same underlying voice across several recurring jobs:

- diplomatic-memory reading of current crisis
- legality and legitimacy diagnosis under hegemonic stress
- fake-negotiation and no-authority-process critique
- alliance-subordination and imperial self-damage analysis

The strongest current motion is:

`late-2025 legality and hegemony warning -> fake diplomacy and Israeli capture -> Iran leverage and Hormuz pressure -> domestic and strategic decay merge -> China's quiet gain`

That motion is why Freeman now deserves a person arc rather than only a helix plus routing note.
