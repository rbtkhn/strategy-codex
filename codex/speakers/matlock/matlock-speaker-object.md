# Matlock speaker object

WORK only; not Record.

object_shape: single-helix

Purpose: define Jack Matlock as a first-class but light speaker object in the shelf. The current direct corpus is a mature Diesen-anchored guest run, not a broad cross-host interview helix. Its value is diplomatic memory: it anchors current Russia/NATO/Ukraine arguments in the lived practice of ending the Cold War and the later failure to build an inclusive European security architecture.

## Core claim

Matlock matters in the Codex as a carrier of U.S. diplomatic-memory witness:

- negotiated end of the Cold War, not victory mythology
- Reagan-era negotiation method: trust-building, private pressure, cooperation language, and refusal to humiliate the other side
- Bush-era German unification and the NATO-expansion promise dispute
- NATO expansion plus U.S. basing as the central post-Cold War security rupture
- Ukraine as a tragic civil-conflict and proxy-escalation case, not a simple morality play

This makes him most useful as a settlement-architecture object: he preserves how off-ramps were once built and how later policy choices destroyed the conditions for them.
