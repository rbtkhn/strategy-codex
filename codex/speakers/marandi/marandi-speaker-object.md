## Core claim

Marandi is a real speaker object because the notebook repeatedly encounters the same underlying voice across distinct hosts:

- a Tehran-facing legitimacy and red-line register
- a recurring explanation of retaliation, endurance, and compellence limits
- a stable ability to translate immediate crisis incidents into arguments about sovereignty, siege, and regional order

His strength is not primarily immense native-stream depth. It is the persistence of one recognizably Marandi voice across several host environments, thick enough to support speaker-level continuity.

Marandi carries a distinct utility profile:

- legitimacy register
  - one of the notebook's strongest recurring voices for how a regime-adjacent Iranian lane narrates victimhood, red lines, proportionality, and endurance
- translation value
  - repeatedly renders internal Iranian or Tehran-facing logic into durable English-language formulations
- host-transformability
  - remains legible under higher-order Diesen framing, operational-limit Davis framing, and high-pressure Dialogue Works framing
- regional-order pressure
  - especially useful when the notebook needs to understand how Hormuz, GCC exposure, ceasefire conditionality, reparations, and anti-compellence claims are staged from the Iranian side
