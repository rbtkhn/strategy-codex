## Why this guest run matters

Alexander Mercouris matters here as a diplomatic-sequencing and institutional-politics voice paired with Davis's force-feasibility frame. The lane is useful when the notebook needs continuity between:

- U.S. or NATO policy intent and operational sustainability
- Ukraine settlement rhetoric and battlefield attrition logic
- European political will and European material capacity
- Iran/Hormuz escalation and the way a second war drains Ukraine-war resources
- Davis's military-practical skepticism and Mercouris's statecraft / institutional register

What makes this a real host-local arc is the repeated fit:

- Davis asks whether the proposed policy can be executed, supplied, manned, and sustained.
- Mercouris answers through negotiation incentives, institutional brittleness, sanctions blowback, and political legitimacy.
- The lane turns great-power commentary into a test of whether Western policy has a usable exit ramp or only an escalation script.
