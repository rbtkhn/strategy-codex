## Why this guest run matters

Kent matters inside the Davis stream because Davis gives him the most operationally concrete setting: the question is not only whether the Iran war is wrong, but whether it can produce any coherent military-political end state.

The lane is useful because:

- Davis frames the Iran war through force feasibility, U.S. munitions strain, Hormuz pressure, and exit choices
- Kent supplies the veteran/intelligence-community dissent voice: no imminent threat, Israeli/neocon pressure, and Trump trapped between public victory language and private exit need
- the pairing turns "walk away" from a slogan into a repeated test of what a president can still do once coercion fails to win concessions
