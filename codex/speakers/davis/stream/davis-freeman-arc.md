Purpose: compact reuse note for the `Davis x Freeman` run inside the Davis stream. This is not a substitute for the raw-input files. It exists so future routing can quickly answer: what kind of Freeman lane Davis elicits, which files matter most, and where the lane changes shape between the older Ukraine shelf and the later Iran shelf.

## Why this guest run matters

Chas Freeman matters here less as a generic diplomatic elder than as a recurring statecraft-sobriety voice inside a host stream otherwise centered on military practicality, escalation pressure, and force-posture realism.

Inside the Davis stream, that makes the run especially useful when the notebook needs continuity between:

- operational military talk and actual political settlement possibilities
- coercive escalation and diplomatic self-sabotage
- battlefield loss and what cannot be negotiated back
- Iran-war rhetoric and the collapse of strategic objectives

What makes this a real speaker arc is that the same host x guest shape keeps returning:

- Davis frames the crisis through force feasibility, executive choices, and war pressure
- Freeman translates that crisis into diplomatic limits, strategic incoherence, and the costs of forgetting how statecraft works
- the lane repeatedly turns war talk into an argument about what cannot be recovered by coercion once objectives are unclear, diplomacy is degraded, and political end states have gone missing

## Arc shape

This lane has two distinct phases on disk, and they should be read together rather than collapsed into one undifferentiated shelf:

- a `2025` Ukraine settlement proto-lane
- a `2026` Iran-centered diplomatic-collapse lane

The 2025 files establish the earlier Davis x Freeman shape: negotiation realism, battlefield loss, and war-termination sobriety. The 2026 files transform that older shape into an Iran-war lane centered on diplomatic collapse, strategic incoherence, and the mismatch between bombing and political success.

## Orthogonality guide

Quick separation rule:

- `Davis x Freeman` is the settlement-realism and diplomatic-collapse lane.
- `Diesen x Freeman` is the order-transition and maritime-order lane.
- `Dialogue Works x Freeman` is the live crisis-pressure and phony-ceasefire lane.
- `Napolitano x Freeman` is the legal-moral legitimacy and anti-war-pressure lane.

Do not use this arc as a shortcut for Freeman's maritime-order altitude, live crisis tempo, or legal-moral critique. Its distinctive job is to show what coercion cannot negotiate back once diplomacy and political end states have degraded.
