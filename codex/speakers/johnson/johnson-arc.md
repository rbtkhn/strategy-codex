- reading crises through intelligence-style pressure interpretation
- translating escalation theater into leverage loss and coercive failure
- widening local war into Gulf, regional, and world-order consequence
- distinguishing what force threatens from what policy can realistically obtain

The strongest current motion is:

`early-2025 pressure and coercion skepticism -> host-differentiated Iran/Hormuz reading -> live humiliation of escalation -> denser 2026 failed-coercion continuity`

That motion is why Johnson now deserves a person arc rather than only a helix.
