WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: preserve the six recurring arc-threads in Larry Johnson's dense April-May 2026 run so the wider Johnson arc is routeable as a synthesis, not only as scattered host appearances.

Model note: this file is the **canonical Johnson recurring-strand atlas** for the dense 2026 core, not a host-local arc by itself. It names the recurring thematic strands that the month spines and host-local arcs braid differently.

Compatibility note: the older [johnson-thread.md](../johnson-thread.md) file remains a legacy continuity surface from the older expert-thread machinery. It is not a competing canonical Johnson thread system.

## Orthogonality guide

These arc-threads should be read as **distinct recurring strands inside one Johnson run**, not as six stylistic variations of the same anti-coercion conclusion.

Quick separation rule:

- `coercion fails to recover leverage` = pressure tools keep firing, but political and strategic leverage keep shrinking
- `military threats outrun political attainability` = force can threaten more than policy can realistically obtain
- `Hormuz and Gulf exposure turn pressure back on the U.S. camp` = maritime and Gulf-system vulnerability rebound against Washington and partners
- `Israeli/U.S. escalation produces humiliation rather than control` = prestige and deterrence displays become public embarrassment
- `crisis management turns into contradiction, confusion, and process absurdity` = ceasefires, blockades, and White House signaling stop lining up coherently
- `regional and systemic consequence widen beyond the local war frame` = the war starts redrawing wider political, financial, and regional-order structures

If a new Johnson item could sit in several of these without changing how the operator would use it, prefer the narrower parent strand or keep it at month or host level instead of broadening the atlas.

`coercive contradiction -> leverage collapse -> Hormuz rebound -> humiliation tempo -> process absurdity -> wider regional consequence`
