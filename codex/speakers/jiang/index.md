## Boundary

This index is a route map. The profile owns the routing law and maturity warning; the thread and transcript hold current working surfaces; any future strengthening should come from materially embodied Jiang-specific routing evidence, not from the importance of the ideas alone.

Do not treat the presence of `jiang-thread.md` as permission to proliferate new topical thread files. Under the current orthogonality rule, any new thread should exist only when it carries a distinct recurring object, mechanism, and retrieval use.
