## Host transformations

- `Carlson`: turns Kent into a public witness. The question is whether the stated imminent-threat theory, Israeli influence channel, and administration debate process can survive direct inside-system testimony.
- `Davis`: turns Kent into a military-feasibility and walkaway voice. The question is whether the Iran war can be won in any useful political sense and how Trump can exit without further escalation.
- `Diesen`: turns Kent into a historical and structural voice. The question is how Iraq, Syria, ISIS, Israel influence, and U.S. institutional continuity produced the present Iran trap.
- `Nawfal`: turns Kent into a political-pressure voice. The question is why Trump, despite anti-war instincts and campaign promises, remains stuck in a war he appears not to want.
- `Barnes`: does not provide a full conversational host lane, but confirms that Kent's framing became a portable exit-ramp signal in adjacent domestic-legal commentary.
