## Why this guest run matters

John Mearsheimer matters here less as a generic realist brand than as a highly reusable structural-realist voice for:

- great-power geometry and offensive realism
- NATO expansion and the security dilemma
- the limits of military power against durable regional actors
- the way local wars plug into wider order transition

Inside the Diesen stream, that makes the run especially useful when the notebook needs continuity between:

- Diesen's Eurasia and multipolarity framing
- Mearsheimer's structural-realist architecture
- specific theaters like Ukraine, Iran, and NATO decline
