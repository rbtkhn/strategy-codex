## Phase Spine

**Palestine / Gaza implementation and Israel pressure, September-November 2025.** Parsi starts the prelude by rejecting symbolic politics without implementation. Palestine recognition only matters if it becomes architecture, and a Gaza deal only works if Trump keeps pressure on Israel. The regional environment is already shifting away from Washington's Iran-centered story.

**Netanyahu escalation demand and U.S. entanglement, December 2025.** The lane turns toward Israel as an escalation engine. Netanyahu is framed as demanding another Iran war, while U.S. politics risks converting Israeli objectives into American commitments.

**Trump caution, Iran despair, and coercive fantasy, January-February 2026.** Parsi treats Trump as likely to seek caution, bargaining, or exit, but also argues U.S. policy has produced despair inside Iran. The key warning is that even a small attack is not small in Iranian strategic terms; it opens escalation.

**Theocracy resilience, unilateral victory, and off-ramp destruction, March 2026.** Parsi rejects collapse assumptions, argues Iran can refuse to validate Trump's exit narrative, and treats targeting of Larijani as narrowing the diplomatic space. Iran's leverage is political and procedural as much as military.

**Ceasefire / Hormuz bargaining and blockade brinkmanship, April 2026.** The ceasefire becomes a bargaining framework around Hormuz, sanctions relief, and settlement durability. Trump may regain short-term leverage, but blockade threats risk economic self-harm and undermine the better exit path.

**Blockade failure, supremacy illusion, and regional security architecture, May 2026.** The latest authored sequence argues Trump squandered a favorable ceasefire by following a blockade logic, exposed limits of U.S. military supremacy, and faced an Iranian proposal that looked like an unlabeled grand bargain for regional security architecture.
