# Parsi helix

WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: compact note for reading Parsi's authored diplomacy mechanism together with his interview-pressure appearances. This is not a substitute for the forecast ledger, the interview map, the person arc, or the thread atlas.

## Core claim

The Parsi helix is a **two-track policy-process helix**:

- the authored track states the diplomacy mechanism
- the interview track tests that mechanism under host pressure

This is not the same shape as a dense host-transformation shelf. Parsi's stronger structure is authored depth plus targeted interview pressure.

The helix matters because it prevents the notebook from treating his interviews as loose commentary or his essays as isolated papers.

## Orthogonality guide

Quick separation rule:

- The `authored diplomacy track` is where Parsi states settlement architecture, sanctions logic, and implementation-heavy policy mechanism in his own terms.
- The `interview-pressure track` is where that mechanism is pressed by hosts into questions of blame, leverage, off-ramp design, blockade failure, and primacy interpretation.

Do not use the interview layer as a substitute for Parsi's authored policy architecture when drafting statecraft language, and do not use the authored ledger as a substitute for interview-pressure translation when the question is how his diplomacy logic behaves in live argument.
