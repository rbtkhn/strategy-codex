# Parsi cross-year note

WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: clarify where the mature Parsi object really begins and how the shelf should treat `2025-present` continuity without pretending that all captured material has equal arc weight.

## Governing window

The governing continuity window is `2025-present`.

That window is correct for ownership and continuity, but the shelf should distinguish three layers inside it:

1. early authored and interview anchors
2. maturing 2025-2026 recurrence
3. mature 2026 cross-context core

## Early anchors

The 2025 material matters because it shows that Parsi is not a one-month Iran-war object.

Those files establish:

- a durable authored diplomacy mechanism
- early host-distributed interview reinforcement
- pre-2026 continuity for implementation, pressure on Israel, and regional architecture

They should be treated as continuity anchors, not as the sole statement of the mature object.

## Maturing recurrence

By late 2025 and early 2026, the shelf begins to show a clearer two-track structure:

- authored diplomacy mechanism thickens
- interview-pressure reinforcement becomes more legible across multiple hosts

This is where the Parsi object starts becoming reusable as a true cross-context speaker shelf.

## Mature core

The mature center of gravity is 2026, especially March through May 2026.

That is where the shelf becomes dense enough to support:

- a canonical person arc
- a recurring thread atlas
- bounded cross-context month surfaces
- a stable theme-family layer

## Boundary

- `2025-present` is the correct continuity window.
- 2026 is the current mature recurring core, with March and May as the clearest cross-context pressure points.
- Early anchors remain important, but they should not be overstated into a false equivalence with the denser 2026 run.
