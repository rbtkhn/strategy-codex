# Strategy-codex template - book
<!-- word_count: canonical scaffold -->

WORK only; not Record.

**Purpose:** Canonical book template for strategy-codex. A **book** is the month-bounded synthesis and coordination surface inside a year volume.

**Companion contracts:** [NOTEBOOK-CONTRACT.md](NOTEBOOK-CONTRACT.md) · [STRATEGY-NOTEBOOK-ARCHITECTURE.md](STRATEGY-NOTEBOOK-ARCHITECTURE.md)

## Book role

- **Volume** = year (`codex/YYYY/`)
- **Book** = month (`book-YYYY-MM.md` or month meta/index surface)
- **Chapter** = day (`chapters/YYYY-MM/days.md` block)
- **Page** = analytical unit handled separately

The book should coordinate the month's chapters, open themes, and synthesis trajectory without replacing chapter or page work.

## Book scaffold -> `book-YYYY-MM.md`

# Strategy-codex book - `YYYY-MM`

WORK only; not Record.

**Use it for:**

- month theme / operating frame
- chapter index or orientation
- carry-forward tensions
- month-scale synthesis notes

**Skeleton:**

```markdown
# Book YYYY-MM
WORK only; not Record.

## Theme

## Chapter index

## Carry-forward tensions

```

**Rule of use:** the book is a month coordination surface, not a place to restate every chapter or page. Keep it thin and directional.

## Relationship to chapters and pages

- Chapters do the daily continuity work.
- Pages do the focused analytical work.
- Books keep the month legible across those smaller units.
