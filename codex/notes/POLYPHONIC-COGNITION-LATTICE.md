# Polyphonic Cognition Lattice
<!-- word_count: 140 -->

WORK only; not Record.

**Doctrine:** a polyphonic cognition lattice is the notebook pattern that emerges when multiple expert lanes keep their own voice, but recurring markers line up across them.

## Access-layer discipline

The lattice is downstream of access.

That means:

- discovery adapters are not lattice voices
- ingestion receipts are not themselves interpretive lines
- repair queues are not the field itself
- coverage scores are visibility aids, not judgment substitutes

The lattice begins only after the field is visible enough to hear distinct voices without confusing absence, scrape failure, or undercapture for genuine silence.

## What recent ingest failures taught

The April 2026 month-benchmark failure clarified an important boundary:

- the fragile layer is **how the notebook reaches the field**
- the durable layer is **how the notebook preserves provenance, difference, and trust once the field is reached**

So the lattice should be hardened primarily through:

- better provenance
- better visibility of what is present, absent, hidden, and unresolved
- stable classifications and repair logic

It should not overidentify itself with any one temporary extraction method.

## Use

- Keep each lane distinct.
- Look for shared constraints, not shared phrasing.
- Treat recurrence as a clue to structure, not as automatic agreement.
- Preserve differences in tone, authority, and scope.
- Distinguish true cross-voice convergence from shared undercapture or access failure.

## What it is not

- Not a forced synthesis.
- Not a collapse of one voice into another.
- Not a claim that separate analysts are making the same argument.
- Not a claim that the current access layer has already shown the full field.

## Practical test

If Pape, Mercouris, and Alkorshid all point to the same material pressure, but each from a different lane of evidence, the lattice is active. The notebook should surface that overlap while still keeping the sources separate.
