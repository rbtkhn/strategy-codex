# Academy Open Tensions

WORK only; not Record.

This note preserves unresolved academy shelf distinctions. It is not a doctrine closure. Use it when a shelf starts borrowing another shelf's shape too quickly.

## Current Tension

The academy pattern now has mature shelves beside early shelves:

- **Mature:** theology, military, politics.
- **Early:** statecraft, singularity.

The risk is template reflex. A shelf should not automatically receive a 25-book path, workshop, or bridge just because another shelf did. Each shelf should mature from its own pressure.

## Shelf Warnings

**Theology must not become** pure abstraction that forgets institutions, coercion, bodies, and command responsibility.

**Military must not become** map-thinking that explains violence so well it forgets faces, conscience, and sacred legitimacy.

**Politics must not become** campaign or current-events miscellany. Its center is regime form, legitimacy, parties, persuasion, and mass power.

**Statecraft must not become** a duplicate of politics or military. Its distinct object is decision under external constraint: diplomacy, war powers, alliances, sanctions, intelligence, and grand strategy.

**Singularity must not become** generic AI news or model-release tracking. Its distinct object is agency under acceleration: alignment, recursive improvement, automation, cognitive infrastructure, and human meaning.

## Politics And Statecraft

Politics asks: how do regimes, parties, publics, legitimacy, and institutions form power?

Statecraft asks: how do decision-makers use power under constraint among other powers?

They overlap at sovereignty, war powers, sanctions, intelligence, and executive authority. They should not merge. Politics is the internal grammar of rule; statecraft is the external and strategic conduct of rule.

## Singularity First Move

Singularity should not start with a 25-book Bookshelf path. The catalog has adjacent anchors, but not enough direct singularity density for a clean canon.

Best first maturity move:

1. **Workshop first:** agency, acceleration, alignment, automation, substrate, meaning.
2. **Bridge second:** connect to theology, politics, statecraft, and military.
3. **Canon later:** build only when the source shelf is dense enough or new rows are deliberately added.

## Furtwangler Rule

When the academy wants to mature a shelf, hold the tension open long enough to ask:

- What is this shelf's real object?
- What must this shelf not become?
- Which adjacent shelf is most likely to distort it?
- Does it need canon, workshop, or bridge first?
- What unresolved distinction should be preserved for the next pass?
