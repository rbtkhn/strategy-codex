# Academy

WORK only; not Record.

Academy is the study layer for strategy-codex: topical shelves that turn reading, concepts, and writer lanes into reusable judgment.

Enter here when a question is bigger than one transcript, one book, or one expert thread. The academy should help an operator decide what to read first, what pressure to test, what adjacent shelf can correct the blind spot, and what reusable note should survive the session.

## Start Here

Read [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) first. It defines the shared academy form:

1. **Canon** - a reading path or curated source shelf.
2. **Workshop** - active-use methods, instruments, sheets, and prompts.
3. **Bridge** - connections to adjacent shelves, strategy work, Bookshelf rows, Predictive History, or writer lanes.

Then read [cross-shelf-bridge.md](cross-shelf-bridge.md) for the first mature bridge: theology and military learning from each other through pressure, agency, responsibility, force, conscience, and the face inside the map.

Use [open-tensions.md](open-tensions.md) when a shelf starts maturing by template reflex rather than from its own pressure.

Use [next-questions.md](next-questions.md) when the shelf is organized but the next live question is not yet obvious.

## Operating Rule

Academy shelves should produce instruments, not just inventories. A good session leaves behind a sharper question, source sheet, pressure test, cross-shelf note, or warning that can be reused.

Pressure may explain action, but it must not excuse it. Preserve both structure and responsibility.

## Boundary

Academy shelves organize study objects and conceptual routes. They do not replace speaker folders, raw-input provenance, Bookshelf catalog rows, host-local arcs, Predictive History source boundaries, or Record surfaces. `ph-civ/` is the exception: it is a mirror checkout of the public ph-civ repo, not an ordinary academy shelf.
