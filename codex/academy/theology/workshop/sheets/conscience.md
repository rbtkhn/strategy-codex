## Purpose

Study conscience as inner pressure: not merely preference, not merely social conditioning, and not automatically divine command.

## Working Questions

- What does conscience know that public justification hides?
- When is conscience sacred pressure, social pressure, or self-deception?
- What does guilt reveal, and what can it distort?
- How does conscience differ from obedience?
- What remains accountable when pressure is real?

## Counter-Reading

Conscience can become narcissistic if it treats inner intensity as proof. It can also become cowardly if it dissolves into conformity. The workshop should test conscience against face, consequence, tradition, and action.

## Output Prompts

- Write one case where conscience resists public order and one where it needs public correction.
- State the difference between sacred conscience and providential certainty.
- Draft a Tolstoy Lens note that preserves both pressure and responsibility.
