# Sacrifice And Violence


## Purpose

Study the line between sacrifice that binds, sacrifice that purifies, and sacrifice that becomes permission for violence.

## Working Questions

- Who or what is being sacrificed?
- Does sacrifice restore the face or erase it?
- What community is coordinated by the sacrifice?
- When does sacrifice become a blade against an outgroup?
- What refusal does the condition require?

## Counter-Reading

Not every sacrifice is violence in disguise. Some sacrifices are restraint, fidelity, grief, or costly care. The workshop should distinguish gift, discipline, atonement, scapegoating, and killing.
