# Love


## Purpose

Study love as recognition, coordination, and non-domination. The point is to distinguish universal love from sentimental cover for mastery.

## Working Questions

- When does love preserve the face of the other?
- When does universal love become a category that erases concrete people?
- What does love coordinate that fear cannot?
- What does love refuse to do even when it could dominate?
- How does love behave under disagreement, betrayal, or danger?

## Counter-Reading

Universal love can become imperial if it claims to know the other's good so completely that consent, grief, and difference disappear. Test every love claim against the concrete face.
