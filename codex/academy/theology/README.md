# Theology

WORK only; not Record.

This shelf is an operator-first study canon for theology-shaped civ-mem satellite material. It is meant to answer one question quickly: **where do I begin?**

The tone should stay serious but alive: clear enough to study from, warm enough to return to, and light enough that the shelf does not become a second bureaucracy.

## Begin Here

Read the essays in this order:

1. [The Simple Condition](essays/THE-SIMPLE-CONDITION.md)
2. [One Subject, Many Tongues](essays/ONE-SUBJECT-MANY-TONGUES.md)
3. [Universal Love and Mastery](essays/UNIVERSAL-LOVE-AND-MASTERY.md)
4. [The Coordination Hypothesis](essays/THE-COORDINATION-HYPOTHESIS.md)
5. [The Delusion of Separation](essays/THE-DELUSION-OF-SEPARATION.md)
6. [AI Ethics from the Condition](essays/AI-ETHICS-FROM-THE-CONDITION.md)
7. [Writing the Book and Death](essays/WRITING-THE-BOOK-AND-DEATH.md)
8. [Preserving Consciousness](essays/PRESERVING-CONSCIOUSNESS-BIOLOGICAL-SYMBOLS.md)

That path moves from the core condition, to shared reference across traditions, to love, coordination, recognition, AI ethics, mortality, and the preservation of consciousness through symbolic work.

## Bookshelf Bridge

The first Bookshelf bridge is [bookshelf-crosswalk.md](bookshelf-crosswalk.md). It uses `Shelf-*` ids from the History Notebook Bookshelf catalog and keeps the order pedagogical rather than bibliographic: sacred traditions, Christian imperial forms, soul-city philosophy, tragedy and epic, Christian imagination, Enlightenment critique, Russian moral psychology, faith crisis, and modern rupture.

The Kaldellis/Wilson pair is the hinge: New Rome and Holy Roman Europe as two Christian-imperial forms that make theology visible as civilizational pressure, not merely private doctrine.
