**Purpose:** Name the theological dimension already latent in the Predictive History Tolstoy Lens without converting the lens into providential history.

## Working Claim

The Tolstoy Lens is primarily a causation lens, but it becomes theological whenever the pressure field includes sacred story, conscience, messianic expectation, eschatology, ritual memory, providence-like pressure, or the moral imagination of a civilization.

The lens asks:

> When a visible actor appears to make history, what invisible field made that actor historically possible?

The theology bridge asks:

> When that invisible field is sacred, moral, or eschatological, how can we describe its historical force without turning it into fate, divine endorsement, or moral excuse?

## Why This Matters For Predictive History

Predictive History already routes theological pressure through several Tolstoy nodes:

- `civ-25` Paul: messianic expectation, diaspora networks, Roman mobility, and doctrinal portability.
- `civ-53` Dostoevsky: Orthodox memory, conscience, guilt, suffering, and inner necessity.
- `gt-22` nation-state: sacred history, eschatological mobilization, legitimacy strain, and institutional trust decay.

The academy-theology contribution is to help the lens distinguish three things that are easy to collapse:

1. **Theological content:** doctrines, sacred stories, ritual forms, eschatological expectations.
2. **Historical pressure:** the way those contents organize action, identity, obedience, sacrifice, legitimacy, and fear.
3. **Moral responsibility:** the remaining accountability of visible actors even when sacred pressure is real.

## Theological Deepening Move

The lens should not ask only, "What pressure made this actor possible?"

In theological terrain, it should also ask:

- What sacred story made obedience imaginable?
- What moral world made action feel necessary?
- What form of conscience did the actor claim, ignore, or deform?
- What eschatological horizon compressed ordinary prudence?
- What ritual, text, or memory carried the pressure across generations?
- What responsibility remains once the pressure field is named?
