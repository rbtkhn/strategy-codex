# Military Workshop Sheets

This surface is non-authoritative and subject to revision.

These sheets are starting benches for the academy-military workshop. They are not summaries of the books; they are reusable prompts for reading campaigns, command decisions, logistics, escalation, and responsibility.

## Order

1. [Sunzi and Clausewitz](sunzi-clausewitz.md) - theory pair for deception, friction, and political purpose.
2. [Napoleon and overextension](napoleon-overextension.md) - decisive maneuver, logistics, tempo, and culmination.
3. [Churchill and self-narrated victory](churchill-self-narrated-victory.md) - grand strategy and actor memoir as source problem.
4. [Byzantium and frontier endurance](byzantium-frontier-endurance.md) - long-war equilibrium, frontier depth, and sacred legitimacy.
5. [Eastern Front industrial war](eastern-front-industrial-war.md) - attrition, operational depth, production, and narrative discipline.

## Expected Output

Each sheet should produce one compact WORK artifact: a campaign note, an instrument amendment, a source-boundary warning, a cross-shelf bridge note, or a Bookshelf pairing.
