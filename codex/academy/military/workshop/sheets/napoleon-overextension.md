# Napoleon And Overextension


## Purpose

Study operational brilliance and its failure modes: tempo, decisive maneuver, corps organization, logistics, empire, and the point where success outruns sustainment.

## Bookshelf Anchors

- **The Campaigns of Napoleon (Shelf-0303)** - operational art, decisive maneuver, corps system, logistics, and overextension.
- **On War (Shelf-0146)** - friction, culmination, political purpose, and the danger of mistaking battle success for strategic success.

## Writer-Lane Companions

- [Big Serge](../../bigserge/) for campaign narrative, tempo, and theater-level synthesis.

## Working Questions

- What made the maneuver possible?
- What logistical assumptions did it require?
- Where did operational tempo become strategic overreach?
- What coalition or political pressures changed the military problem?
- What did the commander still choose?

## Counter-Reading

Do not reduce Napoleon to genius or hubris. The workshop should hold organization, state capacity, logistics, enemy adaptation, political ambition, and personal command style together.

## Output Prompts

- Identify the campaign's culmination point.
- Write a logistics vs narrative test for one decisive-maneuver claim.
- Name the command responsibility that remains after structural pressure is described.
