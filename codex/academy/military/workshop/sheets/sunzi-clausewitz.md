# Sunzi And Clausewitz

WORK only; not Record.

## Purpose

Use Sunzi and Clausewitz as a paired theory sheet: victory before battle, deception, indirectness, friction, moral forces, and war as a political instrument.

## Bookshelf Anchors

- **The Art of War (Shelf-0302)** - deception, museum identity knowledge (archive), enemy knowledge, economy of force, and winning before battle.
- **On War (Shelf-0146)** - friction, fog, moral forces, escalation, and political purpose.

## Writer-Lane Companions

- [Big Serge](../../bigserge/) when campaign narrative needs theory checks.
- [Simplicius](../../simplicius/) when escalation narratives need source-critical stress tests.

## Working Questions

- What would victory before battle mean in this case?
- Where is friction hiding inside the clean plan?
- What is the political purpose of the violence?
- What does deception accomplish, and what does it risk?
- Does the theory clarify the case or become quotation theater?

## Counter-Reading

Sunzi can become cleverness without force. Clausewitz can become force without restraint. Use them together so indirect strategy stays material and force stays political.

## Output Prompts

- Write a two-column Sunzi/Clausewitz check for one current campaign claim.
- Identify one place where political purpose is missing.
- Name one friction point the narrative is underplaying.
