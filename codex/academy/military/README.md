# Military

This surface is non-authoritative and subject to revision.

This academy shelf holds military and war-studies reading lanes that are text-first rather than speaker-object folders. Use it for campaign analysis, operational narratives, escalation reading, and source-critical military synthesis.

## Writer Lanes

- [Big Serge](bigserge/) - campaign and theater-level war studies commentary.
- [Simplicius](simplicius/) - conflict, geopolitical, and military-escalation synthesis.

## Bookshelf Bridge

The first Bookshelf bridge is [bookshelf-crosswalk.md](bookshelf-crosswalk.md). It uses `Shelf-*` ids from the History Notebook Bookshelf catalog and keeps the order pedagogical rather than bibliographic: ancient war grammar, Roman campaign practice, medieval and early modern pressure, Clausewitzian theory, naval and siege contests, modern command, industrial war, and civil-military control.

Use the crosswalk as a study path beside the writer lanes. Big Serge and Simplicius are interpretive companions here, not primary authorities.

## Workshop

The active-use layer is [workshop/](workshop/). It turns the military shelf into a bench: read campaign, name force, test logistics, test command responsibility, separate primary from interpretive lanes, and produce reusable notes.

## Boundary

This is an academy shelf, not a raw-input source of truth and not a speaker-object lane. Keep authored commentary distinct from primary wires, official claims, imagery, ORBAT work, and Record surfaces.
