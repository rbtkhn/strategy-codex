WORK only; not Record.

Use this template when a leader, supreme authority, executive office, security council, party center, or successor authority affects settlement credibility. This is not a biography template. It asks whether a current authority can carry durable state interest into a binding instrument.

## Settlement Credibility

- What can this profile sign credibly?
- What can this profile implement credibly?

## Succession / Continuity Risk

## Binding Instrument Test

- What can this profile authorize that survives them?
- What can this profile not concede without breaking their regime or coalition?
- Is this profile expressing the state, capturing the state, or failing to carry the state?
