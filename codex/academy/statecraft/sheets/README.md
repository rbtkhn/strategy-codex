# Statecraft Sheets

WORK only; not Record.

These sheets are reusable prompt benches for academy-statecraft. They are not summaries of Richelieu, Bismarck, or any historical case. They are fast checks for making treaty drafts, policy papers, negotiation briefs, and statecraft memos more durable.

## Order

1. **Richelieu test** - What durable state interest and institution carry the draft?
2. **Bismarck test** - What limited aim, equilibrium, and exit discipline keep the draft from overreaching?
3. **Combined equilibrium test** - Where do institutional capacity and balance-of-power restraint reinforce or contradict each other?
4. **Failure-mode review** - What breaks first: incentives, verification, domestic legitimacy, alliance geometry, administrative capacity, or time?

## Prompt Forms

### Richelieu Test

- State the durable interest in one sentence.
- Name the institution that carries the policy.
- Name the faction, ideology, or patron pressure that can distort it.
- Name the administrative or fiscal burden.
- Name the legitimacy risk.

### Bismarck Test

- State what is enough.
- State what must not be demanded.
- Name the actor to isolate.
- Name the actor to reassure or keep neutral.
- Name the exit, review, or restraint clause.

### Combined Equilibrium Test

- If the draft succeeds, what balance exists six months later?
- Which institution keeps that balance from becoming mere rhetoric?
- Which party can comply without humiliation?
- Which party can defect, and how would that be seen?
- What clause prevents advantage from becoming overreach?

### Failure-Mode Review

- What cannot be verified?
- What cannot be financed?
- What cannot be enforced?
- What cannot be sold domestically?
- What alliance consequence has been underpriced?
- What happens if the strongest party wins too much?

### Folder Choreography

Open [Folder choreography](folder-choreography.md) when a statecraft artifact needs the full lane movement: `civilization/` remembers, `empire/` reaches, `state/` decides, `helix.md` regulates, and `transactions/` bind.

### State Braid Model

Open [state braid model](state-braid-model.md) when several arcs must be kept together as one draftable crisis object rather than split into separate topic notes or clauses.

### Abraham Helix

Open [Abraham helix](abraham-helix.md) when a present crisis is being shaped by the long-run recurrent field created by Judaism, Christianity, and Islam rather than by force, territory, and bargaining alone.

### Speaker Insight To Policy Memo

Open [Speaker insight to policy memo](speaker-insight-to-policy-memo.md) when a raw-input capture or speaker arc contains one useful statecraft insight, but the output needs to remain a policy artifact rather than a speaker summary.

### Comprehensive Power Vs Coercive Center

Open [Comprehensive power vs coercive center](comprehensive-power-vs-coercive-center.md) when a claim about "world power" needs a sharper metric before it becomes a policy memo, treaty frame, or negotiation assumption.

### Pape Coercive-Center Index

Open [Pape coercive-center index](pape-coercive-center-index.md) when a draft needs to track whether crisis leverage is changing the great-power ranking over time.

### Mearsheimer Structural-Realist Scoreboard

Open [Mearsheimer structural-realist scoreboard](mearsheimer-structural-realist-scoreboard.md) when a draft needs balance-of-power logic, overextension checks, and Richelieu / Bismarck overlays.

### Hexagon Arc-Lens Stress Test

Open [hexagon arc-lens stress test](hexagon-arc-lens-stress-test.md) when a crisis, transaction, clause, or current-event read needs the six civilization arc-lenses to vote separately before the draft is trusted.

### Present-Day Arc Extension Matrix

Open [present-day arc extension matrix](present-day-arc-extension-matrix.md) when a live read needs to name the modern carrier of an older civilization arc before routing to `hex`, a transaction object, or a recursive candidate.

### Mutation Statecraft

Open [mutation statecraft](mutation-statecraft.md) when a familiar object keeps its name but changes function under pressure: alliance becomes containment lattice, trade becomes dependency, deterrence becomes escalation platform, infrastructure becomes sovereignty claim, recognition becomes humiliation trap, or republic becomes emergency empire.

### Russia Depth Arc

Open [Russia depth arc](russia-depth-arc.md) when a Russia-lane read invokes exposure, encirclement, buffers, access, system-depth, dependency-depth, or the rule: recognize exposure, not empire; recognize optionality, not dependency; convert power into equilibrium before power becomes encirclement.

### China Order Arc

Open [China order arc](china-order-arc.md) when a China-lane read invokes mandate, order, unity, development continuity, maritime ambiguity, party-state implementation, compute sovereignty, or the rule: preserve order without visible tutelage; preserve face without hiding material constraint; convert capacity into quiet implementation before capacity becomes coercive spectacle.

### Iran Dignity-Sovereignty Arc

Open [Iran dignity-sovereignty arc](iran-dignity-sovereignty-arc.md) when an Iran-lane read invokes dignity, sovereignty, deterrence, sanctions relief, nuclear latency, Hormuz, non-regime-change, verification, martyrdom, or the rule: preserve dignity without trapping sovereignty in permanent resistance; convert endurance into bargaining power before endurance becomes isolation.

### America Empire-Republic Arc

Open [America empire-republic arc](america-empire-republic-arc.md) when an America-lane read invokes empire / republic tension, constitutional authority, executive emergency power, sanctions-finance reach, resource extraction, ideological suppression, alliance burden, maritime order, platform power, technology-industrial capacity, successor legitimacy, or the rule: make empire answer to republic, or republic becomes empire's mask; convert power into consent before power becomes control; keep technology as carrier, not sovereign.

### National Perspective Orthogonality

Open [National perspective orthogonality](national-perspective-orthogonality.md) when the same crisis needs separate statecraft logics. The default Iran / India / fourth-center matrix separates U.S. containment, Russian exploitation-without-entrapment, Chinese leverage-under-energy-constraint, and Iranian legitimation-without-loss-of-control.

When one perspective needs to become a draft, use the matching national lane rather than expanding the comparison sheet: [America](../america/), [Russia](../russia/), [China](../china/), or [Iran](../iran/).

### Authority Carrier Orthogonality

Open [authority carrier orthogonality](authority-carrier-orthogonality.md) when a treaty, compact, or crisis transaction depends on whether leaders, executives, security councils, party-state organs, or split authority structures can actually carry a settlement.

### Crisis Test Casebook

Open [Crisis test casebook](crisis-test-casebook.md) when a new crisis test should be compared against the existing suite, or when the lesson from a test should be routed into the four national lane casebooks.

### Transaction Router

Open [transaction router](transaction-router.md) after a current-event read has classified the crisis object. It decides whether the event is an exact fit, near fit, or no fit for the 12 transaction objects, then points to the relevant bundle, first lane perspective, or candidate shape.

### Great-Power Energy Absorber Threshold

Open [great-power energy absorber threshold](great-power-energy-absorber-threshold.md) when a summit, purchase signal, or sanctions dispute suggests that a great power is absorbing another state's energy flows, but the event has not yet produced a distinct payments, shipping, insurance, sanctions, pipeline, or transit mechanism.

## Expected Output

Each sheet should produce one compact WORK artifact: a treaty clause, memo paragraph, negotiation instruction, sanctions mechanism, review clause, off-ramp, or warning against overreach.
