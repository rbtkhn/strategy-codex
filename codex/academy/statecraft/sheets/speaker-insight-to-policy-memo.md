# Speaker Insight To Policy Memo


Use this sheet when a speaker appearance contains one policy-relevant insight, but the statecraft output must remain a memo, not a speaker summary or worldview import.

## Purpose

Convert a single sourced insight into a usable policy memo while preserving source boundaries:

- raw-input remains provenance;
- speaker folders remain compact speaker state;
- academy-statecraft receives only the statecraft implication, instrument, or memo draft.

## Inputs

- Speaker:
- Host or source:
- Raw-input path:
- Speaker arc or ledger path, if any:
- Policy problem:

## Extraction Rule

Take one insight, not the whole worldview.

Good extraction:

- "A reciprocal enforcement pause needs observable maritime hooks."

Bad extraction:

- "Adopt the speaker's whole theory of empire, decline, motive, and agency."

## Memo Scaffold

### 1. Source-Bound Insight

State the insight in one sentence and link the source.

### 2. Policy Translation

Translate the insight into a statecraft problem:

- What decision does it affect?
- Which institution or negotiator can use it?
- What instrument might express it?

### 3. Richelieu Check

- Durable state interest:
- Institutional carrier:
- Administrative or intelligence requirement:
- Legitimacy risk:
- What must remain outside the memo because it belongs to the speaker's wider worldview?

### 4. Bismarck Check

- Limited objective:
- Actor to isolate:
- Actor to reassure or keep neutral:
- Equilibrium after implementation:
- Off-ramp or restraint clause:

### 5. Memo Paragraph

Draft one policy paragraph that can stand without the speaker's name.

### 6. Boundary Note

Name what was deliberately not imported:

- biography;
- ideology;
- total worldview;
- unsourced claims;
- interview heat;
- host framing;
- unresolved factual claims.

## Output

Produce one compact WORK artifact:

- policy memo paragraph;
- negotiation instruction;
- treaty clause amendment;
- sanctions or relief mechanism;
- institutional warning;
- or source-bound statecraft note.

## Failure Signs

- The memo becomes a speaker profile.
- The speaker's emotional register becomes the policy register.
- The source is used as authority for claims it did not establish.
- The policy conclusion requires the whole worldview to be true.
- The instrument cannot be explained without naming the speaker.
