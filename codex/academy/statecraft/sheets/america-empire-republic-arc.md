# America Empire-Republic Arc

WORK only; not Record.

## Purpose

Use this sheet when an America-lane read, hexagon stress test, transaction, clause, or current event invokes empire / republic tension, constitutional authority, executive emergency power, sanctions-finance reach, resource extraction, ideological suppression, alliance burden, maritime order, platform power, technology-industrial capacity, or successor legitimacy.

The governing test:

**Which empire-republic form is America invoking, and has it crossed into emergency empire, platform rule, ideological suppression, resource extraction, or successor-legitimacy rupture?**

## Core Rule

Make empire answer to republic, or republic becomes empire's mask.

Convert power into consent before power becomes control.

Keep technology as carrier, not sovereign.

## Empire-Republic Forms

| Empire-republic form | Historical spine | Statecraft meaning | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imperial-provincial inheritance | British Empire before 1775, common law, maritime commerce, colonial assemblies, Crown / Parliament | America inherits legal, maritime, commercial, Protestant, parliamentary, and imperial habits before becoming sovereign. | Anti-imperial language forgets inherited imperial technique and repeats it unconsciously. |
| Revolutionary-republic | 1775-1783, Continental Congress, independence, rights, consent, Treaty of Paris | Legitimate power must convert rebellion, force, and alliance into recognized lawful authority. | Liberty language becomes exclusion, conquest, ideological suppression, or moral permission for control. |
| Constitutional-union | Constitution, federalism, Congress, courts, war powers, fiscal authority | Durable instruments need authorization, funding, review, public purpose, and federal legitimacy. | Procedure becomes theater; executive will substitutes for law; faction turns policy into civil-war proxy. |
| Continental-expansion | frontier, Manifest Destiny, Civil War, infrastructure, ports, rivers, resources, dispossession | Scale, land, internal markets, food, energy, and union give America strategic mass. | Continental confidence masks dispossession, ecological exhaustion, resource extraction, sectional rupture, or internal empire. |
| Maritime-commercial order | Navy, Atlantic / Pacific buffers, canals, trade, insurance, dollar reach, open navigation | America projects order through sea lanes, finance, commerce, guarantees, and protected public goods. | Open order becomes control, creditor leverage, sanctions overreach, extraction disguised as development, or free-riding alliance burden. |
| Emergency-empire | executive war, sanctions, intelligence, bases, alliances, covert action, permanent crisis | Speed and reach can stabilize crises when ordinary authority is too slow. | Emergency action becomes standing empire without review, sunset, consent, or lawful exit. |
| Platform-sovereignty | cloud, AI, payments, identity, data, satellites, launch, private contracting, automated permissions | Private technology can carry state capacity, communication, finance, targeting, identity, and infrastructure resilience. | Platforms become quasi-sovereign; private carriers define public policy; surveillance / identity control is laundered as safety or innovation; technology outruns constitutional restraint. |

## Diagnostic Questions

1. What authority object is named: Congress, executive, courts, military command, dollar system, platform, alliance, port, canal, identity system, or successor authority?
2. Which empire-republic form is being invoked?
3. What law, appropriation, review, treaty, alliance consent, or public-purpose claim carries the instrument?
4. Does the instrument create lawful reach, or does it bypass consent through emergency, sanctions, platforms, or private infrastructure?
5. Can a successor administration, court, Congress, military implementer, ally, and public narrative inherit it without repudiation?
6. Does technology carry the state, or does it become sovereign over the state?
7. Does republican language restrain empire, or launder it?
8. What would prove the policy has become resource extraction, ideological suppression, emergency empire, platform rule, allied abandonment, or civil-war proxy?

## Hexagon Use

When running `america hex <topic>`, use the empire-republic arc as an America-specific substrate after the six lenses vote:

- `peace` tests lawful exit, treaty legitimacy, allied burden-sharing, reconstruction limits, and successor-stable restraint.
- `war` prices authorization, civilian command, escalation, war fatigue, and exit design.
- `god` tests rights, oath, covenant, conscience, and forbidden domination.
- `lit` tests republic conscience, dissent, anti-imperial critique, frontier self-making, and liberty language.
- `art` tests civic form, monument, spectacle, mass culture, technology confidence, and public legitimacy.
- `geo` names continental scale, buffers, ports, canals, rivers, water, food, energy, and infrastructure constraints.

Do not let the empire-republic arc become the central pillar of the hexagon. It is an America-lane diagnostic overlay.
