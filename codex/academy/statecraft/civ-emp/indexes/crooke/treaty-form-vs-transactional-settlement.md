# Treaty Form Vs Transactional Settlement

## Purpose

Provide a `civ-emp` source scaffold for the Crooke theme in which serious settlements require lawful state form, treaty credibility, and sovereignty terms rather than investor brokerage or personality-deal optics.

## Cybernetic Activation

Crooke-side activation surface: [Treaty form vs transactional settlement](../../../../../../statecraft/voices/crooke/treaty-form-vs-transactional-settlement.md)

## Backward Historical Question

When do settlements hold because they are carried by real state form, and when do they fail because they were never more than elite brokerage or verbal assurance?

## Source Basis

- Crooke modern examples: end-of-Cold-War verbal assurance, treaty-form insistence, sovereign-settlement blockage
- `civ-emp` retrieval contract for `peace`
- lane-local treaty or settlement objects where they exist
- legacy `civ-mem` only if a specific settlement lineage later needs deeper provenance

## Arc Pattern

`verbal or brokered assurance -> no durable legal carrier -> successor distrust -> strategic grievance or settlement failure`

Counter-pattern:

`state-to-state form -> recognizable sovereignty terms -> inherited obligation -> slower but more durable settlement`

## Continuity / Transformation / Current Carrier / Failure Mode

- Continuity: durable settlements require form, not just process
- Transformation: stakeholder theater often masquerades as diplomacy under modern media conditions
- Current carrier: states, treaties, recognized guarantors, formal legal texts
- Failure mode: leader-broker politics substitutes for state form and collapses under the next power transition

## Statecraft Use

Use this scaffold when drafting or testing:

- treaty form
- guarantees and verification
- whether sovereignty demands are explicit or evaded
- whether a peace process can survive the exit of the current broker

## Counterweight

Formal treaty language alone does not save a settlement if the parties never intended to honor the strategic substance behind it.

## Transaction Hooks

- ask whether the proposed settlement is state-carried or personality-carried
- ask whether unwritten understandings are doing too much work
- ask whether the agreement survives once today's broker leaves office
