## Verification Rule

A first-wave slice is only `verified` when:

1. both volume sides needed for that slice exist
2. lane-local files prefer `civ-emp`
3. at least one canonical family is served on both sides
4. counterweight exists
5. transaction hook exists
6. provenance to upstream `civ-mem` is preserved
7. the slice's symmetry partner is at least `cut_over`
