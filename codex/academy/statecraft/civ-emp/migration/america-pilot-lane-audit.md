## Long-Arc Line Check

The reread of the whole America civ/emp line surfaces three shape issues that still matter even after the latest improvement:

- **Authority ladder is richer than instrument ladder.**
  [State memory](../../america/civilization/objects/state-memory.md) still carries the fuller causal stack: inheritance, rupture, constitutional carry, successor stress, and internal fracture. [Empire instrument](../../america/empire/seed-instruments.md) is much stronger now, but it still lands more as a regulator shelf than as an equally thick historical arc.

- **Empire has stronger sections than opening synthesis.**
  The three instrument families now do real work, but the opening arc and statecraft-use sections are still more compressed than the middle of the file. The next expansion should improve the top-level empire synthesis, not only add depth inside subsections.

- **Civilization still owns more of the restoration language.**
  The pair shares the same hinge logic, but the civilization side still explains the restoration path more fully than the empire side explains how instruments are actually brought back under political control. That is the main qualitative symmetry gap left after the ratio improvement.
