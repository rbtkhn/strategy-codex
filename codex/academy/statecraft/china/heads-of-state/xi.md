## Role In Current State Form

Xi functions in the China lane as the visible carrier of party-state authority, unity, rejuvenation, and anti-disorder discipline. Treat this as a settlement-credibility profile, not biography.

## Authority Base

- Formal authority: top party and state leadership roles, military command through party-state channels.
- Informal authority: ideological line, elite discipline, continuity narrative, anti-corruption / control legacy.
- Security / military authority: central military and security apparatus.
- Coalition authority: party center, state bureaucracy, industrial planners, provincial implementation, major firms.
- Symbolic authority: unity, national rejuvenation, anti-humiliation memory, order.

## Institutional Carrier

Durable instruments need party center, State Council organs, military command, finance / commerce ministries, provincial implementers, firms, banks, ports, and insurers.

## Domestic Coalition

The leader must manage nationalist publics, PLA prestige, party discipline, industrial continuity, provincial compliance, and the risk that compromise looks like weakness.

## Strategic Temperament

China-facing instruments should expect preference for quiet leverage, controlled ambiguity, stability language, and avoiding public ownership of disorder.

## Red Lines

Terms that internationalize internal sovereignty claims, humiliate the party-state, threaten unity, or force premature visible alignment can break carrying capacity.

## Settlement Credibility

- Can sign credibly: stabilization frameworks, infrastructure compacts, energy arrangements, quiet pressure channels.
- Needs institutional carrier: banks, ministries, insurers, ports, firms, party discipline, military restraint.

## Succession / Continuity Risk

Medium. A durable instrument should be framed as party-state continuity and anti-disorder policy, not personal preference.

## Binding Instrument Test

Is the leader expressing the party-state, overcentralizing the state, or failing to let institutions carry the settlement?
