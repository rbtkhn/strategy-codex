# China Chapter Seeds

## Purpose

Accumulate and organize upstream chapter ideas for the China volume build from across `strategy-codex` arcs, speaker shelves, and bridge notes.

This file is now a **pre-drafting organizer**, not just a gathering list. Its job is to reduce seed sprawl into real chapter families while preserving the quote-backed arc pressure that generated them.

## Rule

Use external arcs as **chapter-generators**, not **chapter-governors**.

That means:

- keep the originating arc visible
- preserve the quote cluster that generated the chapter pressure
- let the China helix and strand surfaces decide whether the family survives into a real chapter
- do not let any one speaker or arc silently become the architecture of the volume
