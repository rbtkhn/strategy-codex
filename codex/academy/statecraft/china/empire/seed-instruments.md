# China Empire Seed Instruments

WORK only; not Record.

These seeds translate CIV-MEM and China civilization patterns into empire instruments. State rules should regulate these instruments through lane-local civilization and helix files.

## Infrastructure And Finance Reach

- **Civilization authorization:** Development legitimacy and anti-chaos order.
- **Desired control:** Ports, rail, credit, settlement rails, insurance alternatives, and supply-chain corridors.
- **Adaptive use:** Partners gain alternatives while China reduces dependence on hostile systems.
- **Pathological overreach:** Infrastructure influence becomes visible ownership or policing burden.
- **Statecraft regulator:** Quiet financing, local ownership language, limited guarantees, and nonexclusive access.

## Energy Route Security

- **Civilization authorization:** Order against chaos.
- **Desired control:** Oil flows, shipping insurance, port access, pipelines, and crisis deconfliction.
- **Adaptive use:** China stabilizes flows without appearing to command every regional actor.
- **Pathological overreach:** Energy security turns into premature military exposure or coalition containment.
- **Statecraft regulator:** Commercial carriers, incident channels, shared insurance pools, and public restraint.

## Quiet Partner Leverage

- **Civilization authorization:** Humiliation and containment memory.
- **Desired control:** Partner escalation timing, diplomatic sequencing, and regional forums.
- **Adaptive use:** China restrains partners enough to preserve its long game while avoiding public ownership of their choices.
- **Pathological overreach:** A partner crisis forces China to choose between humiliation and escalation.
- **Statecraft regulator:** Private demarches, face-saving offramps, conditional support, and silence where publicity would harden positions.
