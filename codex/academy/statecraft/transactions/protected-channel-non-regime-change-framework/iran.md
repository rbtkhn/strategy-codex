## State Interest

Iran's durable interest is to preserve sovereign authority after leadership attack while converting resilience into sanctions relief, protected channels, and recognition rather than endless martyrdom escalation.

## Leverage / Constraint

- Leverage being spent: Institutional survival, retaliation capacity, Hormuz leverage, nuclear latency, partner channels, and the legitimacy generated by resisting decapitation.
- Constraint that limits the leverage: If Iran refuses every protected-channel sequence, martyrdom energy can become permanent war logic and alienate potential guarantors.
- Volatility note: Pape may score Iran high as an active coercive center, but durable statecraft requires monetizing leverage before balancing pressure and war exhaustion erode it.

## Richelieu Validation

- Institutional carrier: Supreme National Security Council, Supreme Leader succession structure, foreign ministry, IRGC command discipline, and mediator channel.
- Capacity: Named negotiators, protected communications, calibrated retaliation, sanctions-relief verification, and public legitimacy language.
- Continuity risk: Internal factions may treat talks after martyrdom as dishonor unless the framework explicitly rejects regime-change and partition.
- Long-pressure burden: Iran must maintain deterrence while proving it can regulate retaliation through recognized authority.

## Bismarck Validation

- Limited aim: Secure protected channels, no-regime-change / no-partition assurances, and first-step sanctions relief; do not demand total regional settlement in one move.
- Actor to isolate: Spoilers seeking endless war, partition, or further decapitation.
- Actor to reassure, restrain, or keep neutral: China, Russia, Oman, Gulf states, non-aligned states, and domestic constituencies worried about humiliation.
- Alliance geometry: Use Russia / China / Oman depth to guarantee channel protection without surrendering Iranian agency.
- Restraint after advantage: If channel protection is recognized, calibrate retaliation and keep the mediator path alive.

## Instrument Text

> Iran shall accept mediator-protected talks once the opposing parties publicly affirm that the framework is not a regime-change or partition instrument and identify protected channel carriers. Iran shall treat attacks on named carriers or supreme-authority succession organs as material breach, while reserving calibrated defensive rights. Initial Iranian de-escalation shall be paired with visible sanctions-relief steps and a written assurance that no settlement term requires Iran to validate prior leadership attacks as lawful pressure.
