## State Interest

Iran's durable interest is to preserve deterrence and civilian nuclear dignity while converting latency into sanctions relief, non-regime-change assurances, and recognition as a necessary regional actor.

## Leverage / Constraint

- Leverage being spent: Nuclear latency, enrichment knowledge, hardened infrastructure, partner channels, and capacity to raise costs of attack.
- Constraint that limits the leverage: Open weaponization can trigger balancing, isolation, attack, or partner pressure.
- Volatility note: Latency is useful while it produces bargaining leverage; it becomes dangerous if it hardens adversary consensus around preventive war.

## Richelieu Validation

- Institutional carrier: Foreign ministry, nuclear agency, Supreme National Security Council, sanctions-relief channel, and mediator mechanism.
- Capacity: Inspection management, enrichment limits, relief verification, command discipline, and public legitimacy language.
- Continuity risk: Humiliating verification can be rejected even when materially beneficial.
- Long-pressure burden: Iran must preserve deterrence while proving restraint is sovereign, not submission.

## Bismarck Validation

- Limited aim: Recognition of civilian nuclear rights, relief, assurances, and non-weaponization restraint.
- Actor to isolate: Regime-change advocates and actors seeking preventive war.
- Actor to reassure, restrain, or keep neutral: China, Russia, Gulf states, India, Europe, and domestic factions.
- Alliance geometry: Use Russia and China support to secure terms, not to avoid all restraint.
- Restraint after advantage: Accept renewable limits once relief and assurances become credible.

## Instrument Text

Draft clause:

> Iran shall reaffirm its right to civilian nuclear development while accepting renewable non-weaponization commitments, specified enrichment ceilings, and non-humiliating inspection access in exchange for phased sanctions relief, non-regime-change assurances, and recognition that attacks on safeguarded nuclear facilities void the framework and reopen Iran's reserved rights.

## Mirror Test

- America: This leaves Iran too close to threshold status.
- Russia: This must not let the U.S. retain unilateral snapback control.
- China: This must reduce disorder, not merely preserve Iranian leverage.
- Iran: This must not turn inspection into humiliation.

## Falsifiers / Revisit Triggers

- Falsifier: Iran chooses open weaponization or refuses any inspection under any recognition / relief formula.
- Revisit trigger: Sanctions offer, inspection dispute, attack threat, Russia / China guarantee, or Gulf security proposal.
- Scoreboard movement: Review if structural balancing pressure against Iran rises faster than coercive leverage.
- Actor signal: Iranian proposal, inspection access shift, partner guarantee, or public non-regime-change language.
