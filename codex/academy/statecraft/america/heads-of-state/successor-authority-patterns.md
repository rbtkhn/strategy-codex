# Successor Authority Patterns

WORK only; not Record.

Use this note when the America lane needs to test whether an instrument survives a possible U.S. federal rupture, constitutional crisis, civil conflict, or reconstitution of continental authority.

## Carrier Candidates

- Federal executive with congressional backing.
- Congressional compact or statutory authority.
- Military command under lawful civilian command.
- State coalition or emergency federalism arrangement.
- Allied treaty institution carrying continuity externally.
- Successor continental authority after constitutional fracture.

## Core Test

What part of the instrument remains binding if Washington's current faction loses authority?

## Drafting Bias

Prefer successor-stable guarantees:

- review clauses;
- statutory notification;
- alliance co-carriers;
- escrowed relief sequences;
- legal definitions that do not depend on one leader's rhetoric;
- sunset and renewal procedures.

## Warning

Do not treat the United States as only the current administration when the `america` lane is tracking the possibility of a future America center of power.
