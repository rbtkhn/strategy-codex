# America Peace

WORK only; not Record.

## Purpose

Use American peace memory as a statecraft lens for constitutional settlement, treaty legitimacy, allied order, burden-sharing, procedural restraint, reconstruction, durable exit, and peace that still sounds like republican self-government.

## Civilizational Function

American peace is legitimate when restraint can be narrated as fidelity to the republic, not retreat from responsibility. Peace must pass through law, Congress, allies, courts, military implementation, and public purpose after the headline moment.

The arc is layered:

- **Origin:** Treaty of Paris, constitutional treaty power, congressional authority, and federal structure turned revolutionary victory into recognized order.
- **Continuity:** Monroe perimeter, naval order, alliances, Congress, courts, and reconstruction patterns kept peace tied to lawful authority and institutional design.
- **Transformation:** Marshall reconstruction, Cold War order, Kissingerian bargaining, and postwar alliances made peace a managed architecture rather than mere absence of war.
- **Current carrier:** Congress, executive, courts, military command, allies, NATO-like structures, sanctions authorities, and reconstruction institutions carry American peace.
- **Failure mode:** peace becomes executive theater, creditor leverage, endless enforcement, allied abandonment, or control disguised as order.
- **Transaction use:** test whether a settlement creates lawful exit, allied burden-sharing, review, relief, and successor-stable restraint.

## Statecraft Signals

- **Treaty legitimacy:** agreements need public authority and institutional form.
- **Congressional and legal carry:** peace must survive appropriations, hearings, court challenge, and election cycles.
- **Allied order:** restraint is stronger when burdens and guarantees are distributed.
- **Reconstruction:** victory or ceasefire is incomplete if successor institutions cannot stabilize.
- **Exit credibility:** restraint must be framed as durable order, not exhaustion hidden by rhetoric.

## Failure Mode

Peace memory deforms when settlement is treated as executive theater, allies read restraint as abandonment, domestic factions turn compromise into betrayal, reconstruction becomes endless occupation, or peace becomes creditor/institutional control over another state.
