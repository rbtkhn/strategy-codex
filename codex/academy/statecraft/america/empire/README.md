# America Empire

WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: name America's outward striving for security and control so statecraft can regulate it instead of pretending the drive does not exist.

## Desired Control

America seeks to control or shape maritime order, reserve-currency infrastructure, alliance command, sanctions access, technology platforms, intelligence flows, energy security, and escalation ladders.

## Instruments

- Naval and air power.
- Dollar clearing, sanctions, insurance, and finance.
- Bases, alliances, intelligence sharing, and interoperability.
- Congressional authority, executive orders, legal designations, and emergency powers.
- Technology platforms, cloud infrastructure, export controls, and standards.

## Adaptive Expression

American empire is adaptive when it protects navigation, deters predation, shares burdens, preserves domestic legitimacy, and uses law to limit rather than merely rationalize force.

## Pathological Overreach

American empire becomes pathological when it confuses military reach with political control, bypasses domestic authority, demands allied obedience without burden-sharing, or turns every coercive center into proof that only escalation can restore order.

## Limiting Principle

Control must be bounded by lawful authority, domestic legitimacy, allied burden-sharing, and a settlement path. If the instrument cannot name its exit condition, it is probably imperial drift.

## Drafting Prompts

- What is the smallest control claim America actually needs?
- Which actor must America reassure rather than command?
- What review, sunset, or authorization clause keeps force subordinate to legitimacy?
- Where does coercion strengthen the adversary's claim to system-shaping power?
