# Iran Empire

This surface is non-authoritative and subject to revision.

Purpose: name Iran's outward striving for security and control so statecraft can regulate leverage before it becomes uncontrolled disruption.

## Desired Control

Iran seeks control or bargaining influence over deterrence conditions, Hormuz transit, sanctions relief, regional recognition, partner networks, nuclear latency, and the cost adversaries pay for coercion.

## Instruments

- Hormuz geography, ports, transit conditions, and maritime denial.
- Missiles, drones, air defense, nuclear latency, and hardened infrastructure.
- Partner networks, backchannels, and regional political ties.
- Energy exports, sanctions adaptation, and resource self-sufficiency.
- Dignity-preserving recognition formulas and non-regime-change assurances.

## Adaptive Expression

Iranian empire is adaptive when it converts vulnerability into recognized leverage, protects sovereignty, reopens commerce under conditions, and lets Iran bargain from strength without becoming a permanent spoiler.

## Pathological Overreach

Iranian empire becomes pathological when disruption alienates China, India, Gulf states, or Russia; when deterrence becomes uncontrolled escalation; or when leverage is spent in ways that make sanctions relief impossible.

## Limiting Principle

Leverage must be monetized into recognition, relief, and restraint. If Iran cannot name what it will reopen, accept, or regulate, control becomes self-isolating.

## Drafting Prompts

- What leverage should Iran spend, and what must it keep?
- What transit guarantee preserves deterrence while reopening diplomacy?
- Which partner must Iran reassure before escalating?
- Where does dignity language need an institutional carrier?
