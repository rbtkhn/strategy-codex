# Russia Empire Seed Instruments

WORK only; not Record.

These seeds translate CIV-MEM and Russia civilization patterns into empire instruments. State rules should regulate these instruments through lane-local civilization and helix files.

## Borderland Buffer Control

- **Civilization authorization:** Encirclement and security depth.
- **Desired control:** Military infrastructure, neutrality terms, air defense proximity, and escalation geography.
- **Adaptive use:** Russia secures limited buffers through reciprocal constraints instead of open-ended domination.
- **Pathological overreach:** Buffer logic becomes occupation logic and unifies adversaries.
- **Statecraft regulator:** Reciprocal deployment limits, inspection zones, non-basing clauses, and review periods.

## Energy Leverage

- **Civilization authorization:** State continuity through collapse.
- **Desired control:** Supply routes, pricing, transit, infrastructure, and sanctions workarounds.
- **Adaptive use:** Energy leverage preserves autonomy and bargaining power without burning long-term customers.
- **Pathological overreach:** Reliability collapses and partners accelerate permanent substitution.
- **Statecraft regulator:** Contract floors, transit guarantees, phased relief, and commercial dispute channels.

## Mediator Disruption Optionality

- **Civilization authorization:** Great-power recognition.
- **Desired control:** Timing, diplomatic linkage, partner restraint, and multipolar forums.
- **Adaptive use:** Russia weakens hostile primacy while preserving the option to mediate or restrain escalation.
- **Pathological overreach:** Russia becomes hostage to a partner's crisis calendar.
- **Statecraft regulator:** Conditional support, mediator channels, ambiguity limits, and explicit nonownership of partner escalation.
