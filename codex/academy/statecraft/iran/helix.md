# Iran Civilization / Empire Helix

WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: hold together Iran's dignity-centered civilizational code and its coercive-center leverage so statecraft can convert vulnerability into durable security.

## Civilization Authorizes Empire

Iranian civilization authorizes empire when outward power is framed as defense of sovereignty, dignity, deterrence, and refusal to be managed as a disposable target.

## Empire Protects Civilization

Iranian imperial instruments protect civilization when they deter attack, force recognition, preserve export autonomy, and make outside powers bargain with Iran rather than over Iran.

## Empire Deforms Civilization

Empire deforms Iran when leverage becomes permanent disruption, partner alienation, factional escalation, or a posture that confirms adversary claims that Iran cannot be trusted with recognition.

## Statecraft Regulation

Regulation means controlled transit guarantees, sanctions-relief benchmarks, non-regime-change assurances, mediator channels, and restraint that turns leverage into settlement.
