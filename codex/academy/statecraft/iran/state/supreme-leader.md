## Role In Current State Form

The supreme leader carrier represents ultimate legitimacy, strategic red lines, and the authority to frame restraint as dignity rather than surrender.

## Civilizational Pattern Link

The supreme leader is the current ultimate-legitimacy carrier of [Iran state memory](../civilization/objects/state-memory.md): sovereignty, dignity, martyrdom/endurance, anti-domination, and refusal of humiliation. Use this profile to test whether restraint can be authorized as protection of the revolution and state rather than capitulation.

## Authority Base

- Formal authority: supreme constitutional / clerical authority.
- Informal authority: revolutionary legitimacy, resistance narrative, elite arbitration.
- Security / military authority: ultimate strategic direction over security institutions.
- Coalition authority: clerical, security, conservative, and institutional networks.
- Symbolic authority: sovereignty, dignity, resistance, martyrdom memory, anti-regime-change posture.

## Institutional Carrier

Durable instruments require alignment between supreme authority, government, foreign ministry, security institutions, and legal/public legitimacy channels.

## Red Lines

Terms that look like regime-change compliance, unilateral disarmament, humiliation framed as verification, or surrender of deterrent dignity can break carrying capacity.

## Settlement Credibility

Can authorize restraint when it is framed as reciprocal recognition, sanctions relief, deterrent preservation, dignity, or regional legitimacy.

## Binding Instrument Test

Can this authority frame restraint as protection of the revolution and state, rather than capitulation?
