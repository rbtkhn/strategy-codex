# Iran Civilization Seed Patterns

WORK only; not Record.

These seeds translate CIV-MEM into Iran-lane civilization patterns. State rules should draw from these patterns and the Iran empire folder, not directly from CIV-MEM.

## Dignity And Sovereignty

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–CORE–PERSIA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–DOCTRINE–PERSIA.md`.
- **Pattern:** Iran needs recognition as a sovereign actor, not management as an object of policy.
- **Statecraft use:** Build non-regime-change assurances, reciprocal recognition formulas, and dignity-preserving verification into proposals.
- **Failure mode:** Material relief fails because the language humiliates Iran or appears to bargain over Iran without Iran.

## Resistance And Martyrdom Grammar

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–STATE–PERSIA.md`; `docs/civilization-memory/book/APPLIED-THEOLOGY-TEST-MEM-PERSIA.md`.
- **Pattern:** Pressure can strengthen legitimacy when it is read as domination.
- **Statecraft use:** Convert resistance into regulated leverage: what Iran will reopen, restrain, or verify in exchange for recognition and relief.
- **Failure mode:** The instrument rewards permanent defiance and makes compromise look like betrayal.

## Plateau Continuity

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–INDEX–PERSIA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–ACHAEMENID–DYNASTY.md`.
- **Pattern:** Iran's geography and civilizational depth make survival and autonomy central.
- **Statecraft use:** Treat territorial integrity, export autonomy, and regional voice as successor-stable interests.
- **Failure mode:** A deal assumes the current regime is the whole Iranian question and misses deeper state continuity.
