## Case Anchor

The local reference case is enterprise agent governance: Agent 365 as a control-plane pattern, and Grace-Mar/strategy-codex as a local-first, human-gated analogue. The comparison is useful only if the boundary remains clear: enterprise control planes govern agent fleets; the local substrate protects authorship, provenance, and Record authority.

## Working Questions

- What can now act faster, cheaper, longer, or at larger scale?
- Who or what is the agent: model, tool, workflow, admin layer, human operator, institution, or hybrid?
- What objective is being optimized, and who set it?
- Who can inspect tool calls, memory, permissions, traces, receipts, and failures?
- Where does authority live: model context, enterprise tenant, local repo, gate, human judgment, or external policy?
- What can stop the system before it canonicalizes a mistake?
- What human role, dignity, attention pattern, or relationship is displaced?

## Counter-Reading

Agent governance can become a comfort story. Observability is not authority, traces are not judgment, and administrative control is not the same as humane alignment. Conversely, local sovereignty can become theater if it lacks receipts, rollback, and usable review paths.

## Worked Example: Compute As Public Control Plane

Use the data-center power front from [The Innermost Loop - 2026-05-17](innermost-loop-2026-05-17.md) when the question is not "what can the model do?" but "what substrate must society surrender for the model to keep scaling?"

- **Agent:** Hyperscaler-scale compute buildout, plus the utility and policy machinery that routes power toward frontier-model capacity.
- **Objective:** Secure enough energy, land, and infrastructure to maximize model capability and deployment speed.
- **Authority:** Split across labs, cloud operators, utilities, regulators, local governments, and capital providers. This is the warning: no single actor cleanly owns the public consequence.
- **Observability:** High at the capex and siting layer, weak at the legitimacy layer. We can often see the buildout before we can see who had the right to decide it.
- **Rollback:** Poor. Once financing, siting, and grid-routing decisions harden, reversal becomes more expensive politically and economically than initial approval.

### Boundary Warning

Public-resource allocation must not be treated as a mere optimization variable inside the compute race. If the intelligence control plane quietly captures the power-allocation control plane, governance becomes ceremonial.

### Human Meaning Check

The human office most at risk here is local civic judgment: the regulator, town, or public steward becomes the slow signature at the end of a pipeline whose real choices were made upstream by capability competition and capital urgency.

### Falsifier

This warning weakens if major AI buildouts can show all three at once:

- durable surplus power rather than zero-sum public tradeoff
- transparent public review with legible receipts
- real local veto or meaningful refusal power, not symbolic consultation

## Output Prompts

- Mark one boundary that must not be absorbed by enterprise tooling.
- Name one human skill or office that becomes weaker if the agent layer succeeds.
- Draft one test that would prove whether a system preserves authorship rather than merely logging activity.
