# Coffee D - Singularity Operating Sheet

WORK only; not Record.

## Purpose

Use Coffee D when the live question is agency under acceleration: AI substrate, agent control, alignment, displacement, recursive improvement, or human meaning under technical change. Coffee D is not the default route for monetization, offers, revenue, or public copy unless those are being examined as singularity test surfaces rather than handled as plain operations.

## Operating Thesis

Steward protects the system. Engineer builds the system. Strategist chooses direction. Singularity tests the future substrate those choices must survive.

Coffee D is WORK-first. Do not treat `candidates`, `recursion-gate`, or governed Record updates as part of the normal singularity pass.

## Horizon Stance

Work as if we may be in the foothills, not at the summit.

That means:

- treat multiple upward-bending fronts as a reason to inspect, not as permission to declare arrival
- do not treat a vivid demo, product launch, or one strong benchmark as enough evidence by itself
- anchor the pass to at least one source or existing workshop sheet before making a horizon claim
- classify the front under review: compute, interface, trust, labor, biology, or control plane
- end with one operator consequence: prepare, monitor, contain, or route away

Coffee D should help the operator act early without pretending the future is already settled.

## Coffee D Menu Use

A. Frontier pulse - pick one current acceleration signal and map why it matters.

B. Agent control plane - identify the acting system, permissions, receipts, rollback, and human gate.

C. Alignment/substrate warning - name what is optimizing, where authority lives, and what must not be absorbed.

D. Reuse artifact - turn the session into a sheet, checklist, bridge question, or next-watch item.

## Trial Protocol

Coffee D Singularity is under a three-session practical trial. Until three real uses have been reviewed, end each Coffee D session with:

- `Trial n/3`
- verdict: `useful`, `mixed`, or `weak`
- reusable artifact, if any
- next watch item, if any

Keep D only if it clarifies agency, control, substrate, displacement, or reuse better than Steward, Engineer, or Strategist would have done.

## Route Away From D

- Revenue, offers, positioning, sales, pricing, and public copy route by explicit `work-business`, `write`, `skill-write`, or named commercial request unless the operator is explicitly treating that business lane as a singularity-academy proving ground.
- Current-event judgment without a substrate or agency question routes to **C. Strategist**.
- Implementation, tests, scripts, and specs route to **B. Engineer**.
- Git, gate, integrity, branch, and repo stewardship route to **A. Steward**.

## Consolidated Live Test Cases

The workshop may intentionally absorb selected external-looking lanes when they are being used to test the singularity thesis against reality.

- `work-business`: use when the business is a live case for agentic leverage, recursive workflow improvement, control-plane questions, or labor compression.
- `work-cici`: use when the cohort, onboarding, or advisor loop is a live case for recursive AI assistance, agent-governed learning, or distributed beginner uplift.
- `work-dev`: use when agent runtimes, OpenClaw, external tools, evals, control planes, substrate, workbench paths, or code-intelligence loops are the live test surface for recursive technical leverage.

In those cases, keep the canonical lane files where they live and add one singularity question rather than renaming the whole task:

- Is the system improving itself, or only helping once?
- What human gate still matters?
- What durable leverage survives after the demo glow fades?

Keep the output in WORK notes, sheets, or comparisons. Do not turn the singularity pass into candidate staging overhead.

### `work-dev` frame

Use `work-dev` as the technical proving ground inside `singularity-academy`.

- thesis: recursive AI should not only assist one task; it should create durable technical leverage through better agent control planes, evals, substrate discipline, code intelligence, and reusable automation loops
- overlay clusters: OpenClaw and external agents, diagnostics and control planes, evals and benchmarks, workbench/interface artifacts, and code-intelligence pilots such as CodeGraph
- evidence that counts: faster or safer exploration, clearer impact analysis, stronger rollback/receipt surfaces, reusable technical workflows, and compounding gains that survive outside one lucky session
- weak evidence: isolated demos, one-off scripts, impressive model output without durable operator leverage, or convenience paths that blur authority and rollback
- control-plane question: where do permissions, receipts, rollback, memory, and human gate still live when more technical work is handed to agents or control layers?
- singularity value: if the pattern works here as well as in `work-business` and `work-cici`, the singularity thesis starts to look like a real systems claim rather than a business-only or onboarding-only story

### `work-cici` frame

Use `work-cici` as the human-uplift and coordination test surface inside `singularity-academy`.

- thesis: recursive AI should not only help an already-strong operator; it should also help newer or distributed people start, learn, coordinate, and finish real work more reliably
- evidence that counts: repeated task completion, lower activation energy, faster onboarding, reusable runbooks, better review loops, and gains that generalize beyond one person or one prompt
- weak evidence: one-off demos, impressive chats without durable workflow change, or operator heroics disguised as AI leverage
- control-plane question: what still requires human review, trust, permission, escalation, or rollback when AI is inserted into beginner or cohort workflows?
- singularity value: if the pattern works here as well as in `work-business`, the thesis starts to look like a system claim rather than a personal productivity trick

## Output Prompts

- Name the acceleration: what became faster, cheaper, more autonomous, more scalable, or more recursive?
- Name the horizon: is this a foothills signal, a possible-climb signal, or an overclaimed summit claim?
- Name the agent: human, model, tool, institution, workflow, market, or hybrid system?
- Name the control plane: who can inspect, permission, stop, roll back, or audit it?
- Name the boundary: what must not be absorbed into convenience?
- Name the displacement: what skill, role, institution, market, or meaning-structure is weakened or transformed?
- Name the reuse: what artifact should survive this session?
