# Apprentice Studio - 30-Day Pilot

WORK only; not Record.

## Purpose

`Apprentice Studio` is a 30-day pilot that uses:

- `Grace Gems` as the real-work substrate
- `cici-ai` as the onboarding and progress surface
- `singularity-academy` as the evaluation layer

The pilot tests one narrow claim: AI-guided beginners can complete repeatable, review-gated business tasks with durable improvement, not just produce impressive one-off chats.

This is a singularity test, not volunteer coordination dressed up with frontier language. The question is whether recursive AI scaffolds can lower activation energy, increase repeatable output, and produce reusable workflow improvements across more than one person.

## Month-One Boundaries

The first month is `back-office first`.

Allowed substrate:

- listing QA
- stone/provenance checks
- policy consistency checks
- market research summaries
- workflow cleanup notes
- draft-only customer message support

Not in month one:

- live customer sends
- pricing decisions
- order promises
- refunds
- sourcing claims not already documented
- any customer-facing truth change without review

All outputs remain WORK artifacts. `singularity-academy` does not stage `candidates` as part of this pilot's normal flow.

## Roles

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| `singularity-academy` | defines the thesis, tracks what is accelerating, and evaluates whether the loop is real |
| `Grace Gems` | supplies the real tasks, policies, and review gate |
| `cici-ai` Telegram | recruits apprentices and asks for one artifact at a time |
| `cici-ai` Core | owns instructions, safety boundary, and task templates |
| `cici-ai` Progress | tracks proof packets, reviewer dispositions, and weekly counts |
| Operator | assigns tasks, reviews outputs, decides reuse, and prevents unsafe live use |

## 30-Day Sequence

### Week 1 - first activation

- recruit 3-5 apprentices
- assign one safe Grace Gems task each
- require one evidence-linked artifact per person
- complete one proof packet per submitted task

### Week 2 - instruction improvement

- repeat the same style of task with cleaner prompts and examples
- compare first-task quality, speed, and confusion against week 1
- log what AI scaffolding reduced friction and what still needed operator rescue

### Week 3 - supervised stretch

- give returning apprentices one slightly harder but still review-gated task
- prefer second-pass work such as improved listing QA, better research synthesis, or higher-quality message drafts
- track whether corrections decrease or artifact reuse improves

### Week 4 - decision review

- review artifact quality, retention, and reviewer burden
- decide whether the loop is reusable, too fragile, or too operator-heavy
- identify at least one workflow or prompt improvement worth keeping

## Proof Loop

Every completed task needs:

- one linked artifact or evidence pointer
- one proof packet
- one reviewer disposition: `accepted`, `accepted with edits`, or `not yet reusable`

Weekly review must answer:

- did activation energy drop?
- did output quality become more reusable?
- did the operator spend less time rescuing basic execution?

## Control-Plane Reading

Use the pilot to inspect:

- where authority lives
- what AI is actually doing versus what the operator is still doing
- whether improvement compounds across people rather than staying trapped in one exceptional session
- whether beginner uplift can survive real review pressure

Weak evidence:

- one-off demos
- long chats without artifacts
- operator heroics misread as apprentice progress
- praise without reuse

Strong evidence:

- repeated task completion
- lower activation energy on later attempts
- better second-task quality
- one prompt, rubric, or workflow improvement reused by multiple people

## Month-One Success Threshold

- at least 3 participants attempt a real task
- at least 2 evidence-linked task completions
- at least 1 returning participant completes a second task
- at least 1 workflow or prompt improvement is reused across more than one person
