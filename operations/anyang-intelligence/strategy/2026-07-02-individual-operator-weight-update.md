## Capability opportunity

**Gap:** A large B2C pool pays for AI tool subscriptions but **does not update production weights** — they remain prompt consumers (high inference noise, zero shipped workflows). Market sells awareness; economic value requires **operator-grade weight updates** (workflow design, verification, embed).

**Pull:** Career switchers and solo knowledge workers need a **credibly scoped first weight update** before a 12-week team sprint — lower ACV but faster cycle count and top-of-funnel for Accelerator upsell.

**Constraint:** Do not launch B2C scale before **one team case study** exists from primary wedge (proof obligation). This brief defines **what to build next**, not what to market now.
