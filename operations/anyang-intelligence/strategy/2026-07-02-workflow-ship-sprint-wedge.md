## Product definition

**Name (working):** AI Operator Accelerator — Workflow Ship Sprint  
**SKU shape:** 12-week cohort · 70/20/10 · team size 3–4 optional  
**Delivery:** Weekly live session + async assignments + **ship milestones at weeks 4, 8, 12**  
**Outcome:** Each participant (or team) ships **one production AI workflow** with documented before/after (time saved, error reduction, or throughput — measurable, not vanity metrics)  
**Certification hook (internal):** Pass bar targets **80/100** on workflow rubric — calibrate at Builder pass; do not market externally until first cohort receipt exists

**Maps to STRATEGIC-PLAN:** Accelerator SKU (§3) · Phase 1 wedge (§7) · Ship + Embed execution mechanics · Growth engine feed (§8)
