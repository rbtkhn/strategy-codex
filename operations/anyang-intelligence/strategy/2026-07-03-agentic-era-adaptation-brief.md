## Agentic risk assessment (2027–2028)

| Trend | Hedge / leverage |
| --- | --- |
| Agent reliability crosses "good enough" for routine workflows | Curriculum refresh trigger: shift module emphasis from exception handling → orchestration design + audit trails |
| Self-improving agent curricula (vendor-provided) | Anyang moat = **closed-loop proof** + sovereign operator identity — not content volume |
| Automated habit formation (lighter human oversight) | Embed phase mechanics stay load-bearing; hybrid module documents **when** to reduce oversight with receipts |
| Enterprise multi-agent platforms mature | Position as **training layer** on top of platforms — governance playbooks portable across vendors |

**Falsifier to watch:** If orchestration evals show <10% human intervention on standard SME workflows by Q2 2027, revisit whether Hybrid Supervision module should become **Must** or merge into core rubric.
