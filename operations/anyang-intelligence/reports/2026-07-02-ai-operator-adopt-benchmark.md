## Part A — Framework overview

### What ADOPT is

The **ADOPT Method™** is the core framework of AI Operator — a practical AI training and transformation company. It is a **5-phase execution model** designed to turn scattered AI usage into sustained organizational behavior change and measurable business results.

**Phases:** **A**lign → **D**evelop → **O**perationalize → **P**ractice → **T**ransform

| Phase | Intent |
| --- | --- |
| **Align** | Pre-program surveys, address fears, leadership buy-in, goal setting, team formation for Innovation Competition. Moves people from "AI will replace me" to "AI is my partner." |
| **Develop** | 12 weeks of weekly live sessions + assignments. Fundamentals (prompting, custom GPTs), department workflows, advanced agents/integrations. Customized via surveys. |
| **Operationalize** | Innovation Competition — teams (3–4 people) identify challenges, build prototypes, ship production solutions with milestones (weeks 4/8/12). |
| **Practice** | 60+ days of daily 30-second AI usage logs, accountability partners, leaderboards, ROI checkpoints. Grounded in habit formation research (~66 days average). |
| **Transform** | Individual "ADOPT Method™ Certified AI Operator" and organizational "AI-Ready Organization" certifications. Governance (policies, playbooks), alumni community, scaling templates. |

### Delivery vehicles

| SKU | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Accelerator** | 12-week cohort program (most popular) |
| **Transformation** | Longer (6–12+ months) enterprise version with deeper integration |

### Strengths (framework)

- **Behavior change focus** — addresses skills fade, lack of habits, weak ownership; accountability and production output over theory
- **Measurable outcomes** — ROI reviews (week 6 checkpoint), shipped solutions (3+ target), documented savings, dual certification (individual + org). *Self-reported claims:* 1,000+ certified, 20,000+ hours saved/month aggregate, multi-million business impact
- **Practical and adaptive** — self-selected projects, industry customization, hybrid live + real work
- **Sustainability** — alumni community, quarterly check-ins (Transformation), governance frameworks
- **Execution discipline** — clear phases, milestones, scoring rubrics (80/100 to pass), experiential learning 70/20/10

### Limitations (framework)

- **Enterprise tilt** — best suited for teams/organizations; individuals may prefer self-paced bootcamps
- **Depth vs breadth** — strong on applied workflows; lighter on advanced ML engineering
- **Claims validation** — ROI multiples (9x–12x in case studies) are self-reported and aggregated; independent validation would strengthen credibility
- **Scale** — cohort + live elements may need heavy customization for very large enterprises

## Part B — Behavioral science foundations

### Core thesis

ADOPT explicitly grounds itself in behavioral science to address why most AI training fails: **skills are taught, but habits and organizational behavior do not stick**. It draws on habit formation, learning theory, social psychology, and behavior change research.

### 1. Habit formation (Practice phase)

- **66-day rule (not 21 days):** References University College London research (Phillippa Lally, 2009/2010) — habit automaticity averages **~66 days** (range 18–254). ADOPT implements **60+ days of daily tracking** to cover the formation window.
- Counters the persistent "21-day myth" (misinterpretation of Maxwell Maltz on physical adaptation).
- **Tiny Habits / low-barrier entry:** Daily **30-second** AI usage log aligns with BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits model — easy to start and repeat until automatic.

### 2. Learning and behavior change models

- **70/20/10 experiential learning** (Lombardo & Eichinger): ~70% doing (Innovation Competition, assignments), 20% social (accountability, leaderboards, cohorts), 10% formal (sessions). "You act your way into new thinking."
- **Cue-routine-reward loops** (Duhigg / Atomic Habits influences): daily logs + partners create cues and social rewards; Innovation Competition provides purpose through shipped solutions.
- **Social proof and accountability:** partners and leaderboards leverage social dynamics; group competition taps belonging and competition motivations.

### 3. Motivation, mindset, fear reduction (Align phase)

- Addresses psychological barriers: AI fear, skepticism, replacement anxiety. Identity shift to "AI partner/operator."
- **Self-Determination Theory** (autonomy, competence, relatedness) via customized goals, self-selected projects, team collaboration.
- Pre-program surveys enable personalization and buy-in.

### 4. Implementation intentions and accountability

- **If-then planning and milestones:** weeks 4/8/12 checkpoints, ROI reviews, explicit success criteria (Gollwitzer's implementation intentions research).
- **Gamification:** leaderboards, celebrations, 80/100 certification rubric — extrinsic structure building intrinsic competence.
- **Production focus (Operationalize):** shipping real solutions delivers **mastery experiences** (Bandura's self-efficacy theory).

### 5. Sustainability and relapse prevention (Transform phase)

- Governance playbooks, scaling templates, alumni community, quarterly check-ins.
- Organizational certification requires a **sustainability plan**.
- ROI documentation creates **evidence-based reinforcement**.

### Behavioral strengths

- Holistic multi-level: individual habits + team/social + organizational systems
- Evidence-informed without being academic
- High stickiness potential: micro-tracking + real output + social elements
- Measurable tie to business outcomes (shipped solutions, ROI, hours saved)

### Behavioral gaps (ADOPT and opportunity for Anyang)

| Gap | Note |
| --- | --- |
| Individual differences | Habit formation varies 18–254 days; fixed 60+ days is average, not personalized |
| Intrinsic motivation | Strong extrinsic structures; identity-based habits ("I am an AI Operator") underweighted |
| Relapse handling | Maintenance strong; explicit "missed day" protocols thin vs modern habit apps |
| AI tool churn | Framework updates curriculum; tying refresh to behavioral nudges could go deeper |

### Anyang hybrid opportunity

Complement Anyang's closed-loop intelligence layer and sovereign operator philosophy with proven habit science:

- 60–66 day micro-logs in operator certification tracks
- Workflow ship sprint (Innovation Competition analog) for `reports/` case studies
- Orient-phase fear addressing and 70/20/10 mix in China pilot for engagement
- **Anyang differentiators:** identity framing ("I operate AI systems"); curriculum factory refresh from market signals; explicit relapse protocol as design gap to close
