## Growth strategy

**Motion:** Proof-first **direct outbound + founder-led content** — not paid scale until week-12 case study exists in `reports/`.

**Phase 0 (now → first pilot enrolled):** Warm network + LinkedIn/YouTube **workflow demo seeds** that point to conversation, not checkout.

**Phase 1 (pilot running):** Document pilot in real time (with participant consent) for internal case-study draft — no public ROI multiples.

**Phase 2 (post wk-12 pass):** Publish one case study → turn on broader inbound (email list, optional landing page, light paid test).

**Channels (priority order):**

| Priority | Channel | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Direct outreach** (founder + warm intros) | Close first paid pilot team ($3k–$8k) |
| 2 | **LinkedIn** | Short workflow before/after posts; DM to ops/marketing leads |
| 3 | **YouTube** | 8–12 min "ship a workflow" demo — proof shape, not course trailer |
| 4 | **Email** (manual list) | Follow-up to engaged commenters / webinar attendees |
| **Deferred** | Paid ads, affiliate, enterprise outbound | Blocked by proof obligations |

**Not in this funnel:** 7-day free challenge (post-rubric top-of-funnel — separate Growth doc later), China WeChat cohort, enterprise Transformation pitch.

## Funnel design

```text
Awareness          Interest              Decision           Pilot
─────────          ────────              ────────           ─────
LinkedIn post  →  DM / call invite  →  Fit call (30m)  →  Pilot SOW + pay
YouTube demo   →  "Reply SHIP"      →  Team charter    →  Cohort kickoff wk 1
Warm intro     →  Share rubric PDF  →  Scope + price     →  Week 6 ROI internal
                                      (internal only)
```

### Stage definitions

| Stage | Goal | Exit criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Awareness** | Target team knows we ship workflows, not prompts | One engaged reply or call booked |
| **Interest** | Prospect sees rubric + 12-week map (WORK PDF or Notion) | Confirmed pain + 3–8 person team |
| **Decision** | Pilot scope, price, start date | Signed pilot agreement + payment |
| **Pilot** | Team completes program | Week 12 rubric pass + case study draft → `reports/` |

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |

## Key message

**Primary (ROI-driven, plain language):**

> Your team already uses AI — but nothing is in production. In 12 weeks we ship one workflow that runs in your real tools, with before/after numbers you can show your boss. Not a prompting course.

**Supporting lines (use one at a time — not a wall of claims):**

- "Pass bar is a production workflow, scored on a 100-point rubric — not a certificate for showing up."
- "Week 4 prototype, week 8 integration, week 12 production — or you don't graduate."
- "We don't scale marketing until we've published a real case study from a pilot like yours."

**Avoid (proof obligations + credibility):**

- ADOPT competitor comparisons in outbound
- "Certified AI operator" in cold outreach
- Income or headcount promises
- Behavior-change / 66-day habit claims before pilot design is filed

**Identity hook (Strategic Plan §10):** "AI is not learned — it is operated." Use only after interest stage, not cold open.

## Conversion path

### Step 1 — Awareness hook (LinkedIn example structure)

**Topic-first lede (required):** Name the workflow class (e.g. invoice intake, lead research, weekly report assembly) before any offer.

**Body:** 3 bullets — before pain, what shipped, one metric.  
**CTA:** "DM **SHIP** if your team has one workflow stuck in demo mode."

### Step 2 — Interest (DM / email)

Send internally (not public):

- Link or PDF: 1-page program summary from [module map](../builder/2026-07-02-accelerator-module-map.md) (week list only)
- Rubric pass bar summary (≥80/100, production required)
- Ask: team size, target workflow, start window

### Step 3 — Decision call (30 minutes)

| Block | Time | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pain + baseline | 10m | Confirm wk 1 charter feasibility |
| Scope | 10m | One workflow boundary; tool access |
| Pilot terms | 10m | Price, dates, success = rubric pass + ROI checkpoint |

**Close artifact:** One-page pilot memo (team, workflow, price, start date, facilitator) — operator review before send.

### Step 4 — Pilot enrollment

- Payment + calendar invite for wk 1 Orient
- Distribute [milestone templates](../builder/templates/) to team lead
- **Internal only:** schedule week 6 ROI filing to `reports/`

### Step 5 — Post-pilot (Growth unlock)

When week 12 passes:

- Publish redacted case study → `reports/`
- Repurpose LinkedIn/YouTube with **named workflow + metric** (operator-approved)
- Optional: open second cohort at list price band
