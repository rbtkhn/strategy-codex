- 1991 as humiliation, collapse, and cybernetic opening rather than industrial continuation
- humiliation to restoration as the main historical spine
- presidency and security-state recentralization as the heaviest late carriers
- United States and NATO as the main external pressure and recognition field
- Ukraine and sanctions as the decisive proof field
- restoration through pressure rather than neutral adaptation
