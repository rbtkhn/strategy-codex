## Five Excerpts

1. **Daniel Davis — walk-away theory**
The most important Davis excerpt is the core anti-escalation frame: stop digging deeper and exit the crisis before it worsens instead of pretending a bigger strike package can solve the problem.

2. **Marandi on non-negotiables**
The strongest Diesen/Marandi excerpt is the clear refusal to surrender sovereignty: Iran will not give up its peaceful nuclear program, enrichment, or control claims around the Strait of Hormuz.

3. **Dialogue Works / Marandi on changed Strait conditions**
The highest-signal operational excerpt is the claim that ships linked to the Gulf states that joined the war are being kept out of the Strait while other traffic can still move, which recasts Hormuz as selective coercive control rather than total closure.

4. **Mercouris on the Victory Day deterrence moment**
The most important Mercouris excerpt is the argument that Russian warnings, including implied Oreshnik threats against central Kiev, were taken seriously enough by Washington that Zelensky was pressured into a short ceasefire.

5. **Davis on systemic economic consequence**
The broadest excerpt in the set is Davis’s warning that the oil and supply shock could push the global economy toward recession or even depression-level damage if the war is not stopped quickly.
