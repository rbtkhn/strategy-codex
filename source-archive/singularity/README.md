# Singularity Source Archive

This namespace is the canonical full-source archive for repo-root `singularity/`.

Canonical paths:

- `source-archive/singularity/innermost-loop/<slug>.md` — Innermost Loop newsletter raw captures
- `source-archive/singularity/moonshots/<slug>.md` — Moonshots podcast verbatim transcript captures (operator paste or fetched captions)

Use this tree for workshop-native full source captures only. Interpretation, longitudinal synthesis, and academy-facing sheets belong in `singularity/workshop/sheets/`, not here.

## Intake triad (operator protocol)

After any external burst (podcast, wire, frontier-lab paper):

1. **Archive** — verbatim capture under this tree (`moonshots/` or `innermost-loop/`).
2. **Synthesize** — workshop sheet in [`singularity/workshop/sheets/`](../../singularity/workshop/sheets/) with **Boundary Warnings**.
3. **Verify** — triage `fact-check` (or deeper pass on headline metrics) **before** promoting claims into [`singularity/notes/`](../../singularity/notes/) or statecraft daily.

**Promotion rule:** No load-bearing vendor statistics in promoted notes without a **Verify receipt** on the workshop sheet (Supported / Contradicted / Unclear / Out of scope) and primary cite where possible.

**Review seats (4/4 before promote):** taste (lane + artifact class), provenance (archive exists), verify (receipt table), ship (commit intent + unverified list at handoff).

See [`singularity/notes/review-as-bottleneck-and-interpretive-pause.md`](../../singularity/notes/review-as-bottleneck-and-interpretive-pause.md).
