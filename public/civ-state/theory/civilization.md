# Civilization

**Inherited order** — legitimacy substrate, continuity-bearing form, and civilizational inheritance.

Status: Active doctrine  
Surface: `civ-state`  
Purpose: govern reads where legitimacy substrate, symbolic inheritance, or continuity-bearing form is load-bearing  
Last updated: 2026-06-17

Not to be confused with the whole-work opening essay [*Civilization and Empire*](../introduction.md) — that essay introduces the book; this page names the **civilization** governing term.

## Why this term

Civilization is what a people **stores**: inherited order, legitimating grammar, and continuity-bearing form that make a polity intelligible to itself across rupture. When the read is civilization-heavy, the fight is usually over what must be preserved, recognized, or restored — not merely who commands today.

## Definition

Civilization is inherited order: the legitimacy substrate, continuity-bearing pattern, symbolic inheritance, and civilizational form that outlive any single ruler or regime.

## Governing question

Ask first:

> Is this object civilization-heavy — about inheritance, legitimacy substrate, or continuity-bearing form?

Useful distinctions:

- legitimacy substrate, sacred civilizational form, or continuity claim → usually **civilization-heavy**
- if outward machinery dominates while inheritance thins → suspect [empire](empire.md) or [entropy](entropy.md)

## Causal connections

### Empire

[Civilization](civilization.md) stores; [empire](empire.md) scales outward. Civilization is not self-defending: without sufficient instrument, inheritance remains symbolically real but poorly carried. When empire outruns civilization, the degrading beat of [entropy](entropy.md) begins.

### Entropy

[Entropy](entropy.md) names what happens when inherited form thins while machinery still runs — civilization hollowed but rhetorically preserved. The civilizational layer may lose carry even when sacred grammar survives as language.

### Memory

[Memory](memory.md) is what civilization **must preserve** across rupture — wound, restoration, succession, humiliation. Memory is the retrieval entry in `memory → legitimacy → carrier → pressure → settlement`.

### Faith

[Faith](faith.md) often authorizes civilizational form — mandate, covenant, orthodoxy, forbidden bargains. When legitimacy is sacred or moral before it is procedural, faith and civilization tend to move together.

## Failure modes

- **civilization without empire** — inheritance symbolically real but lacks carrier, reach, or instrument to defend itself
- **civilization mistaken for empire** — universal claims or coercive reach treated as if they were continuity-bearing form alone
