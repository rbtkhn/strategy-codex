# Memory

Status: Active doctrine  
Surface: `civ-state`

**Desire** is retired as a governing term. Appetite, spectacle, and compensatory overreach → [Empire](empire.md).

**Two roles on one page:** (1) **Governing term** when wound, succession, or continuity claim carries the object. (2) **Temporal grammar** when phase or era governs after another term wins — open the [Civilizational rhythm](#civilizational-rhythm) or [Era law](#era-law) sections without forcing a memory-heavy diagnosis.

## Why this term

Without memory, time and rhythm are meaningless for civilization. Chronology without continuity claim is inert; phase without pattern memory is noise. Memory names what a civilization **must preserve** across rupture — and how it **remembers** recurrence (beautify → amplify → degrade) and form-historical carry (Ancient → Cybernetic). It is also the **first step** in whole-work retrieval movement.

## Definition

Memory is continuity-bearing recollection: inheritance, humiliation, restoration, loss, sacrifice, recurrence, rupture-tracking, **comparative pattern memory** (civilizational rhythm), and **form-historical memory** (era law).

## Governing question

Ask first:

> Is this object memory-heavy — about continuity, wound, restoration, succession, or inherited claim?

When **phase** governs (after a governing term is named):

> Where is this case in beautify → amplify → degrade?

When **era shelf** governs:

> Which form-historical age carries this inheritance and instrument — and where does the 1991 boundary fall?

If behavior feels excessive or theatrical while the civilizational read is clear, open [Empire](empire.md) (amplification or overreach acting on memory).

## Causal connections

### Civilization

[Memory](memory.md) stores what [civilization](civilization.md) must preserve. Civilization without memory-bearing continuity loses intelligibility; memory without civilizational form becomes grievance theater.

### Empire

[Empire](empire.md) scales what memory must defend or restore. The **amplifies** beat of [Civilizational rhythm](#civilizational-rhythm) maps to empire-heavy phases.

### Entropy

Historical shocks — war, revolution, plague, famine — often **distort** memory: wound sacredized, succession frozen, revenge cycle without renewal. The **degrades** beat often surfaces as historical [entropy](entropy.md). [Memory](memory.md) without renewal stays a **memory** read (grievance theater, paralysis), not a substitute for naming the shock on [Entropy](entropy.md).

### Faith

[Faith](faith.md) often **sacralizes** memory — covenant, martyrdom, mandate lost and restored, forbidden humiliation. When wound becomes orthodoxy without renewal, read faith + memory together before widening to empire or entropy.

### Science

[Science](science.md) can **administer** memory — archives, census, succession law, cybernetic identity rails. When continuity is stored procedurally rather than narratively, science-order may carry what looks like a memory read; distinguish living succession from database persistence.

## Civilizational rhythm

**Civilizational motion** names how inherited form and outward instrument move together across cases — not a flat rise-and-fall cycle. Read [Civilization and Empire](../docs/introduction.md) for the opening essay.

History repeats a recognizable rhythm:

```text
civilization beautifies → empire amplifies → entropy degrades
```

| Beat | Verb | Term page |
|------|------|-----------|
| Civilization | beautifies | [civilization.md](civilization.md) |
| Empire | amplifies | [empire.md](empire.md) |
| Entropy | degrades | [entropy.md](entropy.md) |

The three verbs map to three governing terms — full definitions live on those pages. When empire outruns civilization, the braid **frays** on the **degrades** beat — often as historical [entropy](entropy.md). Open [entropy.md](entropy.md) for the shock typology.

The rhythm is **recurrent**, not a closed loop. After degradation, cases may rupture, mutate into residue, or reconstitute under strain. Do not assume automatic return to beautifying on the same carrier.

## Era law

Era cuts are **form-historical**: they mark shifts in how inherited order, outward instrument, and present carrier are carried — not merely calendar dates.

```text
Ancient → Medieval → Colonial → Industrial → Cybernetic
476       1453       1815       1991         post-1991
```

| Era | Closes at | Form-historical meaning |
|-----|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Ancient** | `476` | Founding form, law-religion synthesis, first imperial universalism |
| **Medieval** | `1453` | Continuity through transformation, reconcentration, bridge forms |
| **Colonial** | `1815` | Outward projection, corridor leverage, imperial competition |
| **Industrial** | `1991` | Mass-state, revolution, party-state, industrial war |
| **Cybernetic** | post-`1991` | Deterrence, sanctions, networks, platform authority |

Prefer **Cybernetic** over **Digital** for post-1991 command form.

Each era shifts the balance among [civilization](civilization.md), [empire](empire.md), [faith](faith.md), [science](science.md), [memory](memory.md), and [entropy](entropy.md). **Industrial** shelves often carry revolution and industrial war as entropy manifestations; **Ancient** and **Medieval** shelves often carry plague, famine, and succession war.

| Volume | Era files exposed |
|--------|-------------------|
| China, Persia, Rome | Ancient · Medieval · Colonial · Industrial · Cybernetic |
| Russia, America | Medieval · Colonial · Industrial · Cybernetic |

Late opening ≠ weaker civilization-state seriousness. Per-volume coverage: [Era spine](../docs/era-spine.md). See [Volume Map](../volumes/README.md).

**Industrial / Cybernetic split:** Industrial shelves terminate at `1991`; Cybernetic begins after — load-bearing for Persia, China, Russia, America.

Name governing term and beat before era file descent.

## Retrieval movement

Descent after motion is named:

```text
memory → legitimacy → carrier → pressure → settlement
```

**Motion** = what history tends to do ([Civilizational rhythm](#civilizational-rhythm)). **Retrieval** = how the operator descends the object — start with what continuity claim or wound the object carries.

## Failure modes

- **memory without renewal** — continuity becomes grievance theater or paralysis
- **memory mistaken for mere history** — chronology without continuity claim or succession stake
- **beat misplacement** — historical shock read as mere policy friction, or amplification read as civilizational renewal
- **rhythm substituted for case depth** — beat named without opening volume or term pages
- **era before governing term** — chronology chosen while civilization/empire/faith/science is still unclear
- **1991 boundary ignored** — post-Cold War command read through Industrial shelves alone
