## Why era law exists

Era cuts are **form-historical**: they mark shifts in how inherited order, outward instrument, and present carrier are carried — not merely calendar dates.

## Definition

The shared spine:

```text
Ancient → Medieval → Colonial → Industrial → Cybernetic
476       1453       1815       1991         post-1991
```

| Era | Closes at | Form-historical meaning |
|-----|-----------|-------------------------|
| **Ancient** | `476` | Founding form, law-religion synthesis, first imperial universalism |
| **Medieval** | `1453` | Continuity through transformation, reconcentration, bridge forms |
| **Colonial** | `1815` | Outward projection, corridor leverage, imperial competition |
| **Industrial** | `1991` | Mass-state, revolution, party-state, industrial war |
| **Cybernetic** | post-`1991` | Deterrence, sanctions, networks, platform authority |

Prefer **Cybernetic** over **Digital** for post-1991 command form.

## Governing question

Ask first:

> Which form-historical age is carrying this inheritance and instrument — and where does the 1991 boundary fall?
