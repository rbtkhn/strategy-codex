## Short synthesis

**Republic → principate** is the volume’s **transformation hinge**: the chain **does not break**, but the **instrument** changes register. **Civilization** holds what is inherited — civic law, citizenship grammar, republican ethic, co-primary Greek/Latin media. **Empire** names who **institutionalizes** the new form — Caesar and Augustus as hinges, principate as durable machinery.

**Memory** places the node once on `#chain-spine` so mythic substrate (Romulus, Aeneas) does not steal the political opener. **Faith** and **science** stay cross-reads: sacred order and procedural law are **adjacent**, not the primary fracture surface at this beat. **Entropy** correctly reads **empty** — confusing transformation with rupture would mis-route **476** and collapse the Civ↔Empire↔Entropy delineation table.

The hexagonal lesson: continuity can **mutate form** without a shock row — the membrane asks *who owns inheritance* (civ) vs *who owns the instrument* (empire) while the chain keeps moving.
