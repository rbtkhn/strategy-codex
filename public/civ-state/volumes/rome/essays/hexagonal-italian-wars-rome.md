## Short synthesis

This node teaches **split rupture ownership** inside one peninsula arc. **Empire** names the **successor_instrument theater** — French kings, Naples, Borgia condotte, Charles V pivot — without elevating any participant to Roman chain head. That is **overreach and competition**, not the same thing as **entropy** owning a universal rupture.

**Civilization** holds the **ethic** layer: Medici patronage as partial-inheritance retrieval, Savonarola as prophetic shock adjacent to rupture, Michelangelo as sacral-classical grammar under papal commission. **Faith** authorizes and strains simultaneously — papal instrument peak (Alexander VI adjacency) meets Sack shock on sacred-art carry.

**Entropy** must keep the membrane honest: the **Italian Wars** row stays **`comparative`** — peninsula comparative strain with `contested-weight` tags — while only the **Sack of Rome (1527)** earns **`rupture-primary`**. Confusing long war with universal rupture collapses the same membrane **1453** and **476** entries defend.
