## Short synthesis

**1054** confirms **archetype D**: the load-bearing fracture is **sacred truth-order**, not imperial jurisdiction or procedural codification. **Faith** owns **`rupture-primary`**; **entropy** correctly reads **`cross-ref only`** — the volume's standing exception to “rupture-primary lives on entropy.”

**Memory** indexes the node on `#faith-spine` and exports the **1054–1453** sacred-language fork without duplicating chain-head logic. **Empire** shows institutions **persist** through schism — patriarchates, papacy, HRE branches — so the shock is **authorization**, not the disappearance of outward machinery. **Civilization** holds sacred **ethic** inheritance; **science** stays thin until later scholastic/canon hybrid rows.

The hexagonal lesson paired with **1453**: 1054 splits **sacred carry** while the **bridge-carrier** survives; 1453 terminates the bridge. Collapsing them loses the membrane.
