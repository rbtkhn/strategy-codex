## Short synthesis

**529** is the wave-1 node that shows a **hinge without a new entropy rupture**. **476** already broke western imperial jurisdiction; Justinian's commission **restitutes procedural grammar** in governable Latin — a **science** and **codification_medium** event first, an **empire** hinge second.

**Memory** places the row on `#science-spine-eastern` so the node does not sprawl across every term file. **Civilization** names what is inherited: the juridical ethic of making inheritance **textual** after strain. **Faith** stays thin — canon–civil overlap is real but subordinate to procedural encoding. **Entropy** correctly reads **empty** for primary rupture: confusing codification with shock would collapse the same membrane that keeps **Italian Wars comparative** separate from **Sack rupture-primary**.

The hexagonal lesson: not every load-bearing Roman moment is a rupture — some are **restitution projects** that preserve grammar through codification while carriers remain fractured.
