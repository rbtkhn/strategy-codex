## Roman law — entropy lens

Roman law under **rupture**: western fall, schism, reformation fracture, plague, revolution — when codes survive as **rhetoric without carry**. The governing question is which shock broke mandate, courts, succession, or provincial administration — not routine legal dispute.

- **Western fall** — imperial jurisdiction without imperial body
- **Schism and reformation** — fractured universal law-bearing
- **Law after shock** — performed legitimacy vs living application

When war, revolution, or demographic collapse governs, govern **entropy** first; distinguish from empire-overreach ([Empire (Rome)](empire.md)).
