## Roman law — science lens

Roman law is **codification and procedure**: how inheritance becomes governable text across rupture. The chain runs *Twelve Tables* → praetorian edicts → *Corpus Juris Civilis* — jurists, institutes, applied competence — not laboratories, but **verification and administrative truth-order**.

- **Codification** — systematization after imperial strain (Justinian)
- **Jurists and institutes** — teachable, applicable law
- **Governable text** — continuity stored as procedure

When the seam is compilation, application, or expert class authority, govern **science** first; pair with [Civilization (Rome)](rome-civilization.md) — science **encodes** civilization here; it does not replace it.
