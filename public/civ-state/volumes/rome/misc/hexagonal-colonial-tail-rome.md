## Short synthesis

The **colonial procedural tail** shows Rome's grammar **exporting** through **instruments** that are explicitly **not** sovereign chain heads. **Empire** names the maritime-colonial machinery — Castile/Portugal Atlantic openers, personal unions, codified export — while **civilization** names what **disperses** as **partial inheritance** in Latin America.

**Science** owns the **procedural** story: civil-law transplantation, Siete Partidas lineage, post-independence carry without a second papal-class head. **Faith** authorizes through **mission instruments** under **`contested-weight`** — sacred grammar travels, but colonial history cannot be naively Romanized. **Entropy** reads **wind-down**: instruments terminate, carry scatters — parallel to science's contested export tail, **not** a replacement for **476** or **1453** rupture-primary rows.

**Memory** indexes without elevating colonial branches to chain head. The membrane lesson: **procedural and sacred carry can outlive the instrument** that carried them — dispersion is not restoration.
