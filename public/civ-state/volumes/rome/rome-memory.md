## Sacred grammar — continuity and chain (Rome)

Roman sacred grammar survives the republic-to-empire mutation, Christianization, western collapse, Eastern Roman continuation, schism, papal narrowing, and Vatican residue. The carrier changes repeatedly, but the grammar of universal order through law and sacred legitimacy remains legible.

*Transformed continuity under fractured sovereignty — grammar persists, sovereignty splits, residue carries.*

## Roman law — memory lens

Roman law is **legal continuity**: succession of codes, East/West split, and restitution of what must not be lost. Rome's chain — `Republic → … → Pope Leo XIV` — survives partly because **legal memory** migrates carriers: republic, empire, Eastern Rome, papal residue.

- **Succession of codes** — restitution projects (Justinian)
- **East/West split** — one grammar, fractured administration
- **Restitution** — what humiliation or rupture must be reversed in law-memory

When the seam is continuity claim, successor order, or forbidden loss, govern **memory** first; place era via [Era law](../../theory/memory.md#era-law) (Ancient → Medieval hinge load-bearing for codification).
