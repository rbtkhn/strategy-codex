# America Secondary Sources - Colonial

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the colonial America primary shelf when founding contradiction, slavery, constitutional formation, settler-republic expansion, or the Atlantic transfer need sharper chronology or anti-hagiographic correction.

## Core Clarifiers

- Bernard Bailyn, for ideological origins and Atlantic argument.
- Gordon Wood, for constitutional and republican transformation.
- Edmund Morgan, *American Slavery, American Freedom*, for contradiction at the constitutional root.
- Winston Churchill, shorter recurring use from *A History of the English-Speaking Peoples*, for Anglo carry across the Atlantic without making him the sole founding interpreter.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Jill Lepore and related critical founding scholarship, as a correction against sanctified founding compression.
- Slavery and settler-colonial scholarship, as a correction against reading constitutional inheritance without structural domination.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is declaration language, constitutional text, legal wording, or founding self-description. Open this shelf when the difficulty is contradiction, Atlantic inheritance, or keeping the era from collapsing into either civic scripture or pure denunciation.
