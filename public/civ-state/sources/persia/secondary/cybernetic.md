# Persia Secondary Sources - Cybernetic

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the cybernetic Persia primary shelf when deterrence, sanctions survival, Gulf/Hormuz leverage, regime durability, or negotiation-floor reading need stronger present-tense clarification or counterweight against collapse fantasy.

## Core Clarifiers

- Vali Nasr, for political economy, regional strategy, and durable statecraft incentives.
- Suzanne Maloney, for sanctions, political economy, and regime durability without romanticism.
- Trita Parsi, for negotiation architecture, sanctions bargaining, and external-policy sequencing.
- Afshon Ostovar, for force structure and regional military posture.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Maloney and related scholarship, as a correction against triumphalist deterrence readings.
- Parsi and external-policy critics, as a correction against regime-collapse or coercive-fantasy readings that refuse the actual bargaining floor.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is doctrine, official speech, sanctions language, or negotiation wording. Open this shelf when the problem is how to read deterrence, survivability, and bargaining room without collapsing into either apocalypse or romantic resilience.
