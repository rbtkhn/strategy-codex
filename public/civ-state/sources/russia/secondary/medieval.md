# Russia Secondary Sources - Medieval

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the medieval Russia primary shelf when chronicle-bearing Rus, Orthodoxy, Horde pressure, Muscovite regathering, or late-opening documentary discipline need stronger chronology and anti-myth correction. This file owns the main Russia-side explanation of why Will Durant is useful here: broad civilizational synthesis and comparative readability, but not specialist Russian final authority.

## Core Clarifiers

- Simon Franklin and Jonathan Shepard, *The Emergence of Rus*, for Kievan formation and documentary grounding.
- Janet Martin, *Medieval Russia*, for chronology, political sequence, and regathering context.
- Dimitri Obolensky and related Orthodox/Byzantine scholarship, for sacred and civilizational carry.
- Will Durant, *The Story of Civilization*, used as broad comparative synthesis support where the late-opening chain needs readable civilizational placement.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Charles Halperin and related Horde scholarship, as a correction against pure-national teleology and simple Mongol interruption storytelling.
- Specialist Muscovy scholarship, as a correction against reading medieval Russia as already fully modern-autocratic in finished form.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is chronicle language, ecclesial wording, or Muscovite self-description. Open this shelf when chronology, late-opening discipline, or anti-myth correction is the real need.
