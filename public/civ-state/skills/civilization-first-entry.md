# Civilization-first entry

Enter the book **by case and sovereignty chain** when inheritance ownership matters more than abstract governing vocabulary.

## Use when

The real question is:

- **which civilization-state owns this inheritance**
- **where the sovereignty chain opens**
- **what present carrier bears it now**

Prefer [governing-term-first](governing-term-first.md) instead when the case is known but **which term is load-bearing** is not (legitimacy vs instrument vs entropy vs faith vs science vs memory).

## Core law

Constitutional volume order (sovereignty-chain emergence):

```text
China → Persia → Rome → Russia → America
```

Within each volume:

```text
volume introduction → Civilization chapter → Empire chapter
```

The whole work still opens with [Civilization and Empire](../docs/introduction.md) (opening essay — not the book title), then [Reader Guide](../docs/reader-guide.md), then this entry path.

## Steps

1. **Confirm case ownership** — which civilization-state case carries the inheritance under pressure? If unclear, scan [Volume map](../volumes/README.md) doorways before comparing cases.

2. **Open the volume doorway** — that case's `README.md` and thematic `introduction.md` (e.g. Mandate of Heaven, Order of Asha — **not** the whole-work opening essay).

3. **Read Civilization then Empire** — continuity and legitimacy substrate first, outward instrument second. See [Reader Guide](../docs/reader-guide.md#how-to-read-one-civilization-volume).

4. **Open sacred grammar** when legitimacy or truth-order governs — [Reader Guide § Sacred grammar](../docs/reader-guide.md#sacred-grammar-volume-local) and the volume's `sacred-grammar.md`.

5. **Descend to sources** when evidence, era, or attribution trouble appears — run [source-lattice-read](source-lattice-read.md) (`sources/{civ}/` shelves, not volume-local stubs).

6. **Descend to sub-lenses** only after Civilization and Empire have clarified pressure geometry — `geo-strategy`, `secret-history`, `game-theory` beneath Empire.

7. **Cross-case comparison last** — [Cross-case recurrence](../essays/cross-case-recurrence-and-sovereignty.md) after one volume grammar is clear.

## Reading habits (carry through the pass)

- order before moral theater
- legitimacy before raw force
- equilibrium before maximal declaration
- settlement possibility before rhetorical climax

- Open a volume introduction as if it were [Civilization and Empire](../docs/introduction.md)
- Compare Rome and America (or any pair) before each case's chain is legible on its own terms
- Jump to empire chapters when the legitimacy substrate is still unsettled
- Treat **civ-state** as a reader title — the book is **Civilizational Statecraft**
