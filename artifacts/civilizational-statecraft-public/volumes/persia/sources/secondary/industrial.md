# Persia Secondary Sources - Industrial

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the industrial Persia primary shelf when oil sovereignty, coup memory, revolution, war endurance, and institutional survival through 1991 need sharper chronology or stronger counterweight.

## Core Clarifiers

- Ervand Abrahamian, for revolution, political social forces, and state mutation.
- Homa Katouzian, for Mossadegh, modern Iranian state difficulty, and structural context.
- Kenneth Pollack or equivalent Iran-Iraq War scholarship only where campaign and state-survival sequencing need clarification.
- Michael Axworthy, for broad modern-Iran synthesis when transition compression is needed.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Abrahamian and coup scholarship as a correction against reading 1953 or 1979 through thin Cold War cliche alone.
- War and sanctions scholarship that corrects both romantic revolutionary readings and pure regime-pathology readings.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is constitutional-revolutionary language, official doctrine, or war-state self-description. Open this shelf when chronology, institutional mutation, or coup-to-revolution sequencing is the real difficulty.
