# Imperial Republic

*The latest formal chain and the deliberately contested edge case.*

America is included as the deliberately contested case: not the cleanest civilization-state example, but essential because the Anglo-American sovereignty chain from Alfred to the present clarifies what happens when civilizational inheritance, maritime empire, settler-republic statehood, and liberal-universal claims sit under one increasingly strained sovereign form. Alfred the Great is the foundational continuity sovereign of the longer chain, not the sovereign beginning of America proper. Read the Anglo-American continuity passing through later American concentration.

Civilization beautifies through constitutional inheritance and civil-religious legitimacy; empire amplifies through maritime command, alliance systems, and universal liberal claim; civilizational entropy when inheritance thins into proceduralism, when liberal-universal empire outgrows the republic, and when sovereignty loses thick legitimacy. The strain helps define the civilization-state concept by contrast.

**Sovereignty chain (narrative):** Anglo constitutional and civil-religious inheritance from Alfred; American republic founding (1776); constitutional carrier (1789); contemporary United States as latest expression.

**Sacred / civilizational grammar:** Anglo constitutional inheritance, Protestant and civil-religious legitimacy, maritime-commercial order, republican self-conception. See [America sacred grammar](../../volumes/america/sacred-grammar.md).

**Present carrier:** contemporary United States as latest sovereign expression of the longer Anglo-American chain.

**Failure mode to avoid:** flattening America into pure invention after 1776, or seamless Anglo continuity with no rupture; ignoring internal contest over continuity and empire together.
