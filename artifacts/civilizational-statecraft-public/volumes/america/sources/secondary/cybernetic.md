# America Secondary Sources - Cybernetic

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the cybernetic America primary shelf when post-1991 unipolar command, 9/11, alliance management, dollar-sanctions machinery, or fragmentation pressure need stronger present-tense clarification or counterweight against triumphalist or terminal-decline readings.

## Core Clarifiers

- Andrew Bacevich, for empire critique, military instrument, and republic-versus-command strain.
- Stephen Wertheim, for primacy doctrine and post-Cold War strategic self-understanding.
- Adam Tooze, for crisis, finance, and system-scale command context.
- Winston Churchill appears only by brief inherited-chain echo here, not as the main explanatory authority, when Anglo continuity still clarifies how the shelf narrates itself.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Daniel Immerwahr and related empire scholarship, as a correction against mainland-only American self-understanding.
- Bacevich, Wertheim, and system historians together, as a correction against both unipolar permanence and melodramatic collapse storytelling.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is official doctrine, sanctions language, alliance speech, or constitutional-state wording. Open this shelf when the problem is how to read post-1991 command under fragmentation without drifting into either imperial triumphalism or instant-decline cliche.
