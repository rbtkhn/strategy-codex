# America Secondary Sources - Industrial

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the industrial America primary shelf when preserved union, industrial ascent, Civil War and Reconstruction, Cold War command, or the 1991 endpoint need stronger chronology or anti-innocence correction.

## Core Clarifiers

- James McPherson, for Civil War chronology, scale, and preserved-union reading.
- Eric Foner, for Reconstruction, constitutional mutation, and contradiction after union preservation.
- Paul Kennedy or Walter McDougall, for industrial-maritime rise and great-power context.
- Winston Churchill, brief recurring use only where Anglo continuity and English-speaking world narration clarify the longer chain without replacing specialist American modern history.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Reconstruction and race-state scholarship, as a correction against pure union-preservation triumphalism.
- Empire and Cold War historians, as a correction against reading industrial America only as domestic constitutional achievement without global instrument formation.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is constitutional amendment, presidential language, war-state wording, or official Cold War doctrine. Open this shelf when the difficulty is contradiction through scale, sequencing from Civil War to Cold War command, or correcting exceptionalist innocence.
