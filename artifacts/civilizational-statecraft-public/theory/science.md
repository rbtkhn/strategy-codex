# Science

## Why this term

**Science** names how claims are authorized through method, verification, expertise, administration, and control — including cybernetic command, risk models, and procedural legality. It is not reducible to laboratories.

## Definition

Science is technical, procedural, expert, managerial, cybernetic, or evidentiary truth-order: how an actor authorizes action through verification and administrative competence.
