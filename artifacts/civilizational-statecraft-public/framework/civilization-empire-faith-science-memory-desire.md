# Civilizational Statecraft Framework

Status: Active doctrine
Surface: `civ-state`
Purpose: provide the primary CIV-STATE interpretive frame for retrieval, routing, and statecraft translation
Last updated: 2026-05-26

Short form:

`civilization / empire`
`faith / science`
`memory / desire`

## Why this doctrine exists

The older `power / truth / time` triad named something real, but it remained too compressed for active CIV-STATE use.

The cleaner active frame is the **Civilizational Statecraft Framework**:

- **civilization**
- **empire**
- **faith**
- **science**
- **memory**
- **desire**

This is not six disconnected topics. It is three structural pairs:

- **civilization <-> empire**
- **faith <-> science**
- **memory <-> desire**

These pairs clarify the main governing tensions of modern statecraft:

- civilization stores inherited order while empire amplifies outward reach
- faith and science operate as coequal truth-orders that can align, hybridize, or compete
- memory carries continuity while desire drives acceleration, prestige, appetite, and rupture

The old triad remains useful as historical residue, but it is no longer the primary active doctrine surface.

The older `god / lit / art / geo / war / peace` families also survive, but only as downstream object classes. They are no longer coequal explanatory doctrine. The new six name **what governs**; the older six name **where that governance becomes visible** in specific statecraft objects.

Terminology note:

- `civ-state` means **civilization state**
- **Civilizational Statecraft Framework** means the governing six-part doctrine
- `statecraft-framework` is the operational skill/interface that applies that doctrine
- `statecraft` remains the downstream operating layer for routing, drafting, and transaction design

## The six terms

### Civilization

Civilization is inherited order: the legitimacy substrate, continuity-bearing pattern, symbolic inheritance, and civilizational form that make a people intelligible to itself across rupture.

### Empire

Empire is outward instrument: the amplification stack that projects power through coercion, procedure, finance, infrastructure, law, alliance, logistics, and reach.

### Faith

Faith is sacred, moral, symbolic, covenantal, or civil-religious truth-order. It names how an actor understands rightful authority, forbidden bargains, meaning, duty, and moral limit.

### Science

Science is technical, procedural, expert, managerial, cybernetic, or evidentiary truth-order. It names how an actor authorizes claims through method, verification, expertise, administration, and control.

### Memory

Memory is continuity-bearing recollection: inheritance, humiliation, restoration, loss, sacrifice, recurrence, and rupture-tracking that shape what a civilization thinks it must preserve.

### Desire

Desire is appetite and acceleration: prestige hunger, novelty drive, expansion impulse, resentment, compensatory ambition, theatricality, overreach, and the urge to escape inherited limit.

## Governing-layer question

Ask first:

> Which of the six is actually governing this object?

Not every object needs all six equally. The discipline is to identify the governing layer or governing pair before drafting.

Useful first distinctions:

- if the object is about legitimacy substrate, continuity-bearing inheritance, or sacred civilizational form, it is usually **civilization-heavy**
- if it is about outward machinery, coercive or procedural reach, sanctions, command, corridors, or platform leverage, it is usually **empire-heavy**
- if the fight is about sacred authorization, covenant, providence, mandate, orthodoxy, or moral boundary, it is usually **faith-heavy**
- if the fight is about expertise, technical administration, verification, risk models, cybernetic control, or procedural authority, it is usually **science-heavy**
- if continuity, humiliation, restoration, succession, or inherited wound governs the read, it is usually **memory-heavy**
- if acceleration, prestige, spectacle, grievance appetite, novelty, or compensatory overreach governs the read, it is usually **desire-heavy**

## Structural pairs

### Civilization and empire

Civilization stores the inheritance; empire scales it outward.

The governing question is not merely who has power, but whether outward instrument is still answerable to civilizational continuity. When empire outruns civilization, the machinery of reach begins to degrade the inheritance it claims to protect.

### Faith and science

Faith and science are coequal truth-orders.

They are not reducible to religion versus laboratories. Both can authorize, restrain, justify, or deform political order. In some cases they align. In others they compete. In many modern cases they hybridize: constitutional creed, technocratic expertise, revolutionary doctrine, procedural legality, and civil religion all sit on this axis.

### Memory and desire

Memory preserves continuity; desire pushes for release, compensation, novelty, prestige, or revenge.

This is where many objects sharpen. If the civilizational and instrumental read is clear but the behavior still feels excessive, theatrical, or compulsive, the missing mechanism is often desire acting on a memory-bearing object.

## Main failure modes

- **civilization without empire** - inheritance remains symbolically real but lacks sufficient carrier, reach, or instrument to defend itself
- **empire outrunning civilization** - instrument expands faster than legitimacy can bear, so control degrades the inheritance
- **faith without science** - sacred or moral truth-order loses procedural competence, verification discipline, or governability
- **science without faith** - procedural or expert order loses moral depth, sacred boundary, or meaningful limit
- **memory without renewal** - continuity becomes grievance theater, paralysis, or sacredized wound
- **desire severed from inheritance** - acceleration, prestige, or appetite float free of continuity and become destabilizing overreach

## Operational use

Use this doctrine when:

- a live object is bigger than one lane's normal grammar
- legitimacy and procedure must be separated rather than collapsed
- continuity and appetite are both clearly active
- the operator needs a higher-order read before lane translation or clause drafting

Do not use it as a substitute for source objects or transaction design. Its job is to identify the governing layer and return you to the right retrieval surface.

## Bottom line

The active CIV-STATE question is no longer:

> Is this mainly about power, truth, or time?

It is now:

> What civilizational inheritance is at stake, what empire instrument is carrying it, what faith-order and science-order authorize it, what memory sustains it, and what desire distorts or accelerates it?
