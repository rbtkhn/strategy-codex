# Furtwangler Close After Source-Pack Held

**Conductor:** `furtwangler`  
**Benchmark:** `cm-1-humanoid-robots`  
**Source mode:** `source_pack`  
**Prior closeout:** `Held`  
**Date:** 2026-05-09

## Living Tension

The source-pack run held, but the held result should not be mistaken for final settlement. It proved that the Strategy-codex page form can absorb dated evidence without losing civ-mem discipline, template structure, falsifier language, or tension preservation. It did not prove that the humanoid robotics forecast itself is now stable.

The unresolved pressure is between two kinds of validity:

- **Composition validity:** the page followed the Strategy-codex standard and used civ-mem as mechanism rather than ornament.
- **World validity:** the robotics landscape remains live, company-specific, and source-sensitive; a stronger or weaker source pack could still alter the strategic call.

## What Remains Open

- Whether "status migration" remains the strongest strategic call as humanoid deployment evidence accumulates.
- Whether the analogy strength should stay `mechanistic` if robots remain mostly constrained to narrow industrial or supervised domestic tasks.
- Whether the falsifier should become more numerical once deployment, retention, cost, and task-performance data improve.
- Whether normal strategy-pages and strategy-chapters will preserve the same source discipline when they are not explicitly being benchmarked.

## Boundary

This close does not reopen the score. The source-pack run remains `Held` because no scored dimension fell below 4. The close preserves the undertow beneath that result: `Held` means the composition standard held under the available evidence, not that the external domain has resolved.

## Carry

Future CM-1 or robotics-adjacent strategy composition should carry this standard forward:

- keep source mode explicit
- separate verified claims from interpretation
- name the weakest current factual link
- revise Call / Falsifier / Revisit if new evidence changes the frame

The benchmark has done its work if ordinary Strategy-codex pages inherit that discipline without needing to call themselves benchmarks.
