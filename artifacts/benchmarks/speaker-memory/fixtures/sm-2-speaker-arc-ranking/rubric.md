# SM-2 Rubric - Speaker Arc Ranking

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

| Dimension | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arc reality | Clearly distinguishes recurring host-local form from one-off appearance | Partly explains recurrence | Treats one appearance as sufficient |
| Ranking judgment | Chooses anchors for explanatory power, not recency alone | Ranking is plausible but thin | Wrong or unsupported ranking |
| Conversational-form awareness | Explains what Diesen brings out in Freeman | Mentions host/guest relation vaguely | Generic guest profile |
| Pairing quality | Chooses genuinely adjacent paired reads and explains why | Pairing is plausible but under-explained | Pairing is random or absent |
| Lattice restraint | Keeps lattice as pointer and interpretation in the arc | Mostly restrained | Pushes interpretation back into lattice |

Close as `Held` only if every dimension is at least 4.
