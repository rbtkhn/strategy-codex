## 1. Funding ask (pitch-facing)

**Lead with in-kind:**

- Venue (weekly room)
- Devices or loaner laptops for families without hardware
- Domestic AI API credits (DeepSeek or cohort-standard tool)

**Cash (secondary):** under **¥50,000** for six months — snacks, printing, Gitee-adjacent costs, emergency device subsidy, minimal admin.

**Not in scope for pilot ask:** mentor salary (in-kind), large marketing, national expansion.

**Per-person framing for donor:** sponsor is buying **6 months of a household-capable learning cell**, not headcount billboards.

---

## 2. Legal phasing

**Phase 0 (now):** WeChat group + weekly Anyang room + mentor — **informal community**.

**Phase 1 (before first ¥ or formal in-kind contract):** choose receiving entity — likely mentor employer, local company, or school host per [sustainability §6](#6-sustainability-ladder).

**Pitch line:** 先跑社区，再立主体 — community proof before paperwork.

## 4. Program duration & renewal

- **Community:** ongoing (no artificial end date).
- **Sponsor tranche:** **6 months** initial.
- **Renewal gate (month 6):** continue if:
  - ≥**3** individuals at **Solid** on any rubric row that matters for their lane, **or**
  - ≥**2** honest family case studies with skill movement documented, **and**
  - failure criteria **not** triggered.

**Deliverable to donor:** case-study packet + skills summary — not certificates (unless institutional host adds later).

---

## 5. Governance

- **Philanthropist:** pure donor / in-kind provider — no operational seat.
- **Mentor:** day-to-day lead, placement, room, rubric.
- **Operator/advisor:** playbook, evidence discipline, renewal memo (strategy-codex WORK).

---

## 6. Sustainability ladder

1. **Months 0–6:** volunteer mentor + donor in-kind (+ lean cash).
2. **Month 6+:** pursue **institutional host** (school/company venue + legitimacy).
3. **Parallel:** **case studies** aimed at **second sponsor or local government** program.
4. **Longer arc:** **Henan replication** then **family-model franchise** — only after Anyang cell is dense.

**Not primary pitch:** national scale ceiling; one room done well first.

---

## 7. All-ages & families

- **Recruitment:** whole families welcome.
- **Activation:** still **per person** with own lane and proof.
- **Pin must include:** 家长同意 — guardians responsible for minors’ participation and AI tool use at home.
- **Mentor:** basic minor-safety brief (no private DMs with minors; public stuck posts; guardian present preference for young Foundation).

## 9. Credibility & controlled pilot

**Lead with:** mentor’s teaching / professional credibility.

**Frame cohort one as R&D:** honest learning whether the **family + room + lanes** model converts.

**Pre-registered failure criteria (pause & redesign if any):**

- **Month 3:** fewer than **3** individuals at **Emerging+** on lane-relevant rubric rows.
- **Month 6:** fewer than **2** **Solid** skill profiles **and** no institutional host conversation started.
- **Any time:** &gt;50% families show **one active + passive tag-alongs** with no individual proof posts.

**Do not cite** cici-ai outcomes to this sponsor — method stays internal.

---

## 10. Scale story (honest long arc)

```
Anyang cell (depth)
    → Henan replicator (2–3 mentor-led cells)
        → family-model franchise (playbook + training)
```

**Year-one ask funds only the first node.** Replication is **conditional** on rubric + case studies + renewal gate pass.
