## One line

**anyang-ai** is a **family-based AI learning cell in Anyang**: weekly in-person sessions, WeChat continuity, China-first tools (Gitee + domestic AI) — building **verifiable habits**, not lectures, not a foreign-tool stack, **no income promises**.

---

## Problem

- Families are curious about AI but stall on terminals, accounts, and shame.
- Async-only groups **silently churn**; this model adds **households + fixed room + SWE mentor**.
- Parents and teens can be on **different learning lanes** (Foundation / Builder) in one WeChat group, each posting their own proof.

---

## Pilot shape (first 6 months)

| Element | Plan |
|---|---|
| Cohort | ~**5–10 families** (mentor’s students + local Anyang recruits) |
| Cadence | **Weekly in-person** + WeChat between sessions |
| Stack | WeChat, Gitee, domestic AI (e.g. DeepSeek); **no** GitHub / Claude / Supabase required in phase 1 |
| Mentor | Anyang **professional software engineer** (largely in-kind; **not** in cash ask) |
| Legal | **Community first, entity later** — formal receiver only before money moves |

The **community is ongoing**; the sponsor tranche is **6 months**, with **renewal gated on skills**, not vanity headcount.

---

## What we ask (priority order)

1. **In-kind first:** venue, loaner devices, domestic AI credits — [checklist](anyang-in-kind-request-list.md)
2. **Lean cash (optional):** under **¥50,000 / 6 months** (snacks, printing, emergency device subsidy)
3. **Not asking for:** mentor salary, mass marketing, national expansion budget

---

## Success = skills, not joins

Per person: **Foundation** or **Builder** lane; rubric **Not yet → Emerging → Solid**.

**Solid** ≈ owns-account proof: goal doc + domestic AI exercise, or Gitee commit path; active **done / stuck** posts in WeChat.

**Month-6 deliverable to donor:**

- Anonymized skills summary
- **2+** honest family case studies (including setbacks)
- Renewal recommendation + whether **institutional host** or **2nd funder** is realistic

**Pause triggers:** &lt;3 Emerging+ by month 3; &lt;2 Solid + no institutional path by month 6; &gt;50% families = one active + passive tag-alongs.

Full rubric: [skills-rubric.md](anyang-ai-progress/skills-rubric.md)
