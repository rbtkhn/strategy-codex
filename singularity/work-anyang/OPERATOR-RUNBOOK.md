## Modes

### 1. Sponsor calibration & pitch

When funding, legal shape, or narrative is unsettled:

1. Read [anyang-philanthropist-calibration.md](anyang-philanthropist-calibration.md) — do not re-litigate settled choices unless operator overrides.
2. For **new sponsor conversations:** lead with **ZH one-pager** + **in-kind list**; use **EN one-pager** only for operator/mentor alignment.
3. **Lead ask:** in-kind (venue, devices, domestic AI credits) → lean cash **<¥50k / 6 months** → **not** mentor salary or national expansion.
4. **Outcomes:** skills rubric (**Not yet → Emerging → Solid**), not join-count posters.
5. **Renewal:** 6-month tranche; month-3 / month-6 failure criteria per calibration doc.

**Pitch guardrails:** no income promises; no **cici-ai outcome transfer** to this sponsor; community-first, entity before money moves.

### 2. Mentor week-zero & lanes

When placing families or planning first physical session:

1. Read [anyang-ai-lanes.md](anyang-ai-lanes.md).
2. Mentor assigns **Foundation (预备轨)** or **Builder (实操轨)** per person — default **generous** to Foundation.
3. **Foundation:** WeChat + in-room wins; **no Gitee week one**.
4. **Builder:** Gitee fork path per [import guide](anyang-ai-core/anyang-ai-gitee-template-import.md).
5. Weekly meeting ends with **visible proof** (screenshot, repo link, or named stuck state in WeChat).

**Guardrail:** mentor unblocks initiation; **does not** complete tasks for students while chat looks active.

### 3. Stack & template

When tooling or onboarding copy is in scope:

1. Default stack: **WeChat + Gitee + domestic AI** — see [China stack SSOT](anyang-ai-cn-software-stack.md).
2. **Do not** default phase-1 cohort to GitHub, Telegram, Claude, or Supabase.
3. **Bridge track** (GitHub/Claude parity) is optional advanced — not beginner path.
4. Student quickstart skeleton: [README.zh-CN.md](anyang-ai-core/docs/anyang-ai/README.zh-CN.md) after Gitee import.

### 4. Progress & renewal evidence

When reporting to donor or deciding pause/renew:

1. Tag progress by `learning_lane=foundation|builder`.
2. Compile **anonymized** skills summary + **2+** honest family case studies (include setbacks).
3. Apply pause triggers: <3 Emerging+ by month 3; <2 Solid + no institutional path by month 6; >50% families one active + passive tag-alongs.

Evidence pointers live under `singularity/work-anyang/archive/placeholders/evidence/` when created

### 5. Replicate (Henan / next cell)

When operator asks to copy the model beyond Anyang:

1. **Depth before breadth** — one cell proven first.
2. Clone **playbook structure** (mission, lanes, stack law, rubric, pitch runtime/artifacts) — not verbatim sponsor promises.
3. Re-run **philanthropist calibration** for the new locale; do not assume Anyang legal/funding choices transfer.

## Operator procedure (typical session)

1. **Orient** — read front door + mission; confirm phase (pre-launch vs live cohort).
2. **Pick mode** — pitch | week-zero | stack | progress | replicate.
3. **Draft or update** WORK files under `singularity/work-anyang/` only — no Record merges.
4. **Cross-check** EN/ZH pitch facts against calibration SSOT before mentor or sponsor handoff.
5. **Close** with explicit next live-owner action (mentor: Gitee import, WeChat pin, room booking, etc.).

## Guardrails

- **WORK only** — not SELF, EVIDENCE, or member gate.
- **Families / all ages** — minors require **家长同意** in WeChat pin; mentor minor-safety brief.
- **Pure donor** — sponsor does not get board seat or day-to-day ops.
- **Do not** cite strategy-codex, companion-self, or Telegram in sponsor-facing ZH copy.
- **Scholarship / employment / equity** language — operator review; no legal commitments in this lane.
