## Why Anyang over the cici-ai shape (Philippines-concentrated)

`anyang-ai` replicates the **cici-ai concept** (cohort activation surface: chat for motion, repos for durability, advisor WORK for evidence) but changes the **execution geometry**. The advantages are structural, not merely demographic.

### 1. Embedded professional mentor + warm recruitment

| cici-ai (Philippines mesh) | anyang-ai (Anyang cell) |
|---|---|
| Hobbyist Telegram mesh; founder + sparse advisor check-ins | **Professional SWE in Anyang** as teacher/mentor |
| Cold or semi-cold invites | **Current students** as wave-one pipeline |
| Async troubleshooting; high silent-stall risk | Mentor can **unblock initiation live** in room or chat |

**What this fixes:** cold-start credibility and the **first hard mile** of setup (fork, secrets, first session).

**Guardrail:** mentor must not become the hidden operator — same rule as cici-ai: **one artifact per member**, no completing tasks for students while chat looks active.

### 2. Weekly physical meetings

| cici-ai | anyang-ai |
|---|---|
| Remote-first (Telegram / Neural Sandbox) | **Weekly in-person** collaboration in Anyang |
| Accountability via async checkpoints | **Synchronous lab sessions** — pair setup, screenshot debugging, shame-reduction by presence |
| "Joined" can mask never starting | Room time tied to **artifact block** every meeting |

**What this fixes:** drop-off from **silent failure** and **long path to first win** — the primary risks named in the cici-ai mission.

**Guardrail:** attendance ≠ success; each meeting ends with **visible proof** (repo link, screenshot, or named stuck state posted to WeChat).

### 3. Population scale — phase-2 lever, not phase-1 assumption

China's population is a **larger recruitment ceiling** once the local model works. Early cohort **serviceable market** is narrower and stronger:

- mentor's current students
- Anyang locals who can attend **weekly**
- word-of-mouth inside that cell

**Disproportion:** for launch, **mentor + weekly room** outweigh **national scale**. Prove conversion in Anyang before optimizing for breadth.

### What cici-ai still leads on (inherit, don't reinvent)

- Documented three-lane arc (social / core / progress)
- Evidence patterns and templates (**adapt vendors** — see [China stack](anyang-ai-cn-software-stack.md))
- Daily brief control-plane pilot and dashboard shapes (WeChat + Gitee proof layer)
- Months of operator evidence and conductor close receipts

**Strategic read:** **anyang-ai is better positioned for initiation under supervision**; **cici-ai is better positioned for async international mesh** once the golden path is boring.

**Falsifier:** if weekly meetings + a professional mentor still produce mostly joins and chat **without fork proof**, the blocker is path/safety/copy — not population.

## Project role of `anyang-ai`

`anyang-ai` is the **local social + physical front door** for an OB1 / companion-self learning cohort in Anyang.

## Mission statement

`anyang-ai` helps AI-curious builders **learn by doing** on the lane that matches their readiness: **[Foundation](anyang-ai-foundation/README.md)** (WeChat + in-room wins before git) or **[Builder](anyang-ai-builder/README.md)** ([China-first stack](anyang-ai-cn-software-stack.md): Gitee + domestic AI + git-local habits). Members ask for help without shame, share visible progress in WeChat and in weekly meetings, and graduate Foundation → Builder when ready. The cohort turns private curiosity into **durable practice** — safe setup, real artifacts, repeatable habits, and peer support — while keeping every person's Record, repo, and infrastructure under **their own control**.

Its job in the work-anyang project is to convert interest into **evidence-linked activation**:

- gather a small team around the mentor's students and local recruits
- help members complete **initiation** with mentor supervision, not mentor substitution
- normalize questions, stuck states, and screenshot-based troubleshooting in the group
- use **weekly physical sessions** as proof sessions, not social-only meetups
- produce advisor signals: join, goal stated, workspace opened, first task, return within seven days, artifact, helper behavior

What `anyang-ai` is **not**:

- not any member's Record and not a place to merge identity truth
- not a passive announcement channel where joins alone count as success
- not a substitute for each member's own instance repo and gate
- not a paid program in this lane unless explicitly redesigned with separate governance

## Family recruitment

**Intent:** recruit **household units** (parent + teen, siblings, spouse pairs, grandparent + student) while counting **activation per person**, not per family join.

### Why families fit Anyang better than cici-ai’s solo mesh

| Benefit | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Warm trust** | Family invite lowers cold-start fear vs stranger Telegram groups |
| **Home accountability** | “Stuck on X” can surface at dinner; less 48h silence |
| **Lane pairing inside home** | One **Builder** (e.g. parent SWE, older sibling) can coach **Foundation** members — credited as *family buddy help*, not mentor ghost-work |
| **Weekly meeting logistics** | Carpool, shared calendar, parents see room is real — reduces flake |
| **Anyang word-of-mouth** | Extended family / neighbor networks spread faster than single-user referrals |
| **Cross-generation literacy** | Parent on Foundation, teen on Builder — same WeChat group, different bars; household AI norm |
| **Device & account help** | Legitimate home support for Gitee signup, IDE install — still must end in **member’s own proof post** |

**Disproportion:** family recruitment is heavier for **retention and initiation** than for **national scale**. The unit of trust is the household; the unit of **proof** remains the individual.

### Operating rules (non-negotiable)

1. **One WeChat group** `anyang-ai` is fine; **every person** posts their own intro, done/stuck, and repo URL (no proxy posting by parent).
2. **Placement per person** — parent Builder + child Foundation is normal; do not assume same lane in one family.
3. **One repo per activated Builder** — family members do not share one Gitee fork as “the family project” for initiation credit.
4. **Buddy help is bounded** — a family Builder may sit beside a Foundation member, but initiation counts only when the **Foundation member** posts their own artifact.
5. **Offer-sensitive language** (scholarship, jobs, equity, **创业收入**) — path language only; **no income guarantees** in pin or pitch.
6. **Progress sheet** — optional `family_id` links household for analytics; metrics still count **activated individuals**.
7. **家长同意** — pin states guardian consent for minors; see [philanthropist calibration §7](anyang-philanthropist-calibration.md#7-all-ages--families).

### Falsifier

Family recruitment **fails** if households produce **one active person + passive tags-along joins** — track `activated_count / family_join_count` in [progress](anyang-ai-progress/README.md).

### Near-term family tactics

- Mentor invites **students’ households** first (wave one).
- Pin explains: **欢迎全家加入；每人有自己的目标与进度（欢迎家庭一起来，每人单独打卡）**.
- Monthly optional **10-minute family check-in** round at end of one weekly session — each person one sentence: done / stuck.

## Primary risk: member drop-off (same family as cici-ai)

Drop-off remains the main failure mode. Anyang mitigations are **stronger** but not automatic:

| Risk | Anyang mitigation |
|---|---|
| Fuzzy "done" | Mentor defines **one shouted milestone** in pinned WeChat + first meeting |
| Silent failure | Weekly room + "reply **done** or **stuck on X**" in WeChat within 48h |
| Mentor does the work | **No private fixes without a public stuck post**; progress lane tracks mentor-assisted vs self-initiated |
| Meeting ≠ progress | Fixed **artifact block** (45m) — **split tables** for Foundation vs Builder |
| Foundation forced onto Gitee | [Placement](../anyang-ai-lanes.md#placement-mentor-week-zero); git only after graduation |

### Funnel metrics (honest)

| Stage | Example signal |
|---|---|
| Joined group | In WeChat `anyang-ai` |
| Attended first physical session | Mentor roll + one ask posted same day |
| Initiation **done** | Foundation bar **or** Builder bar per [lanes](anyang-ai-lanes.md) |
| Graduation | Foundation → Builder sign-off recorded in [progress](anyang-ai-progress/README.md) |
| **7-day active** | Check-in or artifact in rolling week after initiation |
| First working session | Minimal bar defined with mentor (e.g. agent opens repo) |

Measure **where the stairs get steep**, not vanity joins or headcount alone.
