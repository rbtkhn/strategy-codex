# Singularity work membrane

`singularity` uses the shared [work membrane v2](../docs/work-membrane-v2.md) as a bounded architectural doctrine and design system.

The lane question is not primarily "what is the object?" It is "what is the system, what invariant is under pressure, and what bounded design surface should exist next?"

---

## Key rule

`singularity work manufactures durable bounded architectural doctrine, not Record truth`

That means `singularity` may:

- build architecture notes and protocol doctrine
- define design briefs and implementation roads
- compare substrate, alignment, agency, and control-plane patterns
- generate workshop surfaces and experiment briefs
- export complement and interop patterns for outside runtimes

It may not silently convert any of that into identity-bearing Record truth.

### Harness function

The **singularity** channel turns AI-system observations into bounded **architectural doctrine**. Default ceiling: workshop sheet → verified note or essay. See [intelligence-harness.md](../docs/intelligence-harness.md) and [agent-control-plane essay](../essays/agent-control-plane.md).

---

## Dominant membrane classes

`singularity` primarily uses:

- `instrumental work`
- `governed adjacent`
- `external complements`

`runtime / derived` still matters, but the lane has a stronger transport and interop edge than `statecraft`.

### RSI / high-velocity intake routing

- Frontier-lab RSI discourse, podcast bursts, and governance-option material default to **`instrumental work`** (workshop sheets) until **Verify receipt** exists on the sheet.
- Promotion to **`governed adjacent`** (`singularity/notes/`, essays) requires explicit verify lines or primary cites for load-bearing external claims — not agent fluency alone.
- [`record_frozen`](../../config/strategy_codex.yaml) is **interpretive pause** on the identity channel: archive and synthesis may accelerate; Record truth does not. See [review-as-bottleneck-and-interpretive-pause.md](notes/review-as-bottleneck-and-interpretive-pause.md).
- Intake protocol: [source-archive/singularity/README.md](../source-archive/singularity/README.md#intake-triad-operator-protocol) (archive → sheet → verify before promote).

## Temperament

`singularity` should read as:

- architecture-first
- concept-pressure heavy
- invariant-sensitive
- ontology-aware
- prototype-vs-doctrine explicit
- lower-throughput, higher-judgment

If a `singularity` surface drifts into generic AI news logging or loses its boundary between prototype and doctrine, it has left its native grammar.

---

## Main artifact classes

The main `singularity` artifact classes are:

- architecture notes
- runtime doctrine
- protocol specs
- design briefs
- experiment briefs
- interface contracts
- implementation roadmaps
- academy and workshop design surfaces
- complement and interop patterns

Read them by membrane class:

- architecture notes, protocol specs, and stable design doctrine are usually `governed adjacent`
- workshop drafts, pilots, and active design passes are `instrumental work`
- import/export and interop bundles often live under `external complements`

---

## Route grammar inside singularity

Typical route shapes:

- `human-authored note -> design brief -> protocol spec`
- `runtime observation -> experiment brief -> doctrine update`
- `doctrine draft -> governed adjacent architecture note`
- `workshop design surface -> implementation roadmap`
- `interop pattern -> external complement bundle`

The lane stays healthy when it names the system boundary, authority location, and rollback path instead of treating all acceleration writing as one kind of note.

---

## Boundary reminders

- `singularity/notes/*.md` and `singularity/essays/*.md` are durable governed-adjacent outputs, not Record.
- workshop operating sheets are usually `instrumental work` unless explicitly stabilized into doctrine.
- external runtime patterns may be first-class here without gaining merge authority over Record.
- design doctrine may become durable without pretending to be settled identity truth.

---

## What singularity should not become

Avoid turning `singularity` into:

- a generic product-news shelf
- a shadow Record
- a raw benchmark dump with no architectural question
- a statecraft archive lane with different branding

Its proper office is bounded architectural doctrine and design under acceleration.
