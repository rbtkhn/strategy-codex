# Rights and disclosure — commercial safety

WORK only; not Record.

---

## Scope

Predictive History education may publish on YouTube, Substack, podcast feeds, and future course bundles. Every media asset must pass **commercial safety** before distribution.

---

## Checklist (per lesson media pack)

| Item | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Source text | Cite lecture/chapter; corpus edits stay in canonical clone |
| AI-generated visuals | Label reconstructions; no fake documents or maps |
| Likeness | No real-person likeness without clear rights |
| Trademarks / logos | Avoid unless fair-use documented |
| Music / SFX | License or royalty-free; keep receipt |
| Stock footage | License tier matches commercial use |
| Platform AI disclosure | Follow YouTube, Substack, Etsy-equivalent rules as applicable |

---

## Disclosure templates (adapt per platform)

**Video description (example):**

> Visual illustrations and narration assistance may include AI-generated elements. Historical claims are grounded in cited lecture sources; reconstructions are labeled.

**Substack footer (example):**

> Worksheets and quizzes are human-reviewed. Media may include AI-assisted visuals under operator review.

---

## Escalation

- **Hold** publish if rights unclear
- **Reject** asset in media-quality-gate; log in `media-review/`
- **Revise** lesson script if factual claim cannot be sourced
