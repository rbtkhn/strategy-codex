## Agency Under Acceleration

- **Agent:** Frontier labs, hyperscalers, SpaceX/Colossus, enterprise customers, recursive agent harnesses, capital allocators, and public narrative systems.
- **Objective:** Convert scarce compute into high-value tokens, agent labor, infrastructure leverage, market returns, and cultural legitimacy.
- **Authority:** Split across model labs, data-center owners, investors, governments, enterprises, and consumers adopting agent interfaces.
- **Observability:** Uneven. Revenue, compute deals, product launches, and benchmarks are visible; actual capacity, margins, safety behavior, and model-roadmap constraints are less visible.
- **Rollback:** Weak. Once compute contracts, enterprise workflows, agent habits, and market expectations harden, reversal becomes institutionally and economically costly.

## Control-Plane Case Candidate

**Case:** Anthropic using SpaceX/Colossus compute.

- **Agent:** Anthropic enterprise-token engine running on external hyperscaler compute.
- **Objective:** Increase Claude capacity and rate limits for high-value enterprise and coding use.
- **Authority:** Anthropic controls model/service behavior; SpaceX/AI controls physical compute substrate; enterprise customers create demand pressure.
- **Observability:** Public claims reveal deal shape and immediate rate-limit effects, but not full contract, margins, safety obligations, or scheduling control.
- **Rollback:** Weak if customer workflows and Anthropic capacity planning come to depend on external compute access.
