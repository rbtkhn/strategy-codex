## Agency Under Acceleration

- **Agent:** Frontier labs, model orchestrators, foundations, infrastructure owners, robotics ecosystems, governments, and end users delegating more and more loops.
- **Objective:** Convert model intelligence into durable position through orchestration, distribution, substrate control, labor compression, and eventual embodied deployment.
- **Authority:** Split across labs, cloud and chip providers, philanthropic or nonprofit governance structures, and whichever operators own the interface layer for coordinating swarms.
- **Observability:** Benchmark scores, product releases, and public timelines are visible; actual recursive leverage, hidden orchestration burden, labor substitution pace, and governance quality are less visible.
- **Rollback:** Weak. Once organizations restructure work around agent swarms, philanthropy-backed distribution regimes, or robotics ecosystems, reversal becomes economically and institutionally expensive.

## Control-Plane Case Candidates

### Case: Context-inheriting coding swarms

- **Agent:** A parent coding model that can self-fork into many child agents with inherited context.
- **Objective:** Parallelize software work across a large search space while preserving final integration quality.
- **Authority:** The operator sets goals, but the model increasingly manages decomposition, execution, and reintegration.
- **Observability:** Better than black-box autonomy, but still weak once hundreds of parallel threads are running and recombining.
- **Rollback:** Weak if organizations optimize around swarm throughput before they build receipts and auditability.

### Case: AI transition funding as legitimacy infrastructure

- **Agent:** Large AI-aligned foundation capital framed around social calm, worker ownership, and public wealth.
- **Objective:** Pre-empt social disorder by building redistribution or ownership mechanisms around AI gains.
- **Authority:** Foundation governance, political institutions, and whichever actor defines the distribution logic.
- **Observability:** Public narratives and grant structures may be visible; the actual political economy effects are slower and easier to overstate.
- **Rollback:** Mixed. These systems can be redesigned, but once expectations of dividends or ownership claims harden, they become part of the legitimacy bargain.
