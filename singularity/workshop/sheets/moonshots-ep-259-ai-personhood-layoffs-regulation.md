## Agency Under Acceleration

- **Agent:** Frontier labs, cloud and model companies, religious institutions, regulators, benchmark-setters, and users whose workflows are being compressed by coding and reasoning systems.
- **Objective:** Shape the legitimacy layer before the capability layer becomes culturally irreversible.
- **Authority:** Split across labs, governments, religious authorities, infrastructure owners, and whichever interface becomes the public default for cognition and work.
- **Observability:** Public arguments, benchmark scores, and official documents are visible; the deeper steering effects on belief, professional identity, and institutional trust are less visible.
- **Rollback:** Weak. Once AI becomes the expected coding, writing, reasoning, and persuasion layer, reversal is not just technical but social and spiritual.

## Control-Plane Case Candidates

### Case: Human purpose after coding abundance

- **Agent:** Frontier coding model embedded into daily software work.
- **Objective:** Compress implementation, debugging, and problem-solving time.
- **Authority:** Model lab plus the organization that decides where the model is allowed to act, review, or auto-complete.
- **Observability:** Benchmark wins are visible; real supervision burden, hidden failure modes, and junior-skill erosion are less visible.
- **Rollback:** Weak once organizations restructure staffing and expectations around machine-first code generation.

### Case: Legitimacy institutions entering AI governance

- **Agent:** Religious and civic authorities reacting to AI as a human-order problem.
- **Objective:** Preserve dignity, prevent concentration, and shape acceptable uses of intelligence systems.
- **Authority:** Soft but large-scale. These bodies often lack direct model control but can shape public legitimacy, law, and social permission.
- **Observability:** Public statements and encyclicals are highly visible; their causal effect on product strategy and regulation is slower and harder to measure.
- **Rollback:** Mixed. Such institutions can slow adoption stories, but they struggle to reverse infrastructure once a technical stack becomes default.
