## Agency Under Acceleration

- **Agent:** Frontier model lab, hyperscaler, chip company, persistent assistant, shopping/search intermediary, scientific simulation stack, or trust/provenance protocol.
- **Objective:** Win the stack: compute, interface, distribution, enterprise adoption, and trust while compressing human task overhead.
- **Authority:** Split across model providers, cloud vendors, chip builders, distribution owners, and whichever interface becomes the default daily operating surface.
- **Observability:** Product launches, benchmarks, IPOs, and adoption numbers are visible; actual recursive leverage, hidden coordination costs, and long-term trust effects are less visible.
- **Rollback:** Weak once these systems become defaults. Search, shopping, note-taking, code, and memory layers are sticky once users offload habits into them.

## Boundary Warnings

- **Transcript basis only.** This is a source-bound workshop note built from the pasted transcript, not a full source-verification pass.
- **Distribution is not the same as frontier capability.** Several products are discussed as strategically powerful while still being technically mid.
- **Brand sprawl is itself a signal.** Product fragmentation may indicate organizational incoherence even when capability is rising.

- **Ambient interface cuts both ways.** A persistent assistant can reduce friction and also erode presence, attention, and deliberate choice.

## Control-Plane Case Candidates

### Case: Trust as infrastructure

- **Agent:** Provenance/watermarking stack such as SynthID.
- **Objective:** Re-establish authenticity and chain-of-custody in synthetic-content conditions.
- **Authority:** Protocol owner, participating model vendors, camera/device ecosystem, and downstream platforms.
- **Observability:** Better than many other fronts because signatures and credentials are inspectable when present.
- **Rollback:** Weak once synthetic media saturates the environment; provenance becomes a baseline requirement rather than an optional add-on.

### Case: Interface as operating layer

- **Agent:** Persistent assistant spanning search, inbox, shopping, and daily brief.
- **Objective:** Capture intent before it becomes an explicit task.
- **Authority:** The company that owns the default interface and app integrations.
- **Observability:** The user sees outputs and convenience, but not always the ranking, steering, or commercial logic behind them.
- **Rollback:** Weak once the assistant mediates enough routine intent that manual reversion becomes costly.
