# Mapping Functions

NST defines relational transformations between:

## Epistemic Geometry (EG)

- agents
- claims
- event alignment
- divergence structure

## Predictive History (PH)

- temporal evolution of narratives
- retrospective interpretation
- predictive reconstruction

## NST Mapping

NST: EG × PH → R

Where R encodes:

- structural-temporal coupling
- alignment constraints
- transformation relationships
