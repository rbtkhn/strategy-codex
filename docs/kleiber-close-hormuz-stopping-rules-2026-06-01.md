The `Hormuz` tranche taught the right small lesson:

```text
governed archive repair needs stopping rules as much as it needs detection rules
```

The gain was not only that the phrase family was fixed.

The gain was that the family was allowed to end honestly.

What made that possible:

- one bounded audit family
- one bounded direct repair family
- one explicit reviewed exception
- one post-repair artifact proving the remainder was zero

That is better than two common failure modes:

- continuing to widen the pass because adjacent noise is visible
- stopping early with one vague unresolved remainder that keeps reopening the seam

The `Hormuz` example now sets the right shape:

```text
detect clearly
repair narrowly
name the remainder honestly
stop when the family is proved closed
```

That is what a governed interpretive machine should do when repairing its own
substrate.

It should not confuse discipline with endlessness.

```text
the repair pass is finished when the remainder becomes explicit, bounded, and no longer falsely live
```
