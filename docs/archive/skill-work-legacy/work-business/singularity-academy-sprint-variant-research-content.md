# AI Operating System Sprint - Research / Content Team Variant

non-authoritative. Tailored offer variant.

## Best fit

- small research teams
- content teams
- media or analysis operators
- transcript-heavy or draft-heavy workflows

## Core pain

The team is generating more output than it can trust, and someone senior is silently repairing the quality.

Typical symptoms:

- AI accelerates drafting but not review confidence
- outputs vary in quality
- status and review standards are informal
- a senior editor, researcher, or founder is still doing hidden cleanup

## Offer angle

Install one review-gated workflow so AI-assisted output stops depending on invisible rescue and becomes easier to inspect, route, and reuse.

## Best workflow types

- research summary and review handoff
- transcript or source-quality review
- editorial draft and approval loop
- operator-facing digest with confidence boundaries

## What changes

By the end of the sprint, the team should have:

- one clearer review standard
- one better receipt for draft quality and approval
- one visible boundary between AI assistance and final human judgment
- one workflow that can be rerun without re-arguing the rules

## Best package

- default: `$2,500` base sprint
- upgrade if multiple reviewers or a second workflow need to be measured

## Good lead sentence

`AI may already be making the team faster, but it is still leaving someone senior to quietly do the trust work.`

## Bad lead sentence

`This will automate your content operation end to end.`
