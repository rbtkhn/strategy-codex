## Selection rule

Each target on this page publicly shows at least one of the following:

- small-team AI publishing or workflow operations
- cohort-based AI education or applied training
- explicit review, feedback, governance, or operating-layer language

## Why these targets fit the offer

### Founder-operator / research-content pattern

These groups are likely to feel:

- review blur
- too many drafts
- hidden trust work
- recurring brief or digest pressure

### Cohort operator pattern

These groups are likely to feel:

- setup versus proof confusion
- rescue-heavy instruction loops
- inconsistent artifact quality
- need for a better repeatable operating surface
