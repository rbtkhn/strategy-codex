# AI Operating System Sprint - Founder-Operator Variant

non-authoritative. Tailored offer variant.

## Best fit

- founder-operators
- very small teams
- operators already using AI daily across planning, reporting, writing, or customer communication

## Core pain

The founder is still doing the hard part invisibly.

Typical symptoms:

- too many drafts
- noisy status updates
- unclear trust in AI-assisted work
- no stable weekly or daily operating surface

## Offer angle

Install one bounded AI workflow that makes reporting, review, and next-step routing easier to trust and easier to reuse.

## Best workflow types

- weekly operator brief
- research summary and decision note
- internal content or planning handoff
- client communication review loop

## What changes

By the end of the sprint, the founder should have:

- one clearer reporting or review workflow
- less invisible synthesis work
- one receipt shape for what AI did and what still needs the founder
- one repeatable operating surface instead of a pile of drafts

## Best package

- default: `$2,500` base sprint
- upgrade to `$5,000` only if there is a second workflow or explicit before/after readout need

## Good lead sentence

`You already have AI activity in the workflow, but the trusted reporting layer still depends on you doing the hard part invisibly.`

## Bad lead sentence

`I want to talk about the singularity and the future of work.`
