# Strategy-Codex Quality Contract Offer

WORK-only offer sketch. Not Record truth.

## One-line Offer

Install a quality-state layer for transcript-heavy research systems so every ingestion pass reports structural gain, transcript purity, unresolved routing risk, and git durability in one receipt.

## Buyer

Independent researchers, media analysts, litigation researchers, intelligence teams, and content operators who ingest large transcript/source corpora and need to know not just what exists, but how clean and usable it is.

## Pain

Most research repositories make structural progress visible before quality progress is visible. Operators can see that files, folders, and routing surfaces exist, but they cannot easily tell whether the underlying text is transcript-grade, cleaned, noisy, summary-grade, legacy-only, unresolved, committed, or pushed.

## Deliverable

A script-backed quality contract with:

- Per-host or per-source quality receipts.
- Counts for routeable artifacts, word mass, transcript-grade material, cleaned transcripts, noisy transcript-bearing material, summary-grade material, and legacy-only material.
- Deltas versus the prior run.
- Unresolved speaker or routing-risk count.
- Scoped git durability line: on disk, verified, staged or not staged, committed or not committed, pushed or not pushed.
- Mandatory closeout language that separates topological progress from text-quality progress.

## Entry Package

**Quality Contract Audit**: 1 corpus, 1 source family, 1 month.

Outputs:

- Source inventory.
- Purity ranking.
- Raw-input gap list.
- Naming and routing warnings.
- One repeatable script or command recipe.
- One operator-facing receipt template.

## Why It Is Distinct

This is not a generic data cleanup pass. The product is continuous epistemic observability: the operator leaves with a quality dashboard primitive that can be rerun after each densification pass.

## Proof Point From Strategy-Codex

The current Strategy-Codex host-shelf quality contract reduced the UX gap between structural completion and transcript-purity completion. A later Coffee C source-hygiene pass found five apparent raw-input gaps, repaired two metadata visibility problems, and reduced the real unresolved set to three.
