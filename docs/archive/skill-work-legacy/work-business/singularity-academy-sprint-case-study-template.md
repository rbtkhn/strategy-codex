# Singularity-Academy Sprint Case Study Template

non-authoritative. Reusable case-study scaffold.

## Purpose

Turn one completed sprint into a credible proof artifact for future sales without inflating the result.

## Template

```text
# AI Operating System Sprint - Case Study

## Client type

- {founder-operator | cohort operator | research/content team | service business}

## Workflow in scope

- {name the workflow}

## Starting problem

- {what was breaking before the sprint}

## What we installed

- {workflow map}
- {brief/reporting layer}
- {review/receipt layer}
- {handoff or task ladder}

## What changed

- {specific improvement #1}
- {specific improvement #2}

## Before/after scorecard

- workflow clarity: {before} -> {after}
- proof clarity: {before} -> {after}
- review visibility: {before} -> {after}
- authority clarity: {before} -> {after}
- reuse: {before} -> {after}
- re-entry: {before} -> {after}

## Evidence

- {artifact or receipt #1}
- {artifact or receipt #2}
- {artifact or receipt #3}

## What remained hard

- {what still needed rescue or stayed weak}

## Why this matters

- {one sentence on why this is commercially or operationally meaningful}
```

## Writing rule

Keep the case study narrower than your temptation.

It is better to prove:

- one workflow became clearer

than to imply:

- the whole team transformed
