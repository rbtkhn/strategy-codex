# Why these four form a cognition watchset

The daily `cognition streams` skill does not try to represent the whole cognition lattice.

It tracks four streams because together they form a practical **daily watchset**:

- [Diesen](../../../codex/COGNITION-STREAMS.md) keeps long-horizon structural and order-level argument in view.
- [Davis](../../../codex/COGNITION-STREAMS.md) supplies live military feasibility, escalation, and command-pressure reading.
- [Mercouris](../../../codex/COGNITION-STREAMS.md) offers extended monologue synthesis with statecraft continuity.
- [Dialogue Works / Alkorshid](../../../codex/COGNITION-STREAMS.md) brings interview-pressure probing that makes guests disclose sharper crisis grammars.

What binds them is not identical ideology or identical format.

What binds them is that, taken together, they provide a repeatable daily spread across:

- structure
- pressure
- synthesis
- probing

That is enough difference to make the set useful, but not so much sprawl that the daily pass becomes diffuse.

So the watchset is intentionally smaller than the full cognition-stream lattice. It is a **daily operating quartet**, not a claim that these are the only important streams in the notebook.
