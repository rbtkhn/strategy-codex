# Packet crosswalk

WORK only; not Record.

**Purpose:** This is a quick chooser for source-hygiene packet work. Use it to decide what kind of packet a seam wants, what notebook move it supports, and when dual-register maturity should block early synthesis.

Pair with:

- [source-hygiene-packets.md](source-hygiene-packets.md) for packet procedure
- [packet-before-synthesis-doctrine.md](packet-before-synthesis-doctrine.md) for packet-first tempo and maturity rules

## Fast chooser

| If the seam's real value is... | Packet shape | Main notebook move | Primary danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| explaining what the conflict is structurally doing | **Mechanism** | preserve a causal model for later Judgment or page synthesis | analyst framework hardening into fact |
| clarifying how bargaining, sequencing, or naming is being done | **Process** | preserve negotiation shape without overclaiming event closure | diplomatic language mistaken for settled outcome |
| revealing red lines, legitimacy language, or political imagination | **Register** | preserve voice and signaling without laundering rhetoric into fact | rhetoric flattened into event claim |
| containing both an official-primary line and a commentator or interpreter gloss | **Dual-register maturity check** | preserve the seam as layered rather than composing it too early | two evidence layers collapsed into one "state says" line |

## Packet shape to notebook move

### Mechanism

Use when a source is most valuable because it explains:

- why escalation traps form
- how chokepoints matter
- what structural incentives are doing

Typical notebook move:

- keep the seam available for later `days.md` Judgment, strategy-page synthesis, or cross-thread comparison
- avoid treating the analyst's whole ladder as quote-grade fact

Working example:

- [pape-page-2026-04-29-youtube-pape-grim-nuclear-iran-inevitable.md](../../../codex/years/2026/pape/pape-page-2026-04-29-youtube-pape-grim-nuclear-iran-inevitable.md)

### Process

Use when a source is most valuable because it clarifies:

- what a ceasefire is being called
- who is sequencing what
- how talks are being framed or limited

Typical notebook move:

- preserve bargaining shape
- keep event-fact and process-language separate

Working example:

- [parsi-page-2026-04-21-x-ceasefire-hormuz-cluster.md](../../../codex/years/2026/parsi/parsi-page-2026-04-21-x-ceasefire-hormuz-cluster.md)

### Register

Use when a source is most valuable because it shows:

- red-line language
- legitimacy framing
- pressure vocabulary
- moral or civilizational posture

Typical notebook move:

- preserve signal and audience register
- do not promote it as neutral event-fact

Working examples:

- [marandi-page-2026-04-21-blockade-islamabad-hormuz.md](../../../codex/speakers/marandi/marandi-page-2026-04-21-blockade-islamabad-hormuz.md)

## Dual-register maturity check

This is not a fourth packet shape. It is a **maturity test** that can sit inside more than one packet type.

Use it whenever a seam contains:

- an official-primary state line
- plus a commentator, elite-English, or interpreter gloss

Required notebook move:

1. preserve the official-primary object
2. preserve the attributed gloss as a second layer
3. wait for institutional corroboration or divergence
4. only then broaden into stronger synthesis

This check matters most when a source would otherwise tempt a fast sentence like:

- "Iran says X"
- "The Vatican and Tehran aligned on Y"
- "Trump's position is Z"

before the seam has actually matured enough to carry that sentence honestly.

## Minimal selection rule

Ask in this order:

1. Is the seam's main value **mechanism**, **process**, or **register**?
2. Does it also contain a **dual-register** problem?
3. If yes, can I safely compose now, or do I need packet-first maturity discipline?

If the answer to `3` is uncertain, stop and preserve the seam in layered form first.
