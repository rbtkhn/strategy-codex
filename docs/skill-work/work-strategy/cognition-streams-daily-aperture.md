# Cognition streams as daily aperture

The canonical operator skill is now **`check streams`**. The older phrase **`cognition streams`** remains the conceptual name for the broader aperture and a legacy activation alias.

`check streams` is more than a convenience wrapper for `yt-dlp`.

It is the notebook's **daily perceptual aperture**: a bounded way of asking what the strategic world is trying to say today through a recurring set of voices.

That framing matters because the skill is not trying to ingest "all relevant video." It is trying to keep one disciplined daily opening:

- wide enough to catch live shifts
- narrow enough to remain reusable
- conservative enough not to confuse clips, fragments, and packaging with the real daily signal

The five tracked streams form a practical watch aperture because they repeatedly surface:

- long-horizon structural argument
- live military or statecraft pressure
- extended monologue synthesis
- adversarial or interview-format crisis probing
- legal-constitutional cross-examination

For the narrower question of why these five belong together as a daily set, see [cognition-streams-watchset.md](cognition-streams-watchset.md).

So the daily skill is not just a downloader. It is a way of preserving a **repeatable first look** before heavier synthesis begins.

The list-first rule is part of that philosophy. It slows the operator down just enough to distinguish:

- what actually deserves attention today
- what is merely promotional packaging
- what can wait for a later pass

For the specific judgment rule behind `main uploads` versus `suspected clips`, see [cognition-streams-attention-discipline.md](cognition-streams-attention-discipline.md).

In that sense, `check streams` sits upstream from notebook judgment much the way a perceptual habit sits upstream from thought: it shapes what becomes visible before deciding what becomes important.
