## Structural claim

World politics relevant to this lane is **polycentric** at the level of **public voice**: outcomes hinge on **which** center (Holy See, U.S. executive, Kremlin, Beijing, Tehran) is saying **what**, to **which** audience. **Mistake class:** treating those outputs as interchangeable slices of one story.

## Information logic

Each thread is a **separate information channel** with its own primary surfaces (watch docs, native pass). Merging channels into one undifferentiated summary is **lossy compression**. Prefer **tagged** bullets, **split** Judgment, or **Thesis A / B** when channels disagree.

## Working definitions (skill use)

| Term | Meaning here |
|------|----------------|
| **Equilibrium** | Political–information equilibrium: multiple authoritative centers remain in play; no single narrator subsumes others' public justification for long. |
| **Entropy** | Information mixing: **high** when wires mix attributions without plane tags; **low** when each load-bearing claim traces to a named channel. |
| **Narrative escalation** | Raising interpretive stakes (observables → moralized → theological/metaphysical) where evidence tiers no longer align. Split planes; do not collapse Thesis A into Thesis B without a seam. |
