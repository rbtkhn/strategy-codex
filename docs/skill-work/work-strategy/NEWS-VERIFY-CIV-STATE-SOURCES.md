## Source-lattice placement

This registry is **corpus tier 3** (current-events news / official) — see [statecraft corpus tiers](../../../docs/source-lattice-beyond-the-repo.md#statecraft-corpus-tiers-strategy-codex). It is also a **doorway** (routing index): it does not substitute for lane primaries.

| Corpus tier | Examples | News-verify |
|-------------|----------|-------------|
| **1** Historical primary | Thucydides, Primary Chronicle, *Federalist Papers*, treaties in named edition | **Out of scope** — [civ-state primary-text acquisition](../../../skills/civ-state-primary-text-acquisition/SKILL.md) |
| **2** Historical secondary | Durant, Gibbon, Goldsworthy, Kaldellis; CIV-MEM | **Out of scope** — mechanism support for tier 1 |
| **3** Current-events news | This registry (lanes + outlets) | **In scope** — sub-tiers **3a–3c** below |
| **4** Current-events commentary | Mercouris, Diesen, Davis, Pape benches; `source-archive/statecraft/` analyst frames | **Out of scope** for news-grade verification — fence interpretation; embedded report hooks lift to tier 3 |

**Reading order** (orthogonal to corpus tier): doorway → primary floor → secondary support → widened interpretation. For a live seam, **3a–3b** is the floor; **tier 4** is synthesis only after verify.

**Blocking rules:**

- Tier **4** cannot substantiate tier **3** facts without tier **3** receipt.
- Tier **3** cannot substantiate tier **1–2** historical claims.
- Tier **2** cannot replace tier **1** on wording disputes.
- Tier **4** may interpret tiers **1–3** once receipts exist.

## Tier law — news-verify (corpus tier 3 only)

Legacy labels **T1/T2/T3** = sub-tiers **3a/3b/3c**. **T4** in older receipts = **corpus tier 4** (commentary), not a wire sub-tier.

| Sub-tier | Legacy | Label | News-verify use |
|----------|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **3a** | T1 | Official / state primary | Satisfies **lane receipt** when dated to the seam |
| **3b** | T2 | Major wire quoting 3a, or authorized readout | Supporting; may satisfy lane only when **3a** not yet published |
| **3c** | T3 | Regional, partisan, syndicated, executive social | **Never** sole lane receipt; mesh / interpretation fence |
| — | T4 | Commentary / internal analysis | **Corpus tier 4** — out of scope for news-grade verification |

**Native language:** When the dispute is **wording**, **scope**, or **attribution**, **3a** in the lane's **native lang** (`fa`, `zh`, `ru`, `he`, `it`, `es`, `fr`, `pt` for Rome) is required before `verify:wire-supported` on that lane. English syndication = **3b supporting** unless no native line exists → `verify:wire-unclear` + escalate.
