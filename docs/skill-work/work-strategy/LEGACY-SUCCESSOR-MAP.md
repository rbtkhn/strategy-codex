## Practical Rule

- put live judgment and prose in `statecraft/` when the object is about states, civilizations, institutions, legitimacy, order, strategic mechanism, or downstream use of singularity/CIV-STATE inputs
- put chronology and continuity in `/codex`
- put domain-native acceleration or agency essays in `singularity/`

When touching **old** `work-strategy` material:

- preserve compatibility unless the migration is explicit
- prefer adding successor pointers rather than forcing broad relocation
- do not teach `work-strategy` as the active canonical owner anymore
