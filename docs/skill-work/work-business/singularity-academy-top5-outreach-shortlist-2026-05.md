# Singularity-Academy Top 5 Outreach Shortlist - 2026-05

non-authoritative. This is a near-term outreach shortlist, not qualification proof.

## Purpose

Turn the public seed list into the `5` strongest first outreach candidates for the AI Operating System Sprint.

These names are chosen for:

- visible fit with current repo proof
- reachable public contact path
- clear pain language that does not require heavy translation

## How to use this sheet

1. Move any real outreach activity into the [Singularity-Academy Sprint Prospect Pipeline](singularity-academy-sprint-prospect-pipeline.md).
2. Use the variant pages to tailor the message:
   - [Founder-Operator Variant](singularity-academy-sprint-variant-founder-operator.md)
   - [Cohort Operator Variant](singularity-academy-sprint-variant-cohort-operator.md)
   - [Research / Content Team Variant](singularity-academy-sprint-variant-research-content.md)
3. Treat this sheet as a starting wedge, not as proof of buyer intent.

## Top 5

### 1. AI Adjacent

- **Archetype:** research / content team
- **Why now:** their public architecture already describes an AI-first editorial pipeline with human final review, which is very close to the sprint's control-plane and review language
- **Workflow pain hypothesis:** agentic drafting may be ahead of review and reuse discipline
- **Likely package:** `$2,500` base sprint
- **Public contact path:** `hello@aiadjacent.com` or `tips@aiadjacent.com`
- **Best first line:** `You already have an AI-first editorial pipeline, but there may still be hidden trust work between draft generation and final review.`

### 2. deep.org

- **Archetype:** founder-operator / research-content
- **Why now:** the publication explicitly emphasizes signal over volume, clarity over hype, and sponsorship independence, which aligns well with the sprint's reporting and proof discipline
- **Workflow pain hypothesis:** a small briefing operation may still rely on invisible editorial trust work and manual quality filtering
- **Likely package:** `$2,500` base sprint
- **Public contact path:** `hello@deep.org`
- **Best first line:** `Your editorial principles already value clarity over hype, but the trusted operating layer behind that standard may still depend on invisible founder work.`

### 3. 2389 Research

- **Archetype:** founder-operator
- **Why now:** they publicly build software and publish research about AI agents and human-computer interaction, with a visible small team and public-shipping ethos
- **Workflow pain hypothesis:** research, product, and publication loops may benefit from a more explicit review and receipt layer
- **Likely package:** `$2,500` base sprint
- **Public contact path:** `hello@2389.ai`
- **Best first line:** `You already ship in public across products and research, but one operating workflow may still be carrying too much invisible founder trust work.`

### 4. Cohorte Bootcamp

- **Archetype:** cohort operator
- **Why now:** the program promises shipped systems, written records, governance runbooks, and mentor feedback, which makes it a strong fit for a sprint focused on proof, review, and reusable operating surfaces
- **Workflow pain hypothesis:** the cohort likely has a repeatable review and verification load that could be made easier to route and reuse
- **Likely package:** `$5,000` proof-loop sprint
- **Public contact path:** public bootcamp/apply route
- **Best first line:** `You already promise a shipped system plus a written operating layer, but the internal proof and review workflow may still be heavier than it needs to be.`

### 5. AIM Program

- **Archetype:** cohort operator
- **Why now:** the program is remote, synchronous, beginner-friendly, and capstone-based, which matches the repo's strongest proof cluster around setup, proof, and operator rescue
- **Workflow pain hypothesis:** beginner motion may still be easier to generate than to verify, especially around proof quality and re-entry
- **Likely package:** `$5,000` proof-loop sprint
- **Public contact path:** public application/contact form
- **Best first line:** `The program already gets people from AI-curious to capstone, but the proof and rescue layer may still be doing too much invisible work.`

## Suggested send order

1. AI Adjacent
2. deep.org
3. 2389 Research
4. Cohorte Bootcamp
5. AIM Program

Reason:

- first three are strongest fit to the current proof packet and easiest to explain without educational-program translation
- last two are strong cohort fits if the operator wants to test the higher-ticket proof-loop package early

## Boundary

- public contact path is not consent
- fit hypothesis is not confirmed pain
- this sheet should accelerate outreach, not replace qualification
