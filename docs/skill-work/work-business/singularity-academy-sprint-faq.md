## FAQ

### What is the sprint, in one sentence?

It is a `30`-day service sprint to make one recurring AI-assisted workflow easier to trust, review, and reuse.

### Who is this best for?

Small teams already using AI, where the main problem is no longer access to tools but hidden trust work, unclear review ownership, weak proof standards, or repeated operator rescue.

### What kinds of workflows fit best?

Best fits are recurring workflows such as:

- editorial or research briefing
- draft-to-review handoff
- cohort or beginner proof loops
- weekly operational reporting

### What do you actually install?

Usually:

- one workflow map
- one reporting or brief layer
- one review and receipt contract
- one authority and rollback map
- one handoff playbook

### Is this consulting or software?

It is a bounded service install first.

If the workflow becomes clearer and more stable, later software or deeper automation decisions can be made from evidence rather than theory.

### Do you build custom tools?

Only lightly, if needed to support the workflow.

The sprint is not a custom software build.

### What is the difference between the `$2,500` and `$5,000` packages?

`$2,500` is the normal starting package for one workflow.

`$5,000` is the upgrade only when a second workflow, a cohort layer, or a more explicit before/after proof pass is clearly needed.

### What does success look like?

Success means:

- the workflow is clearer
- proof standards are more visible
- hidden rescue work is reduced or at least made explicit
- the team has one reusable operating surface instead of another one-off draft

### What does this not promise?

It does not promise:

- broad AI transformation
- autonomous execution
- guaranteed revenue
- better output just because the reporting looks better

### What proof do you have today?

Current proof is workflow and control-plane proof:

- internal operating surfaces
- review-gated brief logic
- receipt and rollback logic
- internal demo packaging that shows how the sprint would be evidenced

It is not yet scaled client proof.

### What happens after the sprint?

You should be left with a workflow your team can rerun more clearly, plus a better basis for deciding whether to stop there, extend the sprint, or invest in deeper tooling later.

### What is the best next step if I am curious?

Reply with one sentence naming the workflow that feels most painful, or take the short workflow call.
