# Operator Playbooks


This page is a discovery layer for a few high-value `work-business` workflows.

It does not replace canonical method. Each playbook points back to the real owning surface.

Wrapper contract: [docs/skills/workflow-wrapper-schema.md](../../skills/workflow-wrapper-schema.md)

## 1. Venture ideation / opportunity brief

### Purpose

Turn current assets, live lanes, and source-backed signals into a small set of operator-ready business opportunities.

### When to use

- You have several active lanes and need to choose the highest-leverage business move.
- You want a compact approval-first brief before spending time, money, or reputation.
- You suspect the next step is an extension of an existing lane rather than a new project.

### Inputs you'll need

- active lanes and recent work
- source-backed signals
- time and budget constraints
- operator risk appetite

### What happens

The workflow scans existing assets, proposes a small set of opportunities, scores them simply, and recommends one reversible next move.

### What you get

- a Top 3 opportunity brief
- one recommended winner
- one reversible first step

### Boundary

This workflow proposes only. It does not spawn lanes, spend money, publish, or bypass approval.

## 2. Offer or business blueprint

### Purpose

Translate operator skills and real constraints into a concrete offer, business model, or one-person business blueprint.

### When to use

- You want to turn a loose business idea into a specific offer.
- You need pricing, user fit, or launch-shape clarity.
- You want an operator brief that is grounded enough to test inside 30-90 days.

### Inputs you'll need

- skills or capabilities being sold
- time and capital constraints
- likely customer type
- current lanes or assets that can be reused

### What happens

The workflow narrows the business model, defines the offer, sketches delivery and pricing, and names the first small validation move.

### What you get

- a business or offer blueprint
- revenue logic or validation logic
- one next build or outreach step

### Boundary

This is a planning surface, not a claim that demand already exists. Thin evidence should stay visible.

## 3. Venture health / monthly operating review

### Purpose

Read one venture month clearly enough to decide whether the business is stable, drifting, or needs one concrete intervention.

### When to use

- It is month close or near a seasonal pivot.
- The venture feels noisy and you need one disciplined pass across books, shop health, marketing, and compliance readiness.
- You want one follow-up action without turning the month into a crisis.

### Inputs you'll need

- venture ledger or summary
- venture dashboard or marketplace stats
- receipts, payouts, expenses, and messages
- marketing plan or workflow reminders

### What happens

The workflow runs a four-movement review:

- books
- shop health
- marketing
- tax / compliance readiness

Each movement ends with an update, a parked note, or an explicit no-change judgment.

### What you get

- a monthly operating review note
- one concrete follow-up action
- clearer visibility into what is stable versus what needs attention

### Boundary

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice. It should not infer business facts without evidence.

## 4. Singularity-academy monetization / AI operating system sprint

### Purpose

Turn the repo's singularity, control-plane, and live-lane assets into a sellable service that can plausibly reach `$5,000` per month.

### When to use

- You want a real commercial wedge for `singularity-academy`.
- Content or workshop thinking is no longer enough; the next step is a paid offer.
- You need one service shape that reuses existing proof instead of inventing a new product category.

### Inputs you'll need

- existing singularity and control-plane assets
- one clear buyer type
- pricing appetite
- willingness to run real outreach and delivery

### What happens

The workflow narrows the monetization path to one bounded service, writes the offer, defines revenue logic, and names the first outreach and proof packet steps.

### What you get

- one service offer
- one `$5,000/mo` roadmap
- one next outbound wedge

### Boundary

This is a planning and packaging surface. It does not claim external demand before outreach or proof before client delivery.
