Each row should point to a real person, team, or organization you could plausibly contact within one week.

If you cannot imagine sending the first message soon, the row is too vague.

## Suggested mix

- `3` cohort or education operators

- `1` small service business with one obvious recurring workflow

## How to fill each column

### Why them now

Answer in one sentence:

- what recent condition makes the offer timely?

Examples:

- their team is already using AI heavily
- they run a recurring cohort
- they publish often but review is clearly strained

### Workflow pain

Name one workflow only.

Examples:

- daily team brief
- cohort onboarding and first-task proof
- research summary and review handoff
- internal customer-response workflow

### Pain sentence to use

Write the exact line you could use in outreach.

Good:

- `It seems like AI is active in your workflow, but the trusted reporting layer is still manual.`
- `You already have member motion, but setup still looks harder to count than to create.`

Bad:

- `I think singularity-academy could help your future of work transformation.`

### Warmth

- `hot` = existing trust or active conversation
- `warm` = known context, reachable with a personalized note
- `cold` = reachable, but not relationship-adjacent

First batch should skew `hot` and `warm`.

### First contact path

Examples:

- direct DM
- email reply to existing thread
- intro through mutual
- Telegram
- GitHub or community thread

## Fast scoring

Before outreach, mark each row:

- `5` = close fit, warm path, clear pain
- `4` = strong fit, some uncertainty
- `3` = interesting but not first-wave priority
- `2` or below = remove from first batch
