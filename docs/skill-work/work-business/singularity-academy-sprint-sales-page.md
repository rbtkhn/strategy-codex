# Singularity-Academy Sprint Sales Page

WORK only. This is draft buyer-facing copy, not market proof.

## Header

Install a small-team AI operating system in 30 days.

Make one recurring workflow easier to route, verify, and compound without giving away authority.

## Who this is for

This sprint is for:

- founder-operators already using AI daily
- small teams with reporting or review friction
- research or content teams that have motion but weak trust surfaces
- cohort or education operators who need beginner-safe proof loops

## The problem

Most teams do not need more AI tools first.

They need:

- one workflow that actually works
- one reporting surface that drives action
- one review layer that makes trust visible
- one clear answer to what AI may do and what still needs human approval

Without that, AI creates sprawl:

- too many drafts
- unclear authority
- enthusiasm instead of proof
- operator rescue hidden behind polished output

## What the sprint does

In 30 days, we install one bounded AI operating layer around a real recurring workflow.

That usually includes:

- workflow map
- daily or weekly brief
- review and receipt contract
- rollback conditions
- beginner-safe or team-safe task ladder
- compact handoff playbook

## What changes by the end

By the end of the sprint, you should have:

- one workflow that is easier to run
- clearer proof standards
- less invisible operator rescue
- a better boundary between assistance and authority
- a system your team can reuse instead of reinventing

## How it works

### Week 1

Map the workflow and identify where AI helps versus where it creates blur.

### Week 2

Install the reporting layer and define what counts as proof.

### Week 3

Install the review gate, receipt shape, and rollback conditions.

### Week 4

Run the loop again, compare what improved, and hand off a reusable playbook.
