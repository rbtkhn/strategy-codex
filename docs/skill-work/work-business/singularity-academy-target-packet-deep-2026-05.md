# Singularity-Academy Target Packet - deep.org - 2026-05

WORK only. Draft prospect wrapper, not sent communication.

## Why deep may be a fit

deep's public standards already emphasize:

- signal over volume
- clarity over hype
- independent judgment
- fast corrections

That suggests the visible output layer is already disciplined.

The likely pain is behind it:

- what becomes a brief
- what gets parked
- what still needs founder filtering
- what is strong enough to ship without invisible rescue

## Likely workflow in scope

Best first workflow hypothesis:

- research or editorial briefing and review handoff

The sprint would not try to redesign the whole operation.
It would focus on one recurring brief or review loop and make the trust layer easier to inspect.

## Why the sprint may be relevant

The AI Operating System Sprint is a `30`-day install for teams that already have strong standards but still rely on hidden human judgment to keep those standards real.

For deep, the likely value is:

- clearer routing of candidate ideas
- a visible review and receipt layer
- less invisible founder filtering
- a reusable operating surface instead of repeated one-off editorial rescue
- a clearer path for deciding when uncertain evidence is strong enough to publish as a brief

## Suggested package

Likely entry:

- `$2,500` base sprint

Reason:

- one workflow is probably enough to test the fit
- the pain seems more about routing and review than about a second workflow

`Is there one recurring editorial or research workflow where the standards are strong, but the operating layer still depends too much on invisible founder judgment?`
