WORK only. Draft prospect wrapper, not sent communication.

## Why 2389 Research may be a fit

2389's public posture centers on shipping:

- products
- research
- experiments
- AI workflow infrastructure with human gates and checkpointing

That kind of public-building rhythm usually creates one familiar strain:

- AI can accelerate parts of the work
- but review, handoff, and trust still collect around founders or senior people

## Likely workflow in scope

Best first workflow hypothesis:

- research-to-public-output handoff

Possible variants:

- research summary to product note
- experiment output to publishable artifact
- internal review before public release
- internal workflow reflection for products that already promise human-gated AI execution

## Why the sprint may be relevant

The sprint is not generic AI consulting.

For 2389, it would likely be a small operating-layer install around one workflow that already exists but still depends on too much invisible senior judgment.

The likely gains:

- clearer reporting around one workflow
- a visible review and receipt layer
- less hidden founder rescue
- a repeatable handoff instead of a one-off push to publish
- better alignment between the company's public control-plane posture and one internal workflow's actual review discipline

## Suggested package

Likely entry:

- `$2,500` base sprint

Reason:

- one workflow should be enough to test whether the hidden trust burden is real
