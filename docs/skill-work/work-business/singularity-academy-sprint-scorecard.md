# Singularity-Academy Sprint Scorecard

non-authoritative. This is a before/after measurement surface, not a guarantee of ROI.

## Purpose

Give the AI Operating System Sprint a repeatable way to show what changed between day 1 and day 30.

The scorecard should make workflow improvement more legible without pretending every workflow can be reduced to a single number.

## Use

Fill this at three moments:

1. before the sprint starts
2. at the midpoint, if useful
3. at the end of the sprint

Use it for:

- operator review
- proposal follow-through
- proof packet strengthening
- future case studies

## Scorecard categories

Score each from `1-5`.

### 1. Workflow clarity

Question:

- can the team clearly name the workflow, owner, and main steps?

### 2. Proof clarity

Question:

- does the team know what counts as completion or evidence?

### 3. Review burden visibility

Question:

- is operator rescue visible and discussable, or hidden and constant?

### 4. Authority clarity

Question:

- is it clear what AI may do and what still requires human approval?

### 5. Reuse

Question:

- can the workflow be rerun without rebuilding the process from scratch?

### 6. Team re-entry

Question:

- can someone re-enter the workflow after a gap without needing a full rescue?

## Short interpretation

After filling the scorecard, answer:

- what improved most?
- what stayed weak?
- what still depends on hidden operator labor?
- what is now reusable?

## Boundary

- this is not a revenue guarantee
- this is not a productivity benchmark against every possible alternative
- use evidence notes, not inflated claims

## Success pattern

A good sprint will usually show:

- clearer workflow naming
- stronger proof standards
- better authority lines
- more visible operator rescue, even if it is not eliminated yet
