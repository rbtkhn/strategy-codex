## Why AI Adjacent may be a fit

AI Adjacent is the closest public mirror of the sprint's logic.

Its public architecture already names:

- AI-first editorial flow
- human final review
- git-backed artifacts
- a human editor maintaining the platform
- daily briefs that are lightly automated

That means the likely pain is not "should we use AI?"
It is more specific:

- where does review still bottleneck?
- where does trust still depend on hidden editor rescue?
- what becomes reusable versus one-off?

## Likely workflow in scope

Best first workflow hypothesis:

- AI-assisted editorial draft to final reviewed publishable artifact

## Why the sprint may be relevant

For AI Adjacent, the sprint would not sell AI adoption.
It would sell one cleaner review and reuse layer inside a workflow that already exists.

The likely gains:

- clearer receipt and review logic
- less hidden trust work between draft and final judgment
- more reusable artifact flow
- a stronger handoff between generation and editorial authority
- a better boundary between the lightly automated daily layer and the final reviewed issue layer
