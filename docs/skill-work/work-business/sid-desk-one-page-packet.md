# Statecraft Intelligence Desk — One-Page Packet

WORK only. Sendable external draft. Not proof of market demand.

## Purpose

Give a close-fit prospect one short page on **Statecraft Intelligence Desk (SID)** without repo vocabulary, harness jargon, or a philosophy lecture.

Use after first contact, when a prospect asks for the one-pager, or when a warm intro needs an attachable scope summary.

---

## What this is

A **managed geopolitical intelligence retainer** — not software, not a wire, not a newsletter.

**Statecraft Intelligence Desk (SID)** delivers **accountable judgment objects** on **your named theaters** (for example: Iran escalation, Gulf shipping, CFIUS-relevant China exposure, sanctions jurisdictions you choose in the SOW):

- **Situation Brief** (*SID Brief*) — daily operating context on those theaters (delta-first: what changed since prior day)
- **Desk Synthesis** — weekly bounded analytical object for internal briefing
- **Transaction memos** — draft background shaped for partner review: pin-cites, escalation ladder, explicit falsifiers
- **Office hours** — directed sessions on retainer scope (not open-ended chat)

The goal is not more headlines. The goal is **objects that survive partner review** when a client asks about escalation, exposure, or sanctions drift.

---

## Best-fit buyers

**Strong fit:**

- sanctions, CFIUS, or national-security practices needing **client-shaped background** faster than associate queues allow
- energy and commodity desks needing **escalation objects** for risk committee — not headline summaries
- allocators and family offices with **tail-risk** in named theaters
- public operators who need **decision copy** separated from audience copy

**Weak fit:**

- buyers wanting a cheap subscription article feed
- firms seeking cyber, compliance, or due-diligence platform in the same SKU
- buyers who need **legal advice to clients** from an external vendor (SID is judgment support for **your** professionals)
- procurement that will only buy multi-seat SaaS before any pilot

## What you keep using

SID sits **above** sources you already pay for:

- **Bloomberg / Reuters** — unchanged
- **RANE or similar** — unchanged; SID often complements geo platform breadth with **escalation memos on named theaters**
- **Internal AI (Copilot, Harvey, custom agents)** — unchanged for firm precedents; SID owns **live external theater context** with receipts

- up to **three named theaters** (defined in SOW)

- **weekly Desk Synthesis**
- **two transaction memos** per month

Optional add-ons (quoted separately):

- **Surge week** — crisis cadence, extra memo, 48h turnaround

---

## What this is not

- **Not legal advice** — draft background for **your** internal professional use; **you** retain all client-facing legal conclusions
- **Not investment advice** — no trading signals
- **Not counsel of record** — B2B vendor; no direct client communication unless explicitly expanded (not recommended on pilot)
- **Not a software license** — professional services retainer
- **Not 24/7 operations** at base retainer — bounded cadence with defined deliverables
