# Packet before synthesis

**Preferred activation (operator):** say **`packet-first`**.

Use this skill when a strategy seam is clearly important, but not yet clean enough to compose directly into stronger notebook prose.

## When to run

- A seam is valuable but still mixed in evidence quality.
- More than one evidence layer is present and they are doing different jobs.
- An official-primary line is being translated, narrowed, or amplified by a commentator or elite-English gloss.
- A source is pushing you toward a large civilizational or historical inference before the maturity ladder is earned.
- The notebook is at risk of producing one elegant sentence that is flatter than the seam itself.

## Core idea

Not every good source should become judgment immediately.

Some seams should first become **disciplined potential**:

- preserved
- attributed
- tiered
- held open until they mature

## Workflow

1. **Name the seam's real value**
   - Decide whether the seam's main value is:
     - **mechanism**
     - **process**
     - **register**
   - Use [packet-crosswalk.md](../../docs/skill-work/work-strategy/packet-crosswalk.md) if the choice is not obvious.

2. **Check for a dual-register problem**
   - Ask whether the seam contains:
     - an **official-primary** line
     - plus an **attributed gloss**, interpreter layer, or elite-English translation
   - If yes, do not collapse them into one â€œstate saysâ€ or â€œposition isâ€ sentence yet.

3. **Decide whether to packet first**
   - Use packet-first discipline when:
     - factual closure is incomplete
     - rhetoric itself is informative
     - symbolic or historical reach is outrunning evidence maturity

4. **Preserve the seam in the right shape**
   - Build or refine:
     - a **refined page**
     - a **source-hygiene note**
     - or a layered seam note if the owning file already exists
   - Follow [source-hygiene-packets.md](../../docs/skill-work/work-strategy/source-hygiene-packets.md) for the output shape.

5. **Use the dual-register maturity sequence when needed**
   - Preserve:
     1. the **official-primary** object
     2. the **attributed gloss**
     3. any later **institutional corroboration or divergence**
   - Only after that should you broaden into stronger notebook synthesis.

6. **Protect against historical overreach**
   - If the seam tempts a broad historical or civilizational claim, require an intermediate maturity ladder first.
   - Symbolic diplomacy, legitimacy gestures, and audience-facing moral language should not jump directly to â€œalignment,â€ â€œuniqueness,â€ or â€œcivilizational turnâ€ without layered support.

## Minimal operator test

Before composing stronger prose, ask:

1. What exactly is official-primary here?
2. What is still attributed gloss rather than state line?
3. Is the seam's main value mechanism, process, or register?
4. Has any second institutional or field receipt matured the seam?
5. If not, is the unresolved gap itself the most truthful object to preserve?

If `5` is yes, stop and packet first.

## Worked example

### Rome-Persia legitimacy seam

You notice two related objects:

- **official-primary**: Pezeshkian posts to `@Pontifex` in a dignity-and-peace register
- **elite-English gloss**: Marandi uses Christian honorific and Easter-facing language toward a Western audience while framing the U.S. as coercive

The wrong move would be:

- "Iran is aligning with the Vatican"

That sentence is too early because it collapses:

- a state dignity signal
- an elite translation layer
- and a hoped-for institutional pattern

The packet-first move is:

1. preserve the presidential post as the official-primary object
2. preserve the Marandi layer as attributed gloss, not as state line
3. ask what would mature the seam:
   - Vatican acknowledgment
   - repeated Presidency or MFA usage
   - third-party institutional uptake
4. keep the notebook claim narrow until that maturity arrives

The better interim sentence is:

- "A possible Rome-Persia legitimacy wedge is emerging, but the current evidence is still layered: one official dignity signal, one elite-English translation, and no fully mature institutional uptake yet."

That is the feel of this skill: protect the seam from elegance before it earns it.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn packet work into stealth `days.md` composition.
- Do not merge official-primary wording and commentator gloss into one synthetic state line before maturity.
- Do not treat rhetoric as event-fact.
- Do not use packet work to avoid making judgments forever; use it to delay judgment until the seam becomes more honest.
- Do not create a new taxonomy shelf if the seam already has an owning lane or file.

## Success condition

The seam is easier to reuse later, the evidence boundary is clearer, and future synthesis is less likely to flatten layered signals into false certainty.
