# Iran Lit

## Purpose

Use Iranian / Persian literature as a statecraft lens for Ferdowsi, Shahnameh, Persianate continuity, poetry as sovereignty, martyrdom, dignity, memory beyond regime, and language as a carrier of civilizational survival.

## Civilizational Function

Iranian literature keeps sovereignty alive across rupture. Language, epic, poetry, philosophy, and modern literary witness make the Iran lane see that dignity is not merely diplomatic face: it is a carrier of continuity when states, dynasties, and regimes fail.

The arc is layered:

- **Origin:** Persian language and epic preserve civilizational selfhood across conquest and Islamization.
- **Continuity:** Ferdowsi/Shahnameh, poetry, science-philosophy figures, and Persianate administration keep memory beyond regime.
- **Transformation:** modern rupture, Hedayat/Nima, Mossadegh/1953, revolution, war, and sanctions turn literary sovereignty into dignity, witness, and resistance.
- **Current carrier:** public language, poetry, education, diplomacy, state ceremony, clerical-republican rhetoric, and cultural memory carry the arc now.
- **Failure mode:** memory becomes grievance capture, poetry substitutes for institutional guarantees, martyrdom forbids relief, or dignity blocks solvency.
- **Transaction use:** test whether restraint can be narrated as continuity and dignity rather than surrender.

## Statecraft Signals

- **Epic continuity:** Iran can survive state-form change through language and memory.
- **Poetry as sovereignty:** beauty can carry dignity when power is constrained.
- **Martyrdom / witness:** suffering can legitimate endurance, but also harden compromise.
- **Memory beyond regime:** a settlement must speak to Iran, not only to the Islamic Republic.
- **Language as recognition:** wording matters because it determines whether restraint reads as sovereign choice or imposed humiliation.

## Failure Mode

Literary memory deforms when dignity becomes humiliation lock, martyrdom forbids relief, poetic sovereignty avoids concrete institutional guarantees, or memory beyond regime is captured by current carrier preference.
