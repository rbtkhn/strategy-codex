# Iran Civilization Seed Patterns

These seeds translate CIV-MEM into Iran-lane civilization patterns. State rules should draw from these patterns and the Iran empire folder, not directly from CIV-MEM.

## Dignity And Sovereignty

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–CORE–PERSIA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–DOCTRINE–PERSIA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–MEDES.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–ACHAEMENID–DYNASTY.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–ACHAEMENID–CYRUS–II.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–ACHAEMENID–DARIUS–I.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–SATRAPIES.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–SASANIAN–DYNASTY.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–LAW–SASANIAN.md`.
- **Pattern:** Iran needs recognition as a sovereign actor, not management as an object of policy.
- **Statecraft use:** Build non-regime-change assurances, reciprocal recognition formulas, and dignity-preserving verification into proposals.
- **Failure mode:** Material relief fails because the language humiliates Iran or appears to bargain over Iran without Iran.

## Resistance And Martyrdom Grammar

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–STATE–PERSIA.md`; `docs/civilization-memory/book/APPLIED-THEOLOGY-TEST-MEM-PERSIA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–PARTHIAN.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–ISLAM.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–SAFAVID–DYNASTY.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–IRAN–ISLAMIC–REVOLUTION.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–IRAN–ISLAMIC–REPUBLIC.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–WAR–IRAN–IRAQ.md`.
- **Pattern:** Pressure can strengthen legitimacy when it is read as domination.
- **Statecraft use:** Convert resistance into regulated leverage: what Iran will reopen, restrain, or verify in exchange for recognition and relief.
- **Failure mode:** The instrument rewards permanent defiance and makes compromise look like betrayal.

## Plateau Continuity

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/CIV–INDEX–PERSIA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–GEO–IRANIAN–PLATEAU.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–GEO–PERSIAN–GULF.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–QAJAR.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–LAW–CONSTITUTION.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–LAW–MONARCHY.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/PERSIA/MEM–PERSIA–IRAN–MOSSADEGH.md`.
- **Pattern:** Iran's geography and civilizational depth make survival and autonomy central.
- **Statecraft use:** Treat territorial integrity, export autonomy, and regional voice as successor-stable interests.
- **Failure mode:** A deal assumes the current regime is the whole Iranian question and misses deeper state continuity.
