# President / Government Carrier

Refresh current officeholder facts before load-bearing use.

## Role In Current State Form

The president and government carrier implements diplomacy, budgets, public services, trade, sanctions relief, administrative compliance, and public-facing relief.

## Civilizational Pattern Link

The president and government are the administrative carriers of [Iran state memory](../civilization/objects/state-memory.md). They must turn dignity-preserving settlement language into relief, budgets, trade, public services, and visible competence, or the memory of sanctions and broken guarantees will re-harden against restraint.

## Institutional Carrier

Foreign ministry, economic ministries, central bank, energy bureaucracy, parliament, and provincial administration carry implementation.

## Domestic Coalition

The government must manage public hardship, factional suspicion, sanctions fatigue, administrative capacity, and the need to show material gains from restraint.

## Settlement Credibility

Can implement relief, inspections logistics, trade channels, energy exports, and public-order measures if supreme/security carriers do not veto the deal.

## Warning

Administrative signature is not enough for nuclear, Hormuz, or regional-security restraint unless the supreme/security carriers are aligned.
