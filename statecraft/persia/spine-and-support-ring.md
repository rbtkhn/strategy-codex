# Persia Lane Spine And Support Ring

Purpose: name the Persia lane's actual main route and distinguish it from the strengthening surfaces that deepen the route without replacing it.

## Core Spine

The canonical Persia lane spine is:

```text
Helix -> current state carriers -> transaction drafting
```

Read it this way:

1. [Iran helix](helix.md) restores the integrated line of dignity, sovereignty, leverage, deformation, and restoration.
2. [Iran state](state/) decides who can authorize, implement, restrain, veto, or spoil the live move.
3. [Persia transactions](transactions/) converts the resolved lane object into spendable mechanism or clause language.

This is the honest main route. It should stay stable unless the lane itself is redesigned.

## Use Rule

Use the support ring to sharpen, test, or restore the main line.

Do not let support material displace the helix as the primary interpretive surface, and do not jump directly from support material into transaction drafting without resolving the carrier layer first.

## Authority Guard

- `helix.md` remains the primary Persia interpretive surface
- first-wave strand files remain supporting source-bearing lane surfaces
- transaction authority remains in the Persia lane
- bridge adapters remain transition tools, not substitutes for the lane
