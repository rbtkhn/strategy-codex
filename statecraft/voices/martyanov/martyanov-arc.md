# Martyanov Arc

Martyanov's durable statecraft role is to force political and alliance rhetoric back through military means.

He is the shelf to open when the notebook needs a speaker who can connect:

- force generation
- industrial capacity
- fires and attrition
- military balance
- and the point where battlefield reality has already decided what politics is still pretending remains open

## Durable Cross-Host Role

Martyanov is not primarily a legal narrator, domestic-liability narrator, or intelligence-memory narrator.

His cross-host role is narrower and more stable:

- military-systems realism
- force-assessment discipline
- strategic-collapse interpretation
- war-outcome correction through material capability

This makes him a clean complement to:

- [Postol](../postol/README.md) for engineering mechanism and weapon-effect realism

- [Barnes](../barnes/README.md) for domestic liability and room-failure
