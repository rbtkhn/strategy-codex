
# Martyanov Helix

Martyanov is a helix-first speaker object with three materially real but uneven branches.

That means the speaker is best understood through recurring host-conditioned branches rather than through a flat list of appearances or an early speaker-native month ladder.

## Core Branches

### Dialogue Works x Martyanov

This is the clearest mature first-open branch.

It owns:

- live-pressure systems realism
- strategic-collapse language
- force-balance diagnosis under Iran-war heat
- present-tense translation of escalation theater into material military consequence

If the notebook needs the hottest current Martyanov lane, this is usually the best first-open surface.

### Davis x Martyanov

This is a strong real secondary branch.

It owns:

- battlefield outcome
- military feasibility
- attritional correction
- Europe / NATO fantasy versus what the balance has already decided

It does not outrank Dialogue Works for live-pressure crisis reads, but it is stronger than thin reinforcement.

### Diesen x Martyanov

This branch is materially real on disk.

It owns:

- escalation horizon
- order-consequence
- what force reality implies for broader geopolitical structure

It matters, but it remains thinner than the other two branches.

## What The Helix Does

Across these branches, the same Martyanov object survives:

- he drags rhetoric back to force generation and industrial reality
- he turns strategic panic into arguments about material capability
- he keeps reading political failure through what the military balance has already made unavoidable

That is enough to justify a canonical speaker shelf even though chronology still lives more naturally in host-local entries and transcript files.

## Boundary

This is not a flat triple-equal-strand claim.

- `Dialogue Works x Martyanov` remains the clearest first-open branch.
- `Davis x Martyanov` is a strong real secondary branch.
- `Diesen x Martyanov` is materially real, but still thinner than the other two.
- the shelf stays lighter because chronology is still owned more cleanly by host arcs and direct captures than by speaker-native monthly synthesis.
