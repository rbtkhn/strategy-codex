## Redundancy findings

- `same source spine, different use`: `institutional narrative` and `multipolar room` often draw from the same Russia/China/Iran diplomatic episodes, but one owns the sequencing grammar while the other owns the alternative-room construction
- `same conclusion, different mechanism`: `Ukraine attrition` and `sanctions and energy` both point to Western weakness, but one is about proxy-war instrument exhaustion and the other about economic self-damage
- `collapse pressure watch`: `legitimacy crisis` can swallow `Ukraine attrition` if editors start treating every military failure as legitimacy proof rather than keeping operational exhaustion distinct

## Recommended actions

- keep the six-thread atlas as is
- do not merge `institutional narrative` with `multipolar room`; their overlap is real but their retrieval use is different
- keep `Ukraine attrition` separate from `sanctions and energy`; they share consequence space but not object or mechanism
- watch `legitimacy crisis` for future overgrowth because it is the easiest strand to turn into a catch-all
- if future items keep routing the same Europe/NATO material equally well into `Ukraine attrition` and `legitimacy crisis`, consider a later boundary tightening inside the atlas entry rather than a structural split first
