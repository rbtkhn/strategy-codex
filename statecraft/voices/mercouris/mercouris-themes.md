Purpose: hold the top-six Mercouris historical-theme commentary surfaces for the bounded `2025-01-01 -> present` Mercouris arc.

## Use

Open this folder when the task is:

- tracing one recurring historical-theme class across Mercouris's 2025-present material
- preparing supplementary evidence for `civ-state`
- deciding which Mercouris historical patterns deserve stronger backward historical scaffolding

Open [mercouris-historical-examples-audit.md](mercouris-historical-examples-audit.md when the task is ranking, scoring, or widening the evidence base. Open the `civ-state` companion files when the task is backward historical deepening rather than speaker-side recurrence.
