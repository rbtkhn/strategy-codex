## Why this shelf remains lighter than Johnson

Johnson earned a bounded month ladder because:

- he has much denser host diversity
- his shelf performs month-bounded synthesis as a primary retrieval job
- the month shelves do real compression work that host arcs alone no longer perform efficiently

Barnes is not there yet.

The Barnes shelf is better served right now by:

- a strong person arc
- a clear routing note
- a disciplined raw-input bench
- a cross-host note that does not overstate equality

That gives most of the retrieval value without prematurely inventing chronology ownership.
