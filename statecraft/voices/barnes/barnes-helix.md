## Core claim

Barnes is not currently a symmetric multi-strand helix in the Johnson or Ritter sense.

The right reading is:

`dominant Davis strand + real Mercouris reinforcement + thin but meaningful Nima reinforcement + Johnson support-tier residue`

That is stronger than a Davis-only guest run, but weaker than a co-equal double or triple helix.

## What May 26 changes

The May 26 Dialogue Works source matters in three ways:

- it proves the Barnes object does not stop in April
- it gives Barnes a real late-May cross-theater entry where Iran and Ukraine are being spoken in one pressure frame
- it strengthens `Nima x Barnes` from a hypothetical possibility into a real reinforcing branch

What it does **not** do:

- it does not make `Nima x Barnes` co-equal with Davis
- it does not erase Mercouris as the stronger non-Davis reinforcing lane
- it does not justify a full Barnes month ladder on this first pass

So the right reassessment is:

`Nima is now durable enough to name; still not thick enough to govern the shelf`

## Orthogonality guide

Quick separation rule:

- `Davis x Barnes` is where Barnes is most precise on war-powers, legal exposure, and domestic political survivability.
- `Mercouris x Barnes` is where Barnes's executive-instability and anti-regime-change voice is most clearly proven outside Davis, but still inside a stronger Mercouris-native stream object.
- `Nima x Barnes` is where Barnes is most useful for the late-May synthesis of Trump decline, Israel-lobby pressure, Gulf pain, and Iran/Ukraine overlap.

Do not use `Nima` as the default Barnes lane just because it is the newest confirming file. Do not use `Mercouris` to replace Davis's primary maturity. Do not use Johnson support-tier residue as if it were a mature fourth strand.

Also keep one non-Davis seam explicit:

- Barnes = domestic liability, political survivability, factional coherence, and war-powers exposure
- [Napolitano host shelf](../../channels/judging-freedom/README.md) = process-pressure, constitutional absurdity, and blockade skepticism pushed through an institutional-irrationality host frame

That overlap is real, but the first-open jobs are different.
