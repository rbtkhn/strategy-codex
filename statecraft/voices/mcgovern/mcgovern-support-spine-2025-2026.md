
# McGovern Support Spine 2025-2026

Purpose: explain why McGovern is canonical now, why the shelf is still intentionally lighter than a month ladder, and what would justify later extension.

## Why McGovern Is Canonical Now

McGovern is no longer just residue because three things are already true:

- there is a real direct archive body across multiple years and more than one host
- the same intelligence-dissent object is visibly stable across `Diesen`, `Dialogue Works`, and now a materially real `Judging Freedom` branch
- live statecraft routing already needs him as the institutional-memory and elite-unreality complement to Postol, Barnes, Marandi, and Johnson

That is enough for a canonical shelf.

## Why The Shelf Stays Lighter

The shelf should still remain lighter than a Johnson-style month ladder because:

- host arcs still own chronology more cleanly than a speaker-native month stack would
- the object is structurally cross-host, but not yet served better by monthly synthesis than by direct raw-input and host-conditioned entry
- the Judging Freedom branch is real, but not yet thick enough to demand triple-helix or full month-ladder treatment

So the right current shape is:

- canonical speaker identity
- routing discipline
- compact provenance bench
- explicit helix doctrine
- support spine that blocks symmetry pressure

## What Would Justify Extension

Extend beyond this lighter grammar only if one or more of the following becomes materially true:

- the Judging Freedom run becomes thick enough on disk to demand a co-equal branch reading
- one or more host branches becomes dense enough that month-native speaker synthesis is cleaner than host-local chronology
- the notebook starts needing bounded McGovern month support often enough that host-local entry is no longer the cleanest first-open surface

## Growth Rule

Grow this shelf by density, not by symmetry.

The next good extension would likely be a thicker raw-input bench or one bounded support note, not an automatic `2026-01` through `2026-05` ladder.
