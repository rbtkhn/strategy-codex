
# McGovern Routing

Use McGovern when the notebook needs intelligence dissent and institutional memory, not when it mainly needs engineering truth, domestic factional mapping, or a purely legal-process read by itself.

## Open Diesen First

Open [Diesen x McGovern](../../notes/arc-mcgovern-diesen-host.md) first when the real question is:

- how should Russia or Europe-order consequence be read
- what does institutional memory say about elite strategic misreading
- how do NATO narratives detach from reality
- how should long-horizon imperial overreach be interpreted

## Do Not Open McGovern First

Do not open McGovern first when the real question is:

- can the strike physically work or what the weapon can actually do
  - open [Postol](../postol/README.md)
- domestic liability, room-failure, or survivability of the White House coalition
  - open [Barnes](../barnes/README.md)
- constitutional absurdity or blockade irrationality without the intelligence-memory layer
  - open [Johnson](../johnson/README.md) or [Napolitano host shelf](../../channels/judging-freedom/README.md)
- Iranian legitimacy, resolve, or civilizational red lines
  - open [Marandi](../marandi/README.md)

## Current Shelf Rule

McGovern is canonical as a speaker shelf because the same intelligence-dissent object recurs across hosts.

He is not a month-ladder shelf because the chronology is still owned more cleanly by host arcs, source captures, and a bounded raw-input bench than by speaker-native monthly synthesis.
