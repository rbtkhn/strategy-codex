## Core maturity claim

Ritter is mature enough to deserve both a stronger speaker-owned spine and a full bounded native month ladder for the live 2026 run, but still not a chronology takeover.

Why:

- 2025 already materializes a real `Diesen + Dialogue Works` double-helix phase
- January and February 2026 are thick enough to justify canonical segmented entry
- March, April, and May 2026 are true mature retrieval months
- the strongest useful chronology still belongs to host-local arcs and raw-input

So the right reading is:

`mature speaker object with host-led chronology and speaker-owned bounded month synthesis`

not:

`speaker shelf waiting to seize chronology`
