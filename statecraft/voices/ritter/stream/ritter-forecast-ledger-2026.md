## Phase Spine

**Russia / deep-structure warning, early January.** Ritter opens the year with Russia and Karaganov as an order-level warning: Western readings of Russia, deterrence, and strategic patience are not just analytically wrong but dangerous. The Russia lane remains a deep-structure reference point for later Iran and U.S. decline arguments.

**Sanctions, Iranian resilience, and U.S. republic decline, January.** The early corpus pairs external pressure failure with domestic U.S. degradation. Sanctions do not automatically break adversaries; Iranian resilience becomes explicit; U.S. republican decay and strategic overreach appear as linked rather than separate problems.

**Nuclear and second-order escalation warning, February-March.** Ritter's warning register intensifies around nuclear danger, youthful death, indirect consequences, and the claim that regime-change thinking is a double-edged sword. This phase is less a neat forecast sequence than a warning system about blowback.

**Moral break with Israel / Zionism, March-April.** The lane turns sharply prosecutorial. Ritter frames some doctrines and actors as morally corrupt rather than merely mistaken, while also making a formal political break with Israel. This heat is part of the lane and must be tracked without letting it substitute for operational proof.

**Operational warning and new Middle Eastern power paradigm, April.** The final Substack phase returns to military-professional detail through combat search and rescue, then widens into regional order change. The through-line is that U.S./Israeli operational assumptions, sea-control assumptions, and regime-change assumptions are being overtaken by a new balance of power.
