
# Davis Host Law

Purpose: state the governing pressure Davis applies so live statecraft routing can open the right host without relying on retired codex doctrine.

## Core Rule

Davis is the host to open when the real question is:

- what can still work
- what coercion can no longer recover
- what bargaining room is real rather than narrated
- what military pressure no longer translates into a usable settlement

## Governing Transformation

Davis repeatedly pushes guests toward:

- force feasibility
- coercive limits
- bargaining geometry
- inventory and posture reality
- end-state realism

## Open Davis First When

- the political objective and the military plan seem mismatched
- the pressure campaign may be outrunning its settlement logic
- a guest needs practical war-termination testing
- a shelf is trapped in legal or symbolic language and needs practical-room discipline

## Do Not Misread Davis As

- a legal-process host
- a constitutional-war-powers host
- a purely geopolitical-altitude host

Those can overlap, but Davis owns the practical-room test.
