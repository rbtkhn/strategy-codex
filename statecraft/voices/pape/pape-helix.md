
# Pape Helix

Purpose: compact note for reading Pape's authored forecast spine together with his interview-pressure appearances.

## Core Claim

The Pape helix is a **two-strand forecast-pressure helix**:

- the **authored strand** states the mechanism: smart bomb trap, escalation ledger, ground threshold, blockade / shortage clocks, global contraction, and the fourth-center thesis
- the **interview strand** tests that mechanism under public host pressure: Davis for military feasibility, Redacted for macro shock translation, Ryan Grim for fourth-center framing, and Mario Nawfal for warning-sign compression

This is not the same shape as Crooke, Ritter, or a dense host shelf. Pape is primarily an authored forecast object with targeted public-pressure reinforcement.
