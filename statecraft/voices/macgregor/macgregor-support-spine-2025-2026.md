## Core maturity claim

Macgregor is mature enough to deserve both a stronger speaker-owned spine and bounded native monthly synthesis shelves, but still not the same kind of chronology ownership as Ritter.

Why:

- the shelf has a real January-May 2025 opening run across Davis, Napolitano, Tucker, Diesen, and Johnson
- the shelf has real late-2025 continuity through Davis and Diesen
- 2026 March and April are clearly mature retrieval months
- the Macgregor object is highly reusable across hosts
- but the useful chronology still belongs more naturally to host-local arcs than to speaker-native month narratives

So the right reading is:

`mature speaker object with host-led chronology and speaker-owned bounded month synthesis`

not:

`speaker shelf waiting for chronology takeover`
