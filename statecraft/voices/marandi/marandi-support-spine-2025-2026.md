## Core maturity claim

Marandi is mature enough to deserve a canonical statecraft shelf with a strong three-host identity.

Why:

- the shelf has real 2025 anchors rather than a purely local spring-2026 burst
- the three host roles are clearly differentiated on disk
- the spring 2026 cluster is dense enough to make the shelf reusable at speaker scope
- the May 26 source extends the mature cluster cleanly to the current archive endpoint

But the shelf still does not need a full speaker-native month ladder yet because:

- the host-local arcs remain the cleanest chronology owners
- the main retrieval job is host-conditioned Iranian-side interpretation, not month-bounded synthesis
- the shelf already gets most of its value from the arc, routing note, raw-input bench, and helix

So the right reading is:

`mature three-host speaker shelf with host-led chronology`

not:

`proto-Johnson waiting to become month-led by symmetry alone`

Barnes has one dominant mature branch and reinforcement around it.

Marandi is different:

- the host roles are cleaner and more stable
- the shelf is already helix-mature rather than only reinforcement-mature

So Marandi deserves a stronger cross-host claim than Barnes, even though both shelves remain lighter than Johnson.

## Future extension law
