# Marandi arc

Purpose: hold Marandi as a whole speaker object across hosts and across the 2025-present materialized run without prematurely turning the shelf into a Johnson-style month lattice.

## Core claim

Marandi is a real cross-host statecraft speaker object because he repeatedly performs the same translation job across hosts:

- turn Iranian red lines into durable English-language legitimacy arguments
- turn coercive pressure into claims about endurance, retaliation, and failed intimidation
- turn siege, blockade, and bombing stories into sovereignty, complicity, and differentiated-punishment arguments
- turn immediate crisis incidents into wider claims about regional security architecture and post-U.S. order

Marandi is therefore not just a repeated Iran guest. He is a true three-host speaker object.

## Durable cross-host role

Marandi is most useful when a shelf would otherwise stay trapped in Western process language and needs the Iranian regime-adjacent plane:

- how Iran narrates legitimacy and victimhood
- what Iran treats as compellence versus surrender
- how Tehran-facing voices frame Hormuz, siege, and GCC complicity
- where Iranian red lines harden and where conditional diplomacy still survives

That role survives host translation strongly enough that Marandi reads as a speaker shelf rather than a single-lane expert thread.
