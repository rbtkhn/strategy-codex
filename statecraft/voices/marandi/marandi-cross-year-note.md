# Marandi cross-year note


Purpose: clarify where the mature Marandi object really begins and how the shelf should treat `2025-present` continuity without pretending that all captured material has equal arc weight.

## Governing window

The governing continuity window is `2025-present`.

That window is correct for ownership and continuity, but the shelf should distinguish three layers inside it:

1. early anchors
2. maturing recurrence
3. mature spring 2026 core

## Early anchors

The 2025 and early 2026 material matters because it shows that Marandi is not a one-month accident or a single-host burst.

Those files establish:

- host-distributed continuity
- a recognizable Tehran-facing legitimacy and red-line register
- early recurrence before the shelf fully thickens

They should be treated as continuity anchors, not as the sole statement of the mature object.

## Maturing recurrence

By early 2026, the shelf begins to show clearer host-role differentiation:

- Diesen for strategic-order and escalation horizon
- Davis for operational limits and failed intimidation
- Dialogue Works for live pressure, legitimacy, and selective-Hormuz signaling

This is where the Marandi object starts becoming reusable as a true cross-host speaker shelf.

## Mature core

The mature center of gravity is spring 2026, especially April and May 2026.

That is where the shelf becomes dense enough to support:

- a canonical person arc
- a recurring thread atlas
- bounded month-level cross-host surfaces
- a stable theme-family layer

This is the period that should carry most of the interpretive weight unless new earlier material materially changes the balance.

## Boundary

- `2025-present` is the correct continuity window.
- Spring 2026 is the current mature recurring core.
- Early anchors remain important, but they should not be overstated into a false equivalence with the dense April-May 2026 run.
