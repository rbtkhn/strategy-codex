# Parsi helix


Purpose: compact note for reading Parsi's authored diplomacy mechanism together with his interview-pressure appearances.

## Core claim

The Parsi helix is a **two-track policy-process helix**:

- the authored track states the diplomacy mechanism
- the interview track tests that mechanism under host pressure

This is not the same shape as a dense host-transformation shelf. Parsi's stronger structure is authored depth plus targeted interview pressure.
