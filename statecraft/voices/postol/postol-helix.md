
# Postol Helix

Postol is a helix-first speaker object.

That means the speaker is best understood through recurring host-conditioned branches rather than through a flat list of appearances or an early speaker-native month ladder.

## Core Branches

### Davis x Postol

This is the clearest mature first-open branch.

It owns:

- strike feasibility
- bunker-buster skepticism
- destructive-capacity realism
- target survivability
- engineering constraint under war rhetoric

If the notebook needs to know whether a plan can physically work, this is usually the best first-open lane.

### Diesen x Postol

This is a real co-supporting branch.

It owns:

- deterrence consequence
- escalation horizon
- what physical constraints imply for broader balance
- strategic-order consequence downstream of technical fact

It should not outrank Davis for mechanism-first questions, but it is stronger than thin reinforcement.

### Dialogue Works x Postol

This is a real co-supporting branch.

It owns:

- live-pressure missile-effects reads
- air-defense collapse reads
- technical correction under present-tense Iran-war pressure
- hot-stage escalation framing with a still recognizably Postol mechanism core

The branch is already clearly materialized by March 23, April 3, and May 8, 2026.

## What The Helix Does

Across these branches, the same Postol object survives:

- he drags the notebook back to what the systems can physically do
- he turns war prestige claims into mechanism questions
- he narrows ambiguity by forcing discussion onto radars, interceptors, penetration, survivability, and repairability

That is enough to justify a canonical speaker shelf even though the chronology still lives more naturally in host-local entries.

## Boundary

This is not a triple-equal-strand claim.

- `Davis x Postol` is still the clearest first-open branch.
- `Diesen x Postol` and `Dialogue Works x Postol` are real, not ornamental.
- the shelf is helix-first because those branches are materially real.
- the shelf remains lighter because they are not yet equally thick enough to justify a speaker-native monthly ladder.
