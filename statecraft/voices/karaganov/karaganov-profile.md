## Introduction

Sergey Karaganov is the Russian elite **strategic-doctrine and nuclear-deterrence warning** lane: honorary chairman of the Council on Foreign and Defense Policy, former adviser to Yeltsin and Putin, and the most cited in-repo voice for **reinstalling the nuclear fuse**, lowering use thresholds, and **Age of Wars** system framing. Primary recurring surface is **Glenn Diesen** (solo and Mercouris tri-panels; one Mearsheimer triangulation). Treat his speech as **doctrine advocacy and coercive sobering**, not settled Kremlin policy, unless separately evidenced.

## Signature mechanisms

- **Nuclear fuse reinstall:** deterrence decay → lower threshold / limited European strike doctrine → sober elites.
- **Age of Wars:** multipolar transition gap (10–20y) before BRICS+/SCO-style order; biological weapons / cheap missiles as parallel fuses.
- **Dignified Western rollback:** flying-banner defeat vs catastrophe; Ukraine as European center, not sole conflict.
- **Procrastination thesis:** Russia tolerated aggression too long; war should have started earlier (cost minimization claim).
- **World majority:** civilizational multipolar end-state after surviving transition wars.

## Failure modes / overreads

- **Do not** treat Karaganov speech as **confirmed Russian operational order** without separate primary/military evidence.
- **Do not** merge **Ritter** “dark sage” gloss into Karaganov verbatim doctrine.
- Western **call-the-bluff** press behavior is often cited through **Mercouris block** in same episode — tag speaker on each claim.
