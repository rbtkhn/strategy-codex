
# Karaganov support spine 2025-2026

Purpose: explain why Karaganov is canonical now, why the shelf stays lighter than a month ladder, and what would justify extension.

## Why Karaganov is canonical now

Karaganov is no longer only a `codex/speakers/` stub because:

- there is a **ranked Diesen guest arc** with **six** direct archive appearances (2023–2026)
- the full run — Mercouris triads (2023, 2024), 2025 solos, 2026 solo + Mearsheimer panel — is materialized in **Statecraft Archive**
- live routing needs a **Russian elite doctrine object** separable from Kremlin commentary and from Western reaction

That is enough for `statecraft/voices/karaganov/`.

## Why the shelf stays lighter

The shelf should remain lighter than a Johnson-style month ladder because:

- **one host** owns almost all chronology (`Diesen`)
- there is **no cross-host mature core** yet
- Karaganov's value is **doctrinal retrieval**, not month-by-month synthesis symmetry

Current grammar:

- person arc + routing + source index + single-helix note + support spine
- host arc owns ranked episode law
- no faked month ladder beyond what the Diesen run actually provides

## What would justify extension

Extend only if one or more becomes materially true:

- additional **direct** Karaganov guest captures outside Diesen
- repeated notebook demand for Karaganov **without** routing through Diesen host arc
- optional Tier A **voice profile** when operator promotes identity/voice work

## Growth rule

Grow by **density**, not by symmetry with multi-host shelves.

Next likely steps:

1. Optional **voice profile** when operator promotes Tier A identity work.
2. News-verify or synthesis passes on the **2023→2026** doctrine arc as a single continuity object.
