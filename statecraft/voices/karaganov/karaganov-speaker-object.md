# Karaganov speaker object

WORK only; not Record.

object_shape: single-helix

Purpose: define Sergey Karaganov as a first-class but light speaker object in the shelf. The current direct corpus is not a broad cross-host interview run. It is a Diesen-anchored doctrinal branch, plus wider reaction surfaces that treat "Karaganov doctrine" as a nuclear-escalation and Russian strategic-signaling problem.

## Core claim

Karaganov matters in the Codex as a carrier of Russian elite strategic and civilizational vocabulary:

- nuclear-threshold argument and deterrence restoration
- Europe as strategic-moral danger rather than partner
- Greater Eurasia, Siberization, and post-European Russian orientation
- the claim that escalation risk must be made vivid to restore Western fear of nuclear war
- strategic doctrine as a fusion of civilizational language, institutional memory, and hard coercive warning

This makes him most useful as an internal Russian-doctrine object, not as a neutral battlefield analyst or generic Russia commentator.
