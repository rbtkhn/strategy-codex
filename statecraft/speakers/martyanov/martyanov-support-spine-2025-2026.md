# Martyanov Support Spine 2025-2026

Purpose: explain why Martyanov is canonical now, why the shelf is still intentionally lighter than a month ladder, and what would justify later extension.

## Why Martyanov Is Canonical Now

Martyanov is no longer just residue because three things are already true:

- there is a real direct archive body across multiple hosts and over more than one year
- the same military-systems object is visibly stable across `Dialogue Works`, `Davis`, and `Diesen`
- live statecraft routing already needs him as the force-balance and strategic-collapse complement to Postol, McGovern, Barnes, and Johnson

That is enough for a canonical shelf.

## Why The Shelf Stays Lighter

The shelf should still remain lighter than a Johnson-style month ladder because:

- host arcs still own chronology more cleanly than a speaker-native month stack would
- the branch structure is real, but not evenly thick enough to justify a clean month-ladder collapse
- the strongest current use of the shelf is routing and branch distinction, not monthly synthesis

So the right current shape is:

- canonical speaker identity
- routing discipline
- compact provenance bench
- explicit helix doctrine
- support spine that blocks symmetry pressure

## What Would Justify Extension

Extend beyond this lighter grammar only if one or more of the following becomes materially true:

- the `Davis` and `Diesen` branches thicken enough to balance the current Dialogue Works dominance
- the notebook starts needing bounded Martyanov month support often enough that host-local entry is no longer the cleanest first-open surface
- the archive captures enough additional direct material that the branch hierarchy stabilizes without heavy reliance on host-arc inference

## Growth Rule

Grow this shelf by density, not by symmetry.

The next good extension would likely be a thicker raw-input bench or one bounded support note, not an automatic `2026-01` through `2026-05` ladder.
