WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: make the Crooke speaker object faster to route without duplicating host chronology or collapsing authored and interview source classes. This note answers a narrower question than the helix note: **which Crooke surface should the Codex open first for a given kind of Crooke use**.

## Core rule

Crooke does not reduce to one host lane, but he also does not need a fake all-purpose chronology inside the speaker folder. The efficient structure is:

- the speaker folder holds object identity;
- the authored forecast ledger holds Crooke's mechanism spine in his own voice;
- host-local speaker arcs hold the bounded interview transformations;
- the helix compares those interview transformations without erasing source-class discipline;
- the interview-appearances map groups the interview strand by host function;
- the interview month spines and interview arc-thread atlas densify the interview strand without replacing the authored ledger;
- the law thread isolates one real recurring topical lane inside the broader corpus.

So this note is the **speaker-side index** into the real Crooke surfaces.
