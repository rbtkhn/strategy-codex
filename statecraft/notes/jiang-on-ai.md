## Core claim

Jiang is the repo's **strongest repeat speaker** on AI as:

1. **Technically oversold** — supervised machine learning + branding (`neural network`, `deep learning`, `AI`), not thinking; ELIZA-style **persuasion and hallucination**.
2. **Financially unviable** — capex without returns → **public bailout** (Stargate, government-owned data centers).
3. **Surveillance end-state** — AI justified by **government database of everyone**; social control (schools, AI girlfriends, remove human autonomy).
4. **Metaphysically loaded** — OpenAI as **religion/empire**; **Stargate** as occult portal; semester ties AI to **occult + eschatology + imperial decline**.
5. **Geopolitically mis-framed (China strand)** — marketed **US–China AI race** is **theater**; partners on AGI/surveillance; **autocrats win**.

## Technical thesis

Primary source: [2026-05-12 / Game Theory #24: The AI Apocalypse](../source-archive/statecraft/2026-05-12/source-game-theory-24-the-ai-apocalypse-2026-05-12.md)

- **ELIZA** (1966) as ancestor — pattern-matching + human desire to believe
- **Back propagation** / facial recognition as the real mechanism — fancy names as **marketing and occult branding**
- **Edge cases** break the system → remove human driving, autonomy, diversity — `AI demands that we fundamentally restructure human society to benefit AI`

Verbatim anchors:

- `AI doesn't exist. What exists we call supervised supervised machine learning`

- `the AI is fundamentally an occult practice`

## Financial and bailout thesis

Sources: GT-24; [2026-06-10 / Founding Members #01](../source-archive/statecraft/2026-06-10/source-predictive-history-founding-members-01-livestream-2026-06-10.md); [2026-01-05 / Diesen × Jiang](../source-archive/statecraft/2026-01-05/source-diesen-jiang-predictions-2026-empire-rivalry-collapse-2026-01-05.md)

- **Altman + Sanders + Trump** = bailout coalition — `what Sam Altman really wants is for a public bailout`
- Retail/government absorb cost; OpenAI captures upside — FM-01: `unload the cost on retail investors on government on anyone except yourself`
- Diesen Jan-05: grand strategy ties **EV/AI minerals** and dollar pressure to Venezuela/Iran — AI as **macro lever**, not siloed tech sector

Verbatim anchors:

- `AI by itself can't make any money`
- `the government wants to create a database of everyone in the world`
- `Michael Burry … re AI boom resembles financial bubble` (FM-01 Q&A)
