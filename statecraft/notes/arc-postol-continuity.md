# Postol Arc

Postol's durable statecraft role is to convert prestige rhetoric, panic rhetoric, and strategic abstraction into arguments about mechanism.

He is the shelf to open when the notebook needs a speaker who can say:

- what a weapon can actually penetrate
- what an interceptor can actually stop
- what an air-defense system can actually see
- what a target can actually survive
- and which public claims collapse once the engineering assumptions are made explicit

That role is stable across hosts even when the host tone changes.

## Durable Cross-Host Role

Postol is not primarily a moral narrator, domestic-liability narrator, or broad civilizational narrator.

His cross-host role is narrower and more reliable:

- technical correction
- destructive-capacity realism
- deterrence mechanism
- engineering skepticism toward claims of decisive force

This makes him a clean complement to:

- [Barnes](../../README.md) for domestic liability and room-failure
- [Marandi](../../README.md) for Iranian legitimacy and red-line framing
- [Johnson](../../README.md) for process absurdity and elite incoherence
- [Pape](../../README.md) for coercion theory and strategy-level prediction

## Host Branches

The real Postol object is cross-host, but not flat.

- `Davis x Postol` is the clearest mature first-open branch.
  It sharpens strike feasibility, penetration claims, target survivability, and engineering constraint.
- `Diesen x Postol` is a real co-supporting branch.
  It translates technical facts into escalation horizon, deterrence consequence, and strategic-order danger.
- `Dialogue Works x Postol` is a real co-supporting branch.
  It surfaces missile-defense collapse, interception failure, and live Iran-war pressure in a hotter present-tense register.
