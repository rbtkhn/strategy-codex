## Why this guest run matters

Steve Jermy matters here less as a generic naval commentator than as a recurring **maritime-closure, energy-system, and recovery-lag** voice inside a host stream otherwise centered on military feasibility, bargaining pressure, and escalation management.

Inside the Davis stream, that makes the run especially useful when the notebook needs continuity between:

- blockade rhetoric and actual maritime closure geometry
- naval force talk and the practical problem of reopening Hormuz
- conflict escalation and diesel, fertilizer, and shipping-system knock-on effects
- short-term military moves and long-tail energy or food consequence

What makes this a real speaker arc is that the same host x guest shape keeps returning:

- Davis frames the crisis through military feasibility and escalation risk
- Jermy translates that crisis into shipping, energy, maritime chokepoints, and recovery timelines
- the lane repeatedly turns war argument into a question of operational closure geometry and systemic commercial consequence
