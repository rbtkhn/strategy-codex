## Stable baseline claims (verify before quotation-grade)

Claims repeated across multiple captures; **not** treated as verified fact until wire-checked.

1. **Wuhan model** (Pape): second-tier cities rebuild industrial heartland around university–cluster coupling; infrastructure ahead of demand.
2. **Integrated ecosystem** (Pape): AI + robotics + EV + solar + logistics in one stack (Xiaomi ~67s/vehicle cited by executives).
3. **~80% non-oil energy economy** (Pape, Redacted Apr-20) — executive-sourced; high verify priority.
4. **70% of Chinese economy AI-interconnected by 2030** (Escobar, May-21 Shanghai) — plan attribution; verify official source tier.
5. **China uses AI "productively" not as Western hype** (Crooke, Feb-20 Alkorshid) — interpretive; needs concrete examples in log.
6. **Jiang**: LLM = search + persuasion; Stargate = bailout blueprint; US–China **partners** on surveillance.

## Falsifiers / direction breaks

**Direction (trend tier):**

- Wire disconfirms **Wuhan model** or **integrated ecosystem** executive claims (Pape lane flip)
- Official source tier missing for **70% by 2030** (Escobar) — downgrade implementation acceleration
- Western AI capex shows **sustained profitability** without bailout — bubble lane reverses
- Palantir/CHMR chain **wire-contested** at Minab — surveillance acceleration read stalls
- Export-control regime **loosens** without sovereign-AI counter-program — access lane stalls

**Verify queue (claim tier):**

- Pape **67s/vehicle**, **Wuhan** cluster claims — field observation vs audited production stats
- Pape **80% non-oil** — definition of "energy economy"; coal/nuclear/solar breakdown
- Escobar **70% by 2030** — official plan citation (State Council, MIIT, etc.)
- Crooke **productive AI** — named programs, exports, or productivity metrics vs rhetoric
- Jiang **US–China surveillance partnership** — evidence tier beyond interpretive thesis
- Reporter **"I had no idea"** wave (Pape May-13) — track whether summit coverage shifts US elite perception
- **Minab causal AI role** — completed DoD investigating-officer report (beyond preliminary leaks)
- **Starobilsk / Lugansk AI** — second independent line on Palantir-guided drones (beyond Russian-side claims)
