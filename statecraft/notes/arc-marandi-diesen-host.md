# Diesen x Marandi speaker arc

Purpose: compact reuse note for the `Diesen x Marandi` run inside the Diesen stream. This is not a substitute for the raw-input files. It exists so future routing can quickly answer: what kind of Marandi lane Diesen elicits, which files matter most, and how this host-local run differs from the Davis and Dialogue Works Marandi lanes.

## Why this guest run matters

Seyed M. Marandi matters here less as a generic Iran voice than as a recurring strategic-order, escalation-horizon, and blockade-logic lane inside a host stream otherwise centered on civilizational transition, institutional sequence, and multipolar consequence.

Inside the Diesen stream, that makes the run especially useful when the notebook needs continuity between:

- Iranian retaliation logic and wider order transition
- blockade, bombing, or invasion talk and the claim that the war sits inside a broader anti-hegemonic shift
- immediate war threats and the diplomatic or strategic architecture around them
- Tehran-facing resolve and the larger question of what American or Israeli pressure can still plausibly produce

What makes this a real speaker arc is that the same host x guest shape keeps returning:

- Diesen frames the crisis through wider order consequence, strategic sequence, and escalation horizon
- Marandi translates that crisis into Iranian red lines, retaliation logic, and the failure of coercive expectation
- the lane repeatedly turns war talk into an argument about how local escalation feeds back into global structure

## Arc set

This lane is materially present on disk as a 2026 Iran-war run. It should be read as the Diesen-side strategic-order branch of the broader Marandi triad, distinct from:

- `davis x marandi` for operational limits and failed intimidation
- `alkorshid x marandi` for live crisis pressure and immediate regional signaling

## Orthogonality guide

Quick separation rule:

- `Diesen x Marandi` is the strategic-order and escalation-horizon lane.
- `Davis x Marandi` is the operational-limit and failed-intimidation lane.
- `Dialogue Works x Marandi` is the live crisis-pressure and regional-signaling lane.

Do not use this arc as a shortcut for force-feasibility triage or regime-adjacent live-pressure signaling. Its distinctive job is to translate Iranian retaliation logic and Hormuz pressure into wider order-transition consequence.

- Marandi is strongest here on red lines, retaliation logic, and strategic consequence, not on neutral tactical verification.
- This is now a real on-disk 2026 lane, but it remains distinct from the more operational Davis-side Marandi and the more pressurized Dialogue Works Marandi.
- Prefer this guest run when the notebook needs the Diesen-side strategic-order version of Marandi, not the cleaner Davis feasibility lane or the highest-pressure Dialogue Works lane.
