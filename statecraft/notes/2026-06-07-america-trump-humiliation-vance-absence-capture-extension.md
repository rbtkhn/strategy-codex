# America: Trump Humiliation, Vance Absence, Capture Extension - 2026-06-07

The goal is not a new America thesis. It tests whether Macgregor/McGovern/Freeman's June 6 capture read **fires in real time** when Trump claims control and Israel/GCC-enabled strikes proceed anyway.
