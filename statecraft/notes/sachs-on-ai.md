# Sachs on AI

## Purpose

Preserve Jeffrey Sachs's **bounded AI thesis** in the statecraft corpus: **war as AI weapons testbed** (Palantir, IDF cloud stack, Silicon Valley), **moral indictment of AI-targeted killing**, and **China productive AI / manufacturing integration** — distinct from bubble-occult (Jiang) and surveillance architecture (Weichert).

This is the canonical note-class prose home for this object. The Sachs shelf may point here; do not retain a parallel full-authority copy.

Verify tier: **analyst / interview**; Minab attribution = **Maven (Palantir-built) + Claude** per wire reporting — Sachs **“Palantir targeted Minab”** is **assertion tier** (overspecified vs completed DoD inquiry).

## Core claim

Sachs uses AI in three recurring ways:

1. **First AI wars** — live conflict as **test range** for Palantir, Anduril, cloud-for-IDF, Pentagon budgets.
2. **Moral–legal** — AI targeting of **schoolgirls** (Minab) as war crime; politicians treat killing as **video game**.
3. **China industrial AI** — open-source AI, robotics, factory integration as **legitimate modernization** vs US whining and absent industrial policy.

Sachs is **not** a technical AI theorist; he connects AI to **imperial finance**, **lobby capture**, and **manufacturing competition**.
