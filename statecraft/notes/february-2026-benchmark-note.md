# February 2026 benchmark note

## Purpose

Preserve `February 2026` as a reusable month-scale benchmark on the statecraft side so later sessions can distinguish a dense `bridge/setup month` from both a thin month and a mature crisis-core month.

This note is a benchmark note, not a watchlist and not a contradiction audit.

## Why February 2026 Matters

February 2026 is the clearest early `bridge/setup` benchmark month currently visible in the 2026 Iran-war archive.

It is the month where:

- the archive becomes dense enough to show a real emerging war-system object
- multiple speaker lanes are already active, but few have reached their later mature month shape
- escalation, Hormuz, legal-command, recognition, and settlement problems are all present in earlier, less consolidated form

So February is best treated as:

`a dense bridge/setup benchmark month`

not as:

`a thin month that needs repair`

and not as:

`a fully mature benchmark like March`

## Current Month Truth

The current February truth is:

- the month is already archive-dense at `91` source files across `28` captured days
- `Wilkerson` is genuinely thin inside the month, but that thinness is local to the Wilkerson shelf rather than proof that February is weak overall
- `Freeman`, `Ritter`, and `Crooke` all already read February as a `setup/bridge month`
- the archive rollup already shows a real multi-lane month rather than incidental early-year residue

That means February is not currently a live closure campaign month on local evidence. It should only be reopened as one if a finite, URL-backed contradiction queue later appears for a specific speaker-month object.

## What February Is Good For

February should be used for three kinds of later comparison.

### February vs March

February is the stronger benchmark for:

- setup density
- early war-object thickening
- bridge continuity before the archive becomes a mature crisis-core month

March is the stronger benchmark for:

- hot-war maturity
- multi-lane crisis compression
- later benchmark use without much reopening pressure

### February vs May

February is useful as the contrast case for:

- pre-mature escalation
- bridge lanes becoming real
- archive density before settlement architecture and negotiation pressure dominate

May is useful as the contrast case for:

- negotiation/settlement credibility
- underrepresentation judgment
- selective closure or backfill attention

### Month-shape benchmark

February is a strong test case for distinguishing:

- `dense but still bridge/setup`

from:

- `dense and fully mature`

and from:

- `thin and underrepresented`

That is its main month-scale value.

## What February Still Needs

February still has some bounded improvement paths, but they are not urgent closure work.

- month-scale guest-label normalization if later counting precision becomes important
- additional speaker-month truth surfaces if later comparison work needs more than the current Freeman / Ritter / Crooke / Wilkerson anchors
- repeated benchmark use before deciding whether any speaker-local contradiction queue is worth opening

What February does not currently need is a generic backfill campaign or a month-scale underrepresentation story.

## Use Rule

Use this note when the operator asks:

- `do february 2026`
- `what kind of month is February`
- `is February thin or just early`
- `how should February be compared to March or May`

Do not use this note as a watchlist, a missing-source queue, or a contradiction audit. Its job is to preserve February as a bridge/setup benchmark month with stable downstream uses.
