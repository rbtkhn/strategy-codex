# December 2025 benchmark note

## Purpose

Preserve `December 2025` as a reusable month-scale benchmark on the statecraft side so later sessions can distinguish a `late pre-2026 convergence month` from both the earlier 2025 setup months and the denser 2026 Iran-war core.

This note is a benchmark note, not a watchlist and not a contradiction audit.

## Why December 2025 Matters

December 2025 is the clearest late-2025 benchmark month currently visible in the statecraft archive.

It is the month where:

- multiple 2026-core voices are already active in meaningful density
- the archive is dense enough to read as a real month object rather than isolated year-end residue
- legitimacy collapse, alliance fracture, imperial overextension, and regional escalation pressure are all visibly converging before the 2026 hot-war thickening

So December is best treated as:

`a late pre-2026 convergence benchmark month`

not as:

`a thin month that needs repair`

and not as:

`a fully mature 2026 crisis-core benchmark`

## Current Month Truth

The current December truth is:

- the month is already archive-dense at `74` source files across `28` captured days
- `Mercouris` is the dominant continuity carrier, but December still has real multi-host breadth through `Dialogue Works`, `Judging Freedom`, `Daniel Davis Deep Dive`, `Diesen`, and `Neutrality Studies`
- `Wilkerson`, `Freeman`, `Sachs`, `Ritter`, `Martyanov`, and `Parsi` all have meaningful December presence, which makes the month feel like a real convergence field rather than a one-host archive swell
- the month already supports comparison work around pre-2026 system activation, but it does not yet carry the hotter, more unified Iran-war maturity of March 2026

That means December is not currently a live closure campaign month on local evidence. It should only be reopened as one if a finite, URL-backed contradiction queue later appears for a specific speaker-month object.

## What December Is Good For

December should be used for three kinds of later comparison.

### December vs January

December is the stronger benchmark for:

- late-year convergence
- already-visible multi-voice activation
- end-of-year system pressure before the 2026 opening continuity month

January is the stronger benchmark for:

- opening continuity/setup
- authored-heavy carryover into the new year
- dense but still earlier-stage 2026 shaping

### December vs March

December is useful as the contrast case for:

- convergence before full hot-war maturity
- a broad pre-core activation field
- dense multi-lane late-year pressure without the same crisis compression

March is useful as the contrast case for:

- early mature Iran-war density
- fully coherent escalation benchmark use
- stronger crisis-core compression

### Month-shape benchmark

December is a strong test case for distinguishing:

- `dense late pre-2026 convergence`

from:

- `opening continuity/setup`

and from:

- `dense mature hot-war benchmark`

That is its main month-scale value.

## What December Still Needs

December still has some bounded improvement paths, but they are not urgent closure work.

- month-scale guest-label normalization if later counting precision becomes important
- stronger month-specific speaker truth surfaces beyond the existing Wilkerson and Parsi/Sachs supporting anchors if later comparison work needs more December-native shelf objects
- repeated benchmark use before deciding whether any speaker-local contradiction queue is worth opening

What December does not currently need is a generic backfill campaign or a month-scale underrepresentation story.

Use this note when the operator asks:
