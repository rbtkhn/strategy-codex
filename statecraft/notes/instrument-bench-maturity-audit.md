# Transaction-Bench Maturity Audit


See [notes taxonomy](./README.md#thread-and-arc-canonical-draft).


Purpose: measure transaction-bench maturity across the four hardened statecraft lanes using the same bounded-entry, bridge, spine, and proof-anchor dashboard.

## Comparison Table

| lane | orientation coverage | bridge present | bridge ambiguity named | spine/support ring | local native exemplars | shared exemplars | native/share ratio | membrane risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Persia | `4/4` benches, `5/5` sections each | yes | yes | yes | 1 | 0 | `1:0` | low; bridge and support ring are strong, but the lane still relies heavily on adapter-conditioned support surfaces rather than a broader local bench |
| America | `4/4` benches, `5/5` sections each | yes | yes | yes | 1 | 2 | `1:2` | medium-low; membrane is now real, but shared America/Iran crisis objects still carry most of the proof inventory |
| China | `4/4` benches, `5/5` sections each | yes | yes | yes | 1 | 1 | `1:1` | low; lane has one native anchor and one shared strengthening exemplar without visible Iran-coupling |
| Russia | `4/4` benches, `5/5` sections each | yes | yes | yes | 1 | 1 | `1:1` | low-medium; lane now has a native anchor, but the bench still needs a second local family before broker logic and anti-overbinding logic are equally spent |

## Persia

Persia remains the clearest leverage, dignity, and recognition lane. Its membrane is structurally strong because the bridge ambiguity is lane-native and the proof anchor is local, but the lane still leans more on retrieval-conditioned support than on a broad inventory of local transactions.

## America

America now passes the maturity threshold because it has a real native proof anchor rather than only shared bilateral bundles. The remaining weakness is bench depth: the lane doctrine is stronger than its current spread of local exemplars.

## China

China is the cleanest balance so far between native and shared proof. The lane's stabilizer-versus-architecture split is genuinely China-native, and its local transaction anchor already expresses that logic without needing a Gulf or Iran object to explain itself.

## Russia

Russia now falsifies the pattern successfully rather than breaking it. The same hardening layer fits, but only because the bridge ambiguity stays Russia-native: brokered recognition versus anti-overbinding leverage. The bench is now mature enough to stand on its own, but it still needs one more local family before the membrane looks fully thick.

## Overall Result

The top-three recursive-learning pattern now survives across four distinct lane logics:

- Persia: recognition, dignity, leverage
- America: bounded command, burden-sharing, successor authority
- China: continuity, order, anti-coercion architecture
- Russia: strategic depth, brokered recognition, anti-overbinding leverage

The measurable result is no longer only narrative. All four lanes now have:

- full bounded-entry coverage on the four sub-benches
- a lane-native bridge ambiguity
- an explicit spine and support ring
- at least one local native proof anchor

The remaining maturity differences are now about bench depth, not about whether the recursive-learning pattern is real.
