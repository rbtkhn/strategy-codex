## Realism / Incentive Filter

- Does the alleged actor benefit more than it loses? China benefits from pressure and legal ambiguity, but loses if inspection pressure is classified as blockade and triggers premature coalition containment.
- Is the target something the actor still depends on? China still depends on route continuity, commercial predictability, and avoidance of a war clock that damages development legitimacy.
- Is the act consistent with demonstrated behavior, or only with attributed capability? The likely pressure form is inspection, customs, quarantine notice, and maritime ambiguity rather than open declaration of blockade.
- Accident / negligence / private actor / third-party alternative: collision, overzealous local enforcement, insurance panic, or reactive military signaling may harden the object faster than Beijing intends.
- Evidence needed to distinguish intent from exploitation: vessel categories, inspection notices, exclusion rules, customs authority, coast guard / PLA command separation, insurance reaction, and hotline use.
- Reclassified premise if needed: even absent declared blockade, the object becomes a sovereignty crisis once inspection pressure can no longer be distinguished from denial of maritime access.

## Historical Continuity

- Current government: party center, State Council, foreign ministry, customs, coast guard, PLA, ports, and maritime regulators.
- Current state: China as a continuity-seeking continental-maritime power trying to preserve unity and leverage without owning the chaos of open war.
- Predecessor institutions: tribute and coastal control memories, customs authority, strategic ambiguity, bureaucratic standardization, and crisis signaling through administrative classification.
- Geography: Taiwan sits inside the first-island-chain and maritime chokepoint environment where insurance, shipping, and military signaling interact quickly.
- Civilizational memory: order against chaos, unity, humiliation memory, and restoration through disciplined control rather than theatrical overreach.
- Possible successor authority: any successor Chinese authority would still need unity claims, route continuity, and a way to pressure Taiwan without sacrificing development legitimacy.
- What survives regime or constitutional change: the Chinese need to preserve unity pressure while avoiding visible disorder and premature containment.

## Head-Of-State Carrier Check

- Required authority carrier: party center, maritime and customs agencies, foreign ministry, PLA, coast guard, and port / shipping regulators.
- What this carrier can sign credibly: inspection categories, hotlines, safety notice, customs review mechanism, and commercial continuity language.
- What this carrier can implement credibly: vessel classification, bounded inspection, insurer signaling, incident review, and deconfliction channels.
- What this carrier cannot concede without breaking legitimacy: Taiwan-status recognition, public blockade retreat framed as surrender, or uncontrolled inspection chaos that humiliates state authority.
- Succession / continuity risk: if maritime pressure outruns party-state discipline, local or military carriers can force visible ownership faster than Beijing wants.
- Is the carrier expressing the state, capturing the state, or failing to carry the state? The main risk is that military or administrative pressure starts capturing strategic timing from the quieter stabilizer logic.

## State Interest

Preserve leverage over Taiwan without creating shipping panic, public disorder, or coalition hardening that damages Chinese development continuity.

## Leverage / Constraint

- Leverage being spent: maritime proximity, customs claims, market weight, port and shipping influence, and strategic ambiguity.
- Constraint that limits the leverage: shipping panic, insurer reaction, coalition formation, and the risk that visible coercion damages the stabilizer role China prefers.
- Volatility note: China rises through disciplined pressure and falls through overexposed maritime ownership.

## Richelieu Validation

- Institutional carrier: party-state, coast guard, customs, maritime safety, port operators, insurers, and military deconfliction organs.
- Administrative / fiscal / legal / military capacity: vessel categories, published guidance, inspection discipline, hotline use, port and insurer coordination, and bounded signaling.
- Continuity risk: military escalation, market panic, or insurer withdrawal can collapse the distinction between inspection and blockade.
- Ideology or faction pressure: nationalist pressure may reward visible ownership even when it weakens the long game.
- Long-pressure burden: the system must preserve pressure long enough to matter without forcing Beijing to become the visible owner of every maritime incident.

## Bismarck Validation

- Limited aim: inspection discipline and political pressure, not forced war clock.
- Actor to isolate: voices pushing immediate blockade classification or prestige escalation.
- Actor to reassure, restrain, or keep neutral: commercial carriers, insurers, regional states, and actors tempted to internationalize the classification too quickly.
- Alliance geometry: preserve pressure on Taiwan while avoiding a regional coalition built around shipping panic and overclassification.
- Restraint after advantage: if inspection pressure gains leverage, do not convert the opening into triumphal ownership theater.
- Off-ramp: inspection review, incident hotline, safe-passage categories, and bounded public language below blockade recognition.

## Instrument Text

Draft clause:

> China shall frame any Taiwan-adjacent inspection regime as time-bounded maritime safety, customs review, and commercial-risk management rather than as blockade ownership. Named vessel categories, incident hotlines, inspection procedures, and commercial safe-passage guidance shall be published in a way that preserves pressure and administrative ambiguity without requiring foreign recognition of blockade status. Any escalation beyond those categories shall trigger review, consultation, and renewed political authorization before the regime hardens into a denial-of-access object.
