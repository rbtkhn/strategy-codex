# June 18, 2026 MOU — Hormuz governance vs armistice seam

## Purpose

Preserve **Pape Day 60** as a bounded **mechanism note**: official MOU Pt. 5 converts de-escalation from **permanent settlement** fiction into **temporary armistice** with a **governance deadline** on who administers the Strait of Hormuz after sixty days.

The aim is not to re-synthesize J17 or J18 dailies. The aim is to stop **flattening** Hormuz into “open vs closed” when the load-bearing object is **administration, fees, and institutional leverage** after the toll-free window.

## Shared object

Iran–US **14-point MOU** signed ~17–18 Jun 2026. **Pt. 5:** Iran arranges **60-day toll-free** commercial passage (Persian Gulf ↔ Sea of Oman); then **dialogue with Oman** (+ Gulf littoral states) on **future administration and maritime services** in Hormuz.

## Core claim

**Armistice, not settlement.** The sixty-day **no-charge** language (present in **official** text, absent from early Bloomberg draft per Pape) makes the agreement a **bridge** with a **built-in second crisis**:

| Phase | Object | Crisis grammar |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 0–60** | Restore flow; demining; insurance easing | **Closure** crisis (original war trigger) |
| **Day 60+** | Negotiate **who governs** Hormuz services/fees | **Governance** crisis — institutional influence |

**Washington trap (Pape):** After free passage expires, **rejecting** Iranian fees/administration → immediate **re-confrontation**; **accepting** → **ratify** survival-created **leverage**. Trump’s “walk away if talks fail” rhetoric **conflicts** with agreement structure.

**Historical frame (Pape):** Trade-route control → political power (British sea lanes, Panama, Suez). Hormuz ≈ **one-fifth** globally traded oil — even **limited** corridor authority extends Iranian influence beyond the Gulf.

**Stage IV reinforcement:** Official MOU **strengthens** (does not weaken) Pape’s Stage IV read — survival → leverage → **institutional** conversion, not merely economic relief.

Upon signing, Iran **best efforts** for **60 days only** toll-free passage; traffic starts immediately, **instated within 30 days** after demining/obstacle removal; Iran conducts dialogue with **Oman** on **future administration and maritime services** in Hormuz, with other littoral states per international law.

## Falsifiers

1. **Hormuz administration deal** reached **before** day 60 with **US/GCC buy-in** → governance trap **defused**; Pape escalation-trap read **weakens** for that branch.
2. **MOU extended** by mutual consent (Pt. 3) **without** resolving administration → trap **postponed**, not resolved.
3. **US walk-away** + **re-closure** at day 60 **without** Iranian breach → Washington chose confrontation over ratification — trap branch **B** (Pape) **activated**.
4. **Iran imposes fees/administration** with **continued traffic** and US **acquiescence** → Stage IV **institutional influence** read **supported**.
5. **60-day window expires** with **no** traffic normalization (demining lag) → ops-lag thesis (Johnson/Aguilar) **overweights** governance clock — hold both tiers.
