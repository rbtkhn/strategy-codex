Across the canonical run, three doctrinal families recur:

1. **Nuclear threshold / deterrence restoration** — world-war framing; conventional Europe strikes before limited nuclear; Germany as prime target in Karaganov's rhetoric; compatriot pressure for fuller deterrence use.
2. **Anti-Europe strategic orientation** — Europe as source of evil; break with Europe as strategic center; pivot to east/south / Siberia.
3. **Civilizational / Eurasian identity** — spiritual revival, post-West Russian orientation, Greater Eurasia as statecraft vocabulary.
