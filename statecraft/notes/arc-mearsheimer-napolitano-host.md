Napolitano does not make Mearsheimer more tactical than Davis or more architectonic than Diesen. He makes him more immediate about:

- what Washington has already lost
- how executive sequence control collapses once coercion fails

- how anti-war realism looks when filtered through institutional incoherence rather than through bargaining geometry alone

That is why this lane is real reinforcement rather than a third helix strand: it owns a distinct compression of Mearsheimer, but not a fully equal cognition-stream structure.
