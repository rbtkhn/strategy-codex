## Orthogonality guide

This file should be read as the **live-escalation intelligence-dissent** McGovern branch, not as the full McGovern object and not as the order-transition version of him.

Quick separation rule:

- `Alkorshid x McGovern` = present-tense Iran/Russia escalation, intelligence dissent under live pressure, and imperial unreality warnings
- [arc-mcgovern-diesen-host.md](arc-mcgovern-diesen-host.md) = arms-control decay, NATO consequence, and strategic-restraint failure at order-transition altitude

If the notebook needs a former insider warning that a live crisis has outrun official comprehension, route here. If it needs order-level arms-control consequence, Iranian diplomatic positioning, or intelligence-political explanation without McGovern's dissident register, route to the neighboring branches instead.

## Why this guest run matters

Ray McGovern matters here less as a generic antiwar commentator than as a recurring **imperial-unreality, intelligence-dissent, and Iran-war warning** voice inside a host stream otherwise centered on rapid escalation, regional breakdown, and live-pressure synthesis.

Inside the Alkorshid stream, that makes the run especially useful when the Codex needs continuity between:

- Iran-war escalation and U.S. strategic blindness
- Russia and Iran as linked theaters rather than isolated crises
- intelligence warning and the moral-political failure of official narratives
- imperial overreach and the shrinking room before catastrophic miscalculation

What makes this a real speaker arc is that the same host x guest shape keeps returning:

- Alkorshid frames the crisis through live pressure and widening fronts
- McGovern translates that pressure into warnings about intelligence failure, elite unreality, and what state power still refuses to see
- the lane repeatedly turns war headlines into a question of whether the system has already lost contact with its own limits
