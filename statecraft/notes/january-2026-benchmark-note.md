# January 2026 benchmark note

## Purpose

Preserve `January 2026` as a reusable month-scale benchmark on the statecraft side so later sessions can distinguish an `opening continuity/setup month` from both a denser bridge month and a mature crisis-core month.

This note is a benchmark note, not a watchlist and not a contradiction audit.

## Why January 2026 Matters

January 2026 is the clearest `opening continuity/setup` benchmark month currently visible in the 2026 statecraft archive.

It is the month where:

- the archive already has real breadth and density, but much of that density is still pre-mature in form
- authored warning spines, early host lanes, and opening strategic frames become visible before the later February and March thickening
- multiple future crisis objects appear in seed form without yet consolidating into the hotter benchmark shapes of later months

So January is best treated as:

`an opening continuity/setup benchmark month`

not as:

`a thin month that needs repair`

and not as:

`a mature benchmark like March`

## Current Month Truth

The current January truth is:

- the month is already archive-dense at `101` source files across `31` captured days
- `Wilkerson` is thin inside the month, but that thinness is local to the Wilkerson shelf rather than proof that January is weak overall
- `Freeman`, `Ritter`, and `Crooke` already read January as a `continuity/setup` or `continuity carryover` month
- the archive rollup already shows a real multi-lane opening month rather than scattered residue

That means January is not currently a live closure campaign month on local evidence. It should only be reopened as one if a finite, URL-backed contradiction queue later appears for a specific speaker-month object.

## What January Is Good For

January should be used for three kinds of later comparison.

### January vs February

January is the stronger benchmark for:

- opening continuity
- authored-heavy setup
- seed-stage lane visibility before denser month-shape consolidation

February is the stronger benchmark for:

- dense bridge/setup
- clearer early war-object thickening
- stronger month-scale cross-lane pressure before full maturity

### January vs March

January is useful as the contrast case for:

- continuity carryover
- pre-mature crisis shaping
- how a month can be dense without yet being a mature crisis-core month

March is useful as the contrast case for:

- hot-war maturity
- stable benchmark use
- clearly consolidated escalation and bargaining pressure

### Month-shape benchmark

January is a strong test case for distinguishing:

- `dense but still opening continuity/setup`

from:

- `dense bridge/setup`

and from:

- `dense and mature`

That is its main month-scale value.

## What January Still Needs

January still has some bounded improvement paths, but they are not urgent closure work.

- month-scale guest-label normalization if later counting precision becomes important
- additional speaker-month truth surfaces if later comparison work needs more than the current Freeman / Ritter / Crooke / Wilkerson anchors
- repeated benchmark use before deciding whether any speaker-local contradiction queue is worth opening

What January does not currently need is a generic backfill campaign or a month-scale underrepresentation story.

Use this note when the operator asks:
