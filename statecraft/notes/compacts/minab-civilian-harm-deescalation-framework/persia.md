## Realism / Incentive Filter

- Does the alleged actor benefit more than it loses? Iran benefits from preserving Minab as a legitimacy claim, but loses if the claim becomes a permanent barrier to every channel.
- Is the target something the actor still depends on? Iran depends on diplomatic recognition and sanctions relief, so humanitarian dignity must be converted into channel terms rather than unlimited escalation.
- Accident / negligence / private actor / third-party alternative: Leave attribution review to an inquiry mechanism; do not make the first diplomatic step depend on full legal finality.
- Evidence needed: strike records, targeting logs, casualty documentation, school location data, and independent humanitarian access.
- Reclassified premise if needed: If intent is not established, the transaction still holds as a civilian-harm recognition and protected-channel mechanism.

- Current state: Iran as a resource-secure, sanctions-hardened, Hormuz-adjacent coercive center.

## Head-Of-State Carrier Check

- Required authority carrier: Supreme Leader structure, president / government, foreign ministry, Supreme National Security Council, and military command.
- What this carrier can sign credibly: inquiry mechanism, protected channel, humanitarian access, phased transit relief, and sanctions sequencing.
- What this carrier cannot concede without breaking legitimacy: treating Minab as irrelevant, accepting regime-change language, or lifting leverage before dignity is acknowledged.
- Succession / continuity risk: if internal factions read de-escalation as erasure of Minab, the carrier loses room to bargain.

## Pattern / Narrative Conversion

- Civilizational pattern or narrative: martyrdom and violated innocence.
- Authority: named Iranian state carriers authorize the channel and inquiry.
- Restraint: Iran pauses expansion of retaliation while protected channels and humanitarian mechanisms operate.
- Settlement: Minab-class civilian harm is handled through review, memorial dignity, and non-repetition guarantees.

## State Interest

Iran's durable interest is to preserve sovereign dignity after Minab while converting coercive leverage into recognition, sanctions relief, and a regional mechanism that makes repetition less likely.

## Leverage / Constraint

- Leverage being spent: Minab legitimacy, Hormuz control, retaliation capacity, and Russia / China / Oman channel depth.
- Constraint that limits the leverage: maximalizing the wound can block the very settlement that would convert Iranian endurance into durable gains.
- Volatility note: Pape-style coercive leverage favors Iran now, but Mearsheimer-style balancing pressure will rise if Iran appears to weaponize civilian grief without a settlement path.

## Richelieu Validation

- Institutional carrier: Supreme National Security Council, foreign ministry, judiciary / humanitarian documentation body, and mediator secretariat.
- Capacity: casualty documentation, channel discipline, calibrated retaliation, and sanctions-relief verification.
- Continuity risk: domestic legitimacy may fracture if the review mechanism looks like a burial of the issue.
- Ideology or faction pressure: resistance rhetoric can outbid diplomatic prudence.
- Long-pressure burden: Iran must sustain dignity without making settlement impossible.

## Bismarck Validation

- Limited aim: secure recognition mechanism, channel protection, non-repetition language, and first-step relief; do not demand total historical judgment before talks begin.
- Actor to isolate: spoilers seeking to use Minab either to justify renewed U.S. escalation or to make Iranian de-escalation dishonorable.
- Actor to reassure, restrain, or keep neutral: Oman, China, Russia, Gulf states, and domestic Iranian constituencies.
- Alliance geometry: use Russia / China / Oman as guarantor witnesses without surrendering Iranian agency.
- Restraint after advantage: if civilian-harm recognition begins, Iran keeps retaliation calibrated and preserves the mediator path.
- Off-ramp: inquiry plus protected channel plus initial sanctions / blockade relief.

## Instrument Text

Iran shall maintain calibrated defensive rights, but shall sequence any transit easing or military restraint with humanitarian access, memorial dignity, non-repetition language, and visible sanctions or blockade relief.

- Revisit trigger: mediator proposal, public Iranian statement on Minab, humanitarian access offer, blockade relief, or new civilian-harm allegation.
- Scoreboard movement that would require review: Pape leverage falls if Hormuz leverage no longer bites; Mearsheimer risk rises if balancing pressure isolates Iran.
