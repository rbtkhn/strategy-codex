## Classification Discipline Patch

Nothing in this arrangement shall be understood as recognition, waiver, transfer, suspension, or alteration of any party's legal, political, maritime, commercial, or security position.

The arrangement is limited to operational deconfliction, commercial transit continuity, incident prevention, and insurance-stabilizing procedures for named maritime movements.

Inspection, quarantine, customs notice, transit reassurance, and blockade shall remain distinct classifications. No party shall treat one category as automatically proving another without sustained interdiction, notice, failed consultation, and review.

Public communications should describe the arrangement as an incident-prevention and commercial-continuity measure. Parties should avoid blockade, recognition, surrender, enforcement coalition, or reunification-success language in connection with the arrangement.
