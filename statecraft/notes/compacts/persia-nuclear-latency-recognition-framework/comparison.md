# Iran Nuclear Latency - Four-Lane Comparison

Purpose: compare the four national transactions without collapsing latency, weaponization, recognition, and restraint into one generic nuclear deal.

## Orthogonality

| lane | civilization pressure | empire drive | regulatory statecraft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [America](america.md) | lawful nonproliferation, allied confidence, domestic authority | control sanctions, inspections, military deterrence, and coalition alignment | contain latency without hidden regime-change pressure |
| [Russia](russia.md) | sovereignty memory and resistance to coercive precedent | weaken unilateral U.S. control over thresholds | support recognition while preventing proliferation chaos |
| [China](china.md) | order, competence, energy stability, anti-chaos legitimacy | secure energy, finance, trade, and anti-coercion alternatives | preserve Iran as counterpressure without owning nuclear disorder |
| [Persia](persia.md) | dignity, sovereignty, resistance, and no humiliation | preserve deterrence, latency, recognition, and sanctions relief | trade threshold ambiguity for relief and assurances without surrender |

## Shared Settlement Spine

A workable framework needs six shared features:

1. Recognition of Iran's civilian nuclear rights.
2. Renewable non-weaponization commitment.
3. Enrichment or threshold limits with non-humiliating verification.
4. Phased sanctions relief with reversal rules that are not purely unilateral.
5. Non-regime-change and non-attack assurances.
6. Urgent consultation channel before snapback, strike, or breakout response.

## Main Tensions

| tension | why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| America wants containment without legitimizing threshold status | The framework fails if Washington treats recognition as defeat. |
| Iran wants dignity without surrendering leverage | The framework fails if Tehran cannot distinguish restraint from humiliation. |
| China wants stability without ownership | The framework fails if Beijing becomes guarantor of every inspection dispute. |
| Russia wants anti-coercion precedent without chaos | The framework fails if Moscow enables weaponization that damages wider Eurasian stability. |

## Drafting Implication

The first serious instrument should not reopen maximal JCPOA nostalgia or demand unilateral disarmament. It should define renewable latency restraint: civilian-rights recognition, non-weaponization commitment, inspection access, phased relief, non-attack assurances, and a consultation channel before snapback or strike.

## Revisit Triggers

- Iran changes inspection access or enrichment posture.
- U.S. or Israeli attack threats escalate.
- Russia / China jointly propose verification or guarantees.
- Gulf states demand inclusion in the framework.
- Pape coercive-center and Mearsheimer structural-realist scoreboards diverge sharply.
