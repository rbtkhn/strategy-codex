## Crooke trigger (process)

Beirut Hezbollah-target assassination (Sunday) is what **finally** moved the MOU over the line Trump wanted — ironic because it could have undermined the initiative. Substance (nuclear, etc.) deferred 60 days. McGovern treats Crooke as holding a **draft** with phased sticking points; Sachs and Pape both stress **no published English/Farsi text** yet.

## Sachs rupture (politics)

JCPOA was better; current frame returns toward **status quo ante Hormuz** at best. Encouraging signal: **Israeli right meltdown** + Trump expletives at Netanyahu — not charade. Staged **$12B / ~$25B** release pool (verify against MOU text). **Bennett** quoted: regime-change war resumes on Israeli government change. Macron G7 partial Hormuz open headline same day.

## Pape leverage (structure)

Iran negotiating to **win more**, not stop losing. SPR draw (~348M → operational floor 270–300M) makes **time favor Tehran**; **August** more dangerous than June. Strategic prize: **Gulf states nudging US forces out** — not permanent high oil. MOU conditions (via deputy FM): **$12B**, **end Israeli ops in Lebanon**, **Gulf force reduction**, Hormuz fees at 60 days.

## Pape × McGovern — Lebanon hinge (same object)

**Object:** Israeli military operations in Lebanon as **tripwire** for US–Iran MOU survival.

| Dimension | McGovern (interview) | Pape (essay) |
|-----------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Mechanism** | If Netanyahu resumes slaughter → Iran retaliates → US begs Iran not to retaliate (repeat cycle) | If Israel continues Hezbollah strikes **without** Iranian retaliation, MOU may survive; if Lebanon reignites, **everything else secondary** |
| **US domestic veto** | Adelson / Lindsey Graham bloc won't accept Israel leaving Lebanon; Trump must ask "hold 60 days" | (Out of scope — structural indicators only) |
| **Iran condition** | Iran "for some strange reason" cares intensely — Lebanon is key to economy + deal | Explicit **precondition**: complete end to Israeli military ops in Lebanon before long-term process |
| **Trump move** | Pitch to Zionist donors: economy collapsing, got nuclear promise, **don't stir Lebanon** | Falsifier #1 in Pape's weekly watch list |
| **Crooke link** | (McGovern cites Crooke draft; doesn't re-litigate Beirut trigger) | Beirut/Hezbollah ops implied in "continues striking" |

**Convergence:** Both name Lebanon as the **lowest-friction unravel path** — not Hormuz wording, not Geneva choreography.

**Disproportion:** McGovern column is **US domestic politics + personality** (birthday, Adelson phone call). Pape column is **indicator logic** (binary: ops continue vs reignite). Use McGovern for **who blocks Trump on Lebanon**; Pape for **whether the MOU survives the week**.

**Non-contradiction:** McGovern's "Iran retaliates" path is the **reignite** branch Pape warns collapses the framework.

## Falsifiers

- Published MOU text shows **no** Lebanon condition → downgrade Pape deputy-FM row; keep Crooke trigger as episodic only
- Sustained Israeli Lebanon ops **with** Iranian retaliation **and** MOU still signed/enforced → McGovern "US begs Iran" loop weakens
- SPR stabilizes / Hormuz insurance normalizes while Lebanon hot → Pape leverage thesis needs revision
- Israeli withdrawal from Lebanon begins → tripwire defused; watch Pape indicators 2–4 instead
