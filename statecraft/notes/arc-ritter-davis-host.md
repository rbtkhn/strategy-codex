Purpose: compact reuse note for the `Davis x Ritter` run inside the Davis stream. This is not a substitute for the raw-input files. It exists so future routing can quickly answer: what kind of Ritter lane Davis elicits, which files matter most, and how this host-local run differs from the Diesen and Dialogue Works Ritter lanes.

## Why this guest run matters

Scott Ritter matters here less as a generic military commentator than as a recurring operational-failure and war-feasibility voice inside a host stream already centered on military practicality, escalation pressure, and policy risk.

Inside the Davis stream, that makes the run especially useful when the notebook needs continuity between:

- force posture and what the United States can actually sustain
- blockade or bombing claims and the shrinking room for meaningful military leverage
- war rhetoric and the claim that no coherent Iran war plan exists
- stockpiles, skies, and basing pressure versus the official narrative of control

What makes this a real speaker arc is that the same host x guest shape keeps returning:

- Davis frames the crisis through feasibility, force deployment, and military consequence
- Ritter translates that crisis into hard operational limits, false confidence, and the collapse of practical war aims
- the lane repeatedly turns escalation talk into an argument about what the U.S. military can no longer realistically impose

## Orthogonality guide

Quick separation rule:

- `Davis x Ritter` is the operational-feasibility and material-limit lane.
- `Diesen x Ritter` is the escalation-horizon and cross-theater consequence lane.
- `Dialogue Works x Ritter` is the maximum live-war-pressure and loss-of-control lane.
- `Napolitano x Ritter` is the legal-pressure and command-absurdity lane.

Do not use this arc as a shortcut for collapse-temperature rhetoric, Europe/Gulf escalation horizon, or juridical sharpening. Its distinctive job is to test whether the military means, stockpiles, skies, and blockade posture can still produce the stated political objective.
