# Davis x Marandi speaker arc

WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: compact reuse note for the `Davis x Marandi` run inside the Davis stream. This is not a substitute for the raw-input files. It exists so future routing can quickly answer: what kind of Marandi lane Davis elicits, which files matter most, and how this host-local run differs from the Diesen and Dialogue Works Marandi lanes.

## Why this guest run matters

Seyed M. Marandi matters here less as a generic Iran voice than as a recurring operational-limits, war-feasibility, and political-resilience countervoice inside a host stream otherwise centered on military practicality, escalation pressure, and U.S. force-posture realism.

Inside the Davis stream, that makes the run especially useful when the notebook needs continuity between:

- military pressure and Iranian political staying power
- escalation rhetoric and what force can no longer realistically recover
- blockade, bombing, or intimidation talk and the failure of coercive leverage
- U.S. operational options and the claim that the war is already moving beyond Washington's preferred geometry

What makes this a real speaker arc is that the same host x guest shape keeps returning:

- Davis frames the crisis through force feasibility, escalation choices, and operational risk
- Marandi translates that crisis into Iranian resilience, retaliatory logic, and the limits of intimidation
- the lane repeatedly turns war talk into an argument about what coercion cannot achieve once escalation has hardened, local resolve has consolidated, and political outcomes are no longer easily shaped from Washington
