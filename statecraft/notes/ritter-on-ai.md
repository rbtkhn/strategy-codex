# Ritter on AI

## Purpose

Preserve Scott Ritter's **bounded AI thesis** in the statecraft corpus: **AI-assisted targeting without law-of-war scrub** as a **war-crime and kill-chain** problem — not bubble metaphysics (Jiang) or China industrial scaling (Pape).

This is the canonical note-class prose home for this object. The Ritter shelf may point here for continuity; do not retain a parallel full-authority copy on the shelf.

Verify tier: **analyst / investigative scenario** on Minab (Backpack Girls cites Anthropic/Claude — **speculative scenario** per source frontmatter); interview claims on Palantir/Lugansk are **assertion tier** until independently corroborated.

## Core claim

Ritter is the repo's **strongest ex-targeteer voice** on AI in war:

1. **AI is not intelligence** — parameter-driven database sifting; useful **jump-start**, not substitute for human discrimination.
2. **The failure mode is institutional** — Hegseth **dissolved CHMR** (civilian harm mitigation); **maximum lethality** replaced human target scrub.
3. **The mechanism is stale-data + dimension matching** — Claude/Palantir-style systems match **building dimensions** and **old order-of-battle labels**, miss **2013 school carve-out** and **no-strike lists**.
4. **The outcome is war crimes** — Minab girls' school (Iran); Lugansk teachers' college (Ukraine) as **AI-generated targets** outside normal checks.

Ritter does **not** treat AI as US–China race, occult religion, or macro bubble — he treats it as **who gets killed when the targeteer is removed**.

## Institutional thesis (CHMR → Claude)

- Claude recommended **Shajareh Tayyebeh** school — past affiliation with abandoned **Sayyid al-Shuhada** / Asef Missile Brigade compound
- **Feb 28, 2026**: four TLAMs; structure-dimension matching; BDA camera **re-strike** on collapsed school during survivor assembly in prayer room
