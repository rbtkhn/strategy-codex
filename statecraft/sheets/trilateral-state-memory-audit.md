# Trilateral State-Memory Audit


Use this sheet to audit whether the Russia / China / Iran state-memory architecture works as a machine: civilization stores state memory, state carries present authority, objects transmit signals, and transactions test whether authority can become settlement.

This is an audit surface, not a merge surface. It may recommend recursive update candidates, but it does not rewrite lane objects, transactions, CIV-MEM, or PH-CIV.

## Evidence Notes

### Russia

- **Memory:** [Russia state memory](../russia/civilization/objects/state-memory.md) defines post-Soviet continuity after collapse, encirclement, recognition loss, and security-depth restoration.
- **Current carriers:** [Putin](../russia/state/putin.md) is the visible presidential / security-state carrier; [Security Council](../russia/state/security-council.md) is the institutional continuity surface.
- **Transmitter:** [Lavrov](../russia/state/objects/lavrov.md) translates presidential and security-state language, but does not prove institutional carrying capacity by himself.
- **Transaction utility:** strongest for mediator, guarantee, energy, sanctions, Ukraine / Iran linkage, and recognition-formula tests.
- **Friction watch:** Russia can exploit disruption, but overbinding to Iran or dependence on Chinese demand / pricing can reduce optionality.

### China

- **Memory:** [China state memory](../china/civilization/objects/state-memory.md) defines continuity civilization through absorption, standardization, mandate performance, party-state fusion, development legitimacy, and anti-disorder control.
- **Current carriers:** [Xi](../china/state/xi.md) is the visible top-leader carrier; [party-state](../china/state/party-state.md) is the implementation carrier across party organs, State Council, PLA, banks, firms, ports, insurers, and provinces.
- **Transmitter:** [Wang Yi](../china/state/objects/wang-yi.md) transmits diplomatic architecture, anti-disorder language, sanctions resistance, sovereignty doctrine, and quiet mediation.
- **Transaction utility:** strongest for Taiwan maritime access, Hormuz energy continuity, Egypt / Suez / bread, Pakistan command reassurance, and energy absorber threshold testing.
- **Friction watch:** China wants stabilizer influence without public ownership of disorder it cannot command.

### Iran

- **Memory:** [Iran state memory](../persia/civilization/objects/state-memory.md) defines sovereignty under pressure, dignity, siege endurance, 1953 guarantee-distrust, Iran-Iraq martyrdom, sanctions, and compute-siege autonomy.
- **Current carriers:** [Authority structure](../persia/state/authority-structure.md), [Supreme Leader](../persia/state/supreme-leader.md), [president / government](../persia/state/president-government.md), and [IRGC](../persia/state/irgc.md) must align for a settlement to carry.
- **Transmitter:** [Araghchi](../persia/state/objects/araghchi.md) translates sanctions relief, nuclear rights, dignity, transit leverage, and regional architecture.
- **Transaction utility:** strongest for Hormuz, nuclear latency, protected channel, Minab civilian harm, and Iran-local recognition transit.
- **Friction watch:** Iran can monetize leverage, but humiliation lock, martyrdom-as-veto, or IRGC/security implementation failure can block settlement.

## Trilateral Matrix

| zone | Russia | China | Iran | audit implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sanctions resistance | Uses sanctions endurance and alternative channels to weaken Western coercion. | Builds workarounds only when they protect trade, energy, finance, and supply chains. | Needs relief sequencing that does not require surrendering leverage first. | Strong shared interest, but the mechanism must not make China visibly liable or Iran disposable. |
| Energy continuity | Can supply, mediate, and bargain, but risks dependence on Chinese demand and pricing. | Wants energy stability and insurance / shipping continuity above symbolic confrontation. | Uses Hormuz, exports, and latency as bargaining leverage. | Alignment is real when energy mechanisms exist; otherwise it is only a near-fit signal. |
| U.S. coercion resistance | Seeks recognition and optionality against Western pressure. | Seeks reduced U.S. reach without premature visible confrontation. | Seeks non-regime-change, dignity, and relief. | Shared anti-coercion frame needs different public wording for each lane. |
| Alternative architecture | Can provide mediation, guarantees, and great-power recognition language. | Can provide forums, finance, insurance, infrastructure, and quiet pressure. | Can provide restraint, transit reopening, and regional legitimacy if dignity is preserved. | Strongest candidate for a reusable trilateral instrument family. |
| Overbinding risk | Russia may become hostage to Iran's escalation or China's pricing. | China may inherit policing burdens or disorder ownership. | Iran may become an instrument of others' anti-U.S. leverage without recognition. | Any trilateral brief must name who controls timing, price, and exit. |
| Sovereignty / recognition | Wants security-depth recognition without saying sphere of influence. | Wants internal sovereignty claims not internationalized. | Wants recognition as actor, not object. | Same word, three different meanings; use lane-specific phrasing. |

## Audit Use

Before drafting a trilateral memo, objection matrix, or transaction-use brief:

1. Open the lane with the weakest score category.
2. Name the carrier that must make the settlement stick.
3. Confirm the transmitter is not being treated as final authority.
4. Route through the transaction plateau before proposing a new object.
5. Stage recursive candidates only through lane-local updates.
