# Civilization And Empire Cycle

Purpose: make the Civilization and Empire helix diagnostic. Use the cycle to test whether a statecraft lane is preserving civilization, amplifying it, degrading it, or restoring it.

## Core Cycle

**Beautify -> Amplify -> Degrade -> Restore**

- **Beautify:** civilization creates form, memory, art, law, legitimacy, public order, meaning, and inherited excellence.
- **Amplify:** empire extends civilization's reach, security, prestige, commerce, institutions, standards, and control.
- **Degrade:** civilizational entropy appears when amplification becomes extractive, coercive, brittle, self-justifying, or overextended.
- **Restore:** statecraft converts degraded power back into livable order through restraint, reform, settlement, renewal, retreat, or institutional repair.

Short form:

> Civilization beautifies. Empire amplifies. Entropy degrades. Statecraft restores.

## Folder Roles

- `civilization/` tracks beautification: what the civilization created that is worth preserving.
- `empire/` tracks amplification: how that greatness seeks reach, control, security, and prestige.
- `helix.md` tracks degradation: where amplification produces entropy and begins to deform the civilization.
- `state/` and `transactions/` track restoration: what authority, restraint, settlement, or renewal can regulate the cycle.

## Diagnostic Questions

Use these before promoting a seed, drafting a transaction, or staging a recursive update candidate:

- What is being beautified or preserved?
- What instrument amplifies it?
- What entropy does that instrument introduce?
- What restores order before degradation becomes collapse?

## Civilizational Entropy

Civilizational entropy is the internal disorder introduced when a civilization's outward striving for greatness becomes more extractive, coercive, brittle, or self-justifying than the civilization can metabolize.

Entropy signals include:

- security becoming permanent mobilization;
- law becoming domination;
- beauty becoming propaganda;
- legitimacy becoming performance;
- commerce becoming dependency;
- allies becoming traps;
- institutions serving control rather than order;
- restoration becoming nostalgia rather than renewal.

## Statecraft Use

The goal is not to stop amplification. Empire may protect civilization at first. The goal is to regulate amplification before degradation forces restoration through collapse.

When drafting an instrument, name the phase:

- **Beautify:** what civilization good is protected?
- **Amplify:** what reach or control is being used?
- **Degrade:** what entropy risk must be checked?
- **Restore:** what clause, carrier, or settlement repairs the cycle?
