# China Order Arc

WORK only; not Record.

## Purpose

Use this sheet when a China-lane read, hexagon stress test, transaction, clause, or current event invokes order, mandate, unity, development continuity, maritime ambiguity, party-state implementation, compute sovereignty, or fear of disorder.

The governing test:

**Which order form is China invoking, and has it crossed into rigidity, coercion, or responsibility for another actor's disorder?**

## Core Rule

Preserve order without forcing visible tutelage.

Preserve face without hiding material constraint.

Convert capacity into quiet implementation before capacity becomes coercive spectacle.

## Order Forms

| Order form | Historical spine | Statecraft meaning | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mandate-order | Xia / Shang / Zhou, ritual authority, Heaven, ancestor continuity | Legitimacy is proven by visible order, role discipline, provisioning, and moral-political performance. | Ritual becomes hollow performance; harmony suppresses warning signals; mandate loss appears through disorder. |
| Standardization-order | Qin-Han, writing / measure systems, bureaucracy, imperial scale | China prefers mechanisms that can be standardized, piloted, scaled, and kept administratively legible. | Legibility becomes rigidity; local signals are flattened; uniformity produces brittleness. |
| Hydraulic-order | Yellow River, Yangtze, Grand Canal, flood / grain / logistics | State authority is tested by managing flow: water, grain, routes, ports, energy, data, and capital. | Flow control fails visibly; ecological stress or logistics shock becomes legitimacy danger. |
| Fragmentation-memory | Opium, Taiping, Boxer, ROC, Taiwan split | Disorder, foreign penetration, and weak authority make fragmentation intolerable as a settlement form. | Anti-fragmentation logic rejects useful ambiguity or turns every restraint into humiliation. |
| Development-order | Deng reform, ports, infrastructure, trade integration, jobs, industrial upgrading | Peace and restraint are carryable when they protect growth, employment, energy, trade, and modernization. | Development continuity becomes leverage, dependency, or fear-driven overproduction. |
| Party-state-order | Maoist reconstruction, PRC, Xi consolidation, party center, provinces, PLA restraint | Authority must pass through the party-state implementation chain, not just leader-level language. | Implementation becomes visible coercion; center-line discipline suppresses adaptation; provinces, firms, banks, or ports misalign. |
| Compute-order | Digital governance, data sovereignty, AI / cloud stack, automated permissions | Order now includes control of data, compute, identity, platforms, censorship, finance, and infrastructure intelligence. | Compute sovereignty becomes lock-in, surveillance rigidity, tech denial spiral, or automated escalation. |

## Diagnostic Questions

1. What order problem is named: unity, route continuity, food, water, energy, ports, insurance, compute, Taiwan, internal stability, or external containment?
2. Which order form is being invoked?
3. Can the instrument be implemented quietly through party center, State Council organs, PLA restraint, banks, firms, ports, insurers, provinces, and compute / data channels?
4. Does the proposal preserve face while admitting the material constraint?
5. Does China gain optionality in routes, industry, energy, finance, or compute, or inherit dependency and public responsibility for disorder?
6. Does ambiguity reduce escalation, or hide incompatible sovereignty claims?
7. What would prove the order claim has become rigidity, coercion, blockade recognition, or responsibility for another actor's chaos?

## Alliance Mutation Threshold

China can price in hostile or adverse alliances when they remain bounded to a local security problem. It resists even defensive capacity when that capacity mutates into an unbounded regional lattice aimed at China containment.

Use this threshold when reading U.S.-South Korea, U.S.-Japan, AUKUS, NATO-Asia signals, Taiwan support packages, missile-defense upgrades, drone / sensor cooperation, supply-chain security, and port / basing agreements.

The core formula:

**Bounded alliance is tolerable.  
Unbounded lattice is threatening.  
Taiwan-operational integration is intolerable.**

Diagnostic sequence:

1. What is the alliance's original bounded function?
2. What new function is being attached to it?
3. Does the shift add Taiwan language, regional contingency planning, missile-defense integration, drones, sensors, logistics, industrial denial, or visible bloc choreography?
4. Can China still describe the instrument as local deterrence, or must it describe it as containment infrastructure?
5. Would resisting the instrument preserve order, or overread defensive adaptation as encirclement?

## Hexagon Use

When running `china hex <topic>`, use the order arc as a China-specific substrate after the six lenses vote:

- `geo` names flow, route, water, port, energy, food, and maritime constraints.
- `peace` tests face-saving restraint, quiet implementation, and development continuity.
- `war` prices coercion, blockade / quarantine ambiguity, PLA signaling, and escalation.
- `god` tests mandate, unity, ritual order, and forbidden humiliation.
- `lit` tests moral narrative, sages, patience, disorder memory, and grievance capture.
- `art` tests form, ceremony, refinement, infrastructure beauty, and visible legitimacy.

Do not let the order arc become the central pillar of the hexagon. It is a China-lane diagnostic overlay.
