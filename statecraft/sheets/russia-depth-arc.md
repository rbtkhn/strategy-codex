# Russia Depth Arc

WORK only; not Record.

## Purpose

Use this sheet when a Russia-lane read, six-part doctrine read, transaction, clause, or current event invokes Russian depth, exposure, encirclement, recognition, buffers, access, sanctions resistance, or great-power equilibrium.

The governing test:

**Which depth form is Russia invoking, and has it crossed into its failure mode?**

## Core Rule

Recognize exposure, not empire.

Recognize optionality, not dependency.

Convert power into equilibrium before power becomes encirclement.

## Depth Forms

| Depth form | Historical spine | Statecraft meaning | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frontier-depth | Pechenegs, Cumans, Mongols, steppe exposure | Open approaches make security spatial; frontier defense creates the political line. | Offensive capability and defensive security collapse into unlimited frontier expansion. |
| Symbolic-depth | Kiev, Kulikovo, Orthodoxy, Moscow, Third Rome | Russia can preserve meaning, faith, and dignity before full material sovereignty returns. | Symbolic victory outruns actual capacity and becomes grievance license. |
| Imperial-depth | Muscovy, Romanovs, Baltic, Black Sea, Siberia | Exposure is converted into access, buffers, ports, and strategic reach. | Access demand becomes permanent expansion trap or warm-water fixation. |
| Space-depth | Poltava, Napoleon, Barbarossa | Russia survives penetration through distance, time, climate, logistics, industry, reserves, command continuity, and cohesion. | Depth myth hides modernization failure, coalition exposure, or excessive sacrifice. |
| System-depth | Panin, Vienna, Great Game, Yalta/Potsdam, Cold War | Russia gains safety by being recognized inside equilibrium, rules, zones, parity, consultation, and reciprocal restraint. | Equilibrium becomes cover for domination, or Russian power provokes the coalition it fears. |
| Dependency-depth | China energy, sanctions routing, alternative payments, external absorbers | Russia absorbs Western pressure through markets, buyers, payments, logistics, diplomatic shelter, and alternative architecture. | Optionality becomes dependency when the absorber prices, paces, or politically constrains Russia. |

## Diagnostic Questions

1. What concrete exposure is named: route, range, borderland, port, corridor, market, payment channel, command node, or warning time?
2. Which depth form is being invoked?
3. Is the claim bounded by reciprocal limits, monitoring, consultation, neutrality, pricing discipline, or exit rules?
4. Does the proposed recognition preserve third-party sovereignty?
5. Does the instrument expand Russian optionality or create dependency on an absorber?
6. Is power being converted into equilibrium before it summons a balancing coalition?
7. What would prove the depth claim has become imperial depth, dependency-depth, or sacrifice veto?

## Six-Lens Use

When a live read still benefits from separate `god`, `lit`, `art`, `geo`, `war`, and `peace` pressure checks, use the depth arc as a Russia-specific substrate after those lenses are read separately:

- `geo` names the exposure and material constraint.
- `war` prices command, sacrifice, and escalation.
- `peace` asks whether recognition can become reciprocal restraint.
- `god` tests whether non-subordination has become a forbidden-bargain claim.
- `lit` tests whether grievance captures the instrument.
- `art` tests whether visible form makes Russia look recognized or managed.

Do not let the depth arc become the whole doctrine. It is a Russia-lane diagnostic overlay inside a larger six-part frame.
