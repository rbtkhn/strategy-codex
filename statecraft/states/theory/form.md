The six-term **Civilizational Statecraft Framework** spans three pair pages: **Form** (this page), [Truth](truth.md), and [Memory](memory.md). See the [theory shelf](README.md) for the whole map.

## Why this pair exists

Civilization and empire are not the same layer. Civilization **stores** inherited order; empire **scales** it outward through reach, coercion, procedure, and command.

Most live statecraft objects need both terms distinguished before lane descent. Collapsing them produces either symbolic inheritance without carrier or machinery without legitimating depth.

Read [Civilization and Empire](../introduction.md) for the whole-book opening essay. Pair with [Rhythm](rhythm.md) when phase placement in beautify → amplify → degrade governs the read.

## The two terms

### Civilization

Civilization is inherited order: the legitimacy substrate, continuity-bearing pattern, symbolic inheritance, and civilizational form that make a people intelligible to itself across rupture.

### Empire

Empire is outward instrument: the amplification stack that projects power through coercion, procedure, finance, infrastructure, law, alliance, logistics, and reach.

## Governing-layer question

Ask first:

> Is this object civilization-heavy, empire-heavy, or fraying between them?

Useful first distinctions:

- if the object is about legitimacy substrate, continuity-bearing inheritance, or sacred civilizational form, it is usually **civilization-heavy**
- if it is about outward machinery, coercive or procedural reach, sanctions, command, corridors, or platform leverage, it is usually **empire-heavy**
- if machinery runs while legitimacy thins, suspect **empire outrunning civilization**

For the other governing pairs, open [Truth](truth.md) (faith ↔ science) or [Memory](memory.md) (memory ↔ desire).

## Civilization and empire

Civilization stores the inheritance; empire scales it outward.

The governing question is not merely who has power, but whether outward instrument is still answerable to civilizational continuity. When empire outruns civilization, the machinery of reach begins to degrade the inheritance it claims to protect.

This pair is the structural spine beneath [Rhythm](rhythm.md)'s beautify → amplify → degrade law. Civilizational motion is the **historical rhythm** these terms ride through time.

## Main failure modes

- **civilization without empire** — inheritance remains symbolically real but lacks sufficient carrier, reach, or instrument to defend itself
- **empire outrunning civilization** — instrument expands faster than legitimacy can bear, so control degrades the inheritance
