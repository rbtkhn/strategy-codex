Date anchor: **May 28, 2026**.

Sovereign continuity is not complete until it names a present carrier. In the CIV-STATE shelf, that present carrier becomes fully legible only when its sovereign apex is named as well.

This note is therefore not a generic officeholder list. It is the present-tense capstone of the CIV-STATE volume architecture: the point where each sovereignty chain reaches the current world through a live head.

## Case Distinctions

### China

China reaches the present through the PRC party-state. `Xi Jinping` matters here not as a mere officeholder, but as the present apex of a continuity claim that reaches much farther back than the current regime form.

Boundary: do not reduce the continuity claim to party tactics alone.

### Persia

Persia reaches the present through the Islamic Republic, but the sovereign apex is not identical to the presidency.

- Sovereign apex: `Ali Khamenei`
- Executive president: `Masoud Pezeshkian`

Boundary: do not confuse the presidency with the sovereign head inside the Islamic-Republic continuity structure.

### Rome

Rome reaches the present through a transformed residue-carrier rather than through a territorial Roman state. The relevant present sovereign head in this shelf is therefore `Pope Leo XIV`.

Boundary: do not confuse residue-carrier continuity with ordinary state officeholding.

### Russia

Russia reaches the present through the Russian Federation as the latest claimant to a much older chain of regathered authority. `Vladimir Putin` is the present sovereign head through whom that restoration claim is concentrated now.

Boundary: do not confuse restoration claim with simple uninterrupted institutional sameness.

### America

America reaches the present through the United States, but the continuity argument in this shelf remains the longer and contested Anglo-American chain rather than a simple `1776`-only frame. `Donald Trump` is therefore the current sovereign head of the present carrier, not proof that the continuity chain itself is uncontested.

Boundary: do not confuse U.S. officeholding with a settled answer to the continuity question.

## Working Rule

When a CIV-STATE volume reaches the present, it should close with four things:

1. **Current carrier**
2. **Current sovereign head**
3. **Present continuity claim**
4. **Boundary note**

That closure is what turns the volume from civilizational description into live statecraft memory.
