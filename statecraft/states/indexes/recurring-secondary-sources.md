# Recurring Secondary Sources

WORK only; not Record.

Use this surface when a secondary work keeps reappearing across CIV-STATE and the operator needs a governed note rather than repeated ad hoc explanation.

## Recurrence Taxonomy

- `cross_civilizational_recurring`
- `civilization_specific_recurring`
- `era_local`

## Support Role Taxonomy

- `chronology`
- `provenance`
- `translation`

- `counterweight`

## Promotion Taxonomy

- `local`
- `promotable`
- `promoted`

## Will Durant

- `recurrence_class`: `cross_civilizational_recurring`
- `normal_function`: broad civilizational synthesis, continuity framing, transition compression
- `normal_support_roles`: `institutional_context`, `misreading_correction`
- `limits`: not specialist final authority for chronology, translation, provenance, or fine-grained institutional dispute
- `main_explanation_owners`: China ancient, Persia ancient, Rome ancient, Russia medieval
- `promotion_status`: `promotable`

Use Durant when scale and continuity are the real problem. Do not let Durant replace the sharper specialist clarifier or counterweight that keeps a shelf honest.

## Churchill, A History of the English-Speaking Peoples

- `recurrence_class`: `civilization_specific_recurring`
- `normal_function`: Anglo-American continuity narration and self-understanding
- `normal_support_roles`: `institutional_context`, `misreading_correction`
- `limits`: not cross-civilizational authority and not a sufficient check against heroic continuity compression on its own
- `main_explanation_owner`: America medieval
- `promotion_status`: `local`

Use Churchill inside the America volume when the live question is inheritance and narrated continuity. Pair him with counterweights that keep Anglo carriage from becoming mythic self-approval.
