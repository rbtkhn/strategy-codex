# China Secondary Sources - Cybernetic

WORK only; not Record.

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the cybernetic China primary shelf when post-1991 rejuvenation, digital governance, maritime-frontier order, and party-state control need sharper institutional reading or counterweight against both collapse and omnipotence narratives.

## Core Clarifiers

- Elizabeth Economy, work on PRC governance and external posture, for readable late-carrier clarification.
- Carl Minzner, work on end-of-reform and political tightening, for party-state rehardening after reform-era looseness.
- Barry Naughton, work on Chinese political economy, for control, growth, and state capacity without technological mysticism.
- Rush Doshi, used narrowly for strategic posture and system-friction framing rather than as a total China explanation.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Minxin Pei, as a correction against omnipotence readings and frictionless rejuvenation narratives.
- Jude Blanchette and related leadership-state analysis, as a correction against treating cybernetic China as either pure machine or imminent collapse.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is official doctrine, white-paper language, or constitutional-state wording. Open this shelf when the problem is how to read digital governance, rejuvenation, and maritime-frontier control without drifting into techno-fetish or simple-decay caricature.
