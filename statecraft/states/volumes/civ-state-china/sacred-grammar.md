# Sacred Grammar

WORK only; not Record.

Volume-local deep grammar for **Civilizational Statecraft**, Volume I — China.

- **Core grammar**  
  Mandate, ritual order, and anti-disorder legitimacy. Rule is justified when it preserves order, provisioning, continuity, and civilizational dignity; disorder is not just failure but mandate stress.

- **Legitimating image**  
  Heaven does not ratify a ruler once and for all. Authority remains conditional on visible competence, ritual coherence, and anti-fragmentation performance.

- **Continuity through rupture**  
  Zhou moral-cosmological grammar survives dynastic turnover, Qin coercive standardization, imperial bureaucracy, humiliation memory, republican fracture, and PRC restoration. The grammar mutates from ritual rule into administrative performance without losing its anti-chaos core.

- **Relation to sovereignty chain**  
  This grammar sits beneath the traditional foundational-sovereign question. Yu and Xia name the deep opening of the chain, but Zhou gives the clearest legitimacy language through which later Chinese sovereignty becomes intelligible, criticizable, and renewable.

- **Statecraft relevance**  
  China can accept restraint, sequencing, and quiet compromise when they preserve face, implementation competence, unity, and order. It resists bargains that publicize tutelage, disorder, humiliation, or broken command.
