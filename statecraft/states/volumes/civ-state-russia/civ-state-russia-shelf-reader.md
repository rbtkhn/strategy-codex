# Russia Shelf Reader

WORK only; not Record.

## What This Shelf Is For

Use this shelf to read Russia as a late-opening civilization-state whose continuity is carried by regathering after fracture. The shelf is for seeing how sovereignty survives domination, empire, mutation, collapse, and restoration claim without forcing a false ancient documentary opening.

## How To Read The Eras

Read Russia as a shelf of compression, regathering, expansion, mutation, and post-`1991` restoration. Start in the medieval documentary opening, not in fabricated antiquity. Then watch how Orthodoxy, Horde pressure, Muscovite regathering, imperial scale, Soviet mutation, and post-Soviet restoration-through-pressure form one chain.

## What Each Era Is Doing

- `Medieval` establishes chronicle-bearing Rus, Orthodoxy, Horde crucible, and Muscovite regathering.
- `Colonial` carries Romanov scale, service-state form, serfdom, expansion, and recognition through pressure-bearing growth.
- `Industrial` carries mass-state, revolution, Soviet mutation, war endurance, and terminal collapse through `1991`.
- `Cybernetic` carries post-`1991` humiliation, restoration claim, presidency and security-state concentration, and Ukraine-plus-sanctions proof.

## What To Look For

- regathering after fracture rather than smooth continuity
- Orthodoxy as legitimacy carrier
- domination internalized into later sovereign form
- Soviet mutation without full civilizational erasure
- restoration through pressure after `1991`
