# Rome Secondary Sources - Colonial


## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the colonial Rome primary shelf when papal residue, Latin-Catholic expansion, early modern Italy, or outward residue beyond the ancient core need stronger institutional context.

## Core Clarifiers

- John W. O'Malley, for papal, ecclesial, and Catholic institutional continuity.
- Eamon Duffy, for Catholic transformation, continuity, and anti-caricature correction.
- Anthony Pagden and related imperial/Catholic-world scholarship, for residue beyond the Italian core.
- Will Durant, *The Renaissance*, used briefly where broad transition framing from medieval residue to early modern dispersion is helpful.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Reformation and Counter-Reformation scholarship, as a correction against reading papal residue as unbroken universal control.
- Italy and church-state scholarship, as a correction against treating early modern Roman residue as purely spiritual and detached from institutional power.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is papal bull, ecclesial decree, concordat precursor, or self-description. Open this shelf when the problem is how residue disperses outward, how Renaissance and Catholic reform change the carrier, or where universal claim outruns actual control.
