## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the industrial Rome primary shelf when narrowed Vatican carry, nation-state friction, concordat politics, or Latin-world residue through 1991 need stronger chronology or counterweight against false death reading.

## Core Clarifiers

- Owen Chadwick, for church-state modernity, papal adaptation, and industrial-era Catholic politics.
- John Pollard, for Vatican and Italian-state interaction in the modern period.
- Eamon Duffy, for continuity and anti-death reading in modern Catholic history.
- Philippe Chenaux or equivalent Vatican-century scholarship, for institutional sequence through the 20th century.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Modern Italy and Vatican scholarship, as a correction against treating industrial Rome as only national decline.
- Critical church-history work, as a correction against nostalgic residue narratives that ignore political compromise and institutional narrowing.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is papal encyclical language, concordat text, or official Catholic self-description. Open this shelf when the difficulty is how narrowed Roman residue still carries, or when the shelf risks being misread as either dead antiquity or uninterrupted global authority.
