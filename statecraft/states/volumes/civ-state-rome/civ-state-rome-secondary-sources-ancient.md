# Rome Secondary Sources - Ancient

WORK only; not Record.

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the ancient Rome primary shelf when republic-to-empire conversion, legal universality, administrative scale, or late-republic rupture need stronger chronology or anti-cliche correction. This file owns the main Rome-side explanation of why Will Durant is useful here: broad civilizational sweep and readable transition framing, but not specialist final authority.

## Core Clarifiers

- Mary Beard, *SPQR*, for broad republican-to-imperial continuity and social-political framing.
- Ronald Syme, *The Roman Revolution*, for republic-to-principate conversion and political form change.
- Fergus Millar and related Roman-state scholarship, for imperial administration and legal-political scale.
- Will Durant, *Caesar and Christ*, as a broad civilizational synthesis clarifier for republic crisis, Caesar, Augustus, and classical Roman continuity.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Clifford Ando and related legal-imperial scholarship, as a correction against overly personalist readings of Roman order.
- Specialist republican scholarship, as a correction against pure decadence narratives that flatten structural change into moral decline alone.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is law, annalistic memory, constitutional wording, or imperial self-description. Open this shelf when the difficulty is transition sequencing, institutional form, or resisting theatrical decline storytelling.
