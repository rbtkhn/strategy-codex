# Rome Bibliography

This is the working volume bibliography for the Rome CIV-STATE book. Its center of gravity is primary sources first, then bounded secondary and doctrinal support. The Industrial shelf ends in 1991 and the Cybernetic shelf begins after that boundary.

Use it to retrieve Rome as a transformed continuity case:

- republic to empire
- empire to Christian universality
- Christian empire to Eastern Roman continuity
- Eastern Roman continuity to papal residue
- residue to present successor-order comparison
