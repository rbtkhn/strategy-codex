# Persia Secondary Sources - Medieval

WORK only; not Record.

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the medieval Persia primary shelf when Islam as transformed carrier, Persianate continuity, *Shahnameh* transmission, science-law synthesis, or Safavid reconcentration need stronger transmission or institutional context.

## Core Clarifiers

- Marshall Hodgson, *The Venture of Islam*, for Persianate transformation without erasure reading.
- Bert Fragner and related Persianate-world scholarship, for language, transmission, and supra-dynastic continuity.
- Dick Davis on the *Shahnameh*, for transmission, literary memory, and continuity through epic narrative.
- Andrew Newman or equivalent Safavid scholarship, for Shi'a reconcentration and the hinge into later Iranian form.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Specialist Safavid and Islamicate scholarship used to correct any simple "Iran disappeared into Islam" reading.
- Comparative Persianate India work, as a correction against reading medieval Persian continuity as territorially sealed inside the plateau alone.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is doctrinal language, epic self-reading, or legal-political self-description. Open this shelf when the problem is transformed-carrier interpretation, transmission, or where later commentators falsely divide Persian and Islamic continuity.
