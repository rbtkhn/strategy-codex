# Persia Secondary Sources - Ancient

WORK only; not Record.

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the ancient Persia primary shelf when Achaemenid imperial form, Zoroastrian sacred residue, parity-bearing sovereignty, or Roman-comparison temptation need stronger chronology or anti-cliche control. This file owns the main Persia-side explanation of why Will Durant is useful here: broad civilizational sweep and accessible transition framing, but not specialist final judgment.

## Core Clarifiers

- Pierre Briant, *From Cyrus to Alexander*, for Achaemenid scale, administration, and imperial form.
- Touraj Daryaee, work on ancient Iran, for continuity framing and civilizational perspective.
- Maria Brosius, work on Achaemenid and Sasanian state form, for institution and social order.
- Will Durant, *Our Oriental Heritage*, as a broad civilizational synthesis clarifier for ancient Persian continuity and imperial parity.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Amelie Kuhrt and related specialist scholarship, as a correction against simplified orientalist imperial caricature.
- Josef Wiesehofer and related Iran scholarship, as a correction against reading ancient Persia only through Greek or Roman hostile framing.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is imperial inscription, sacred language, or the civilization's own sovereignty grammar. Open this shelf when chronology, comparative scale, or anti-cliche correction is the real need.
