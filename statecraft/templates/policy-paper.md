## Failure Modes

- What would make the recommendation fail?
- What early indicators would show failure?
- What adjustment or off-ramp should be prepared?

## Richelieu Check

- Does this serve a durable state interest?
- Which institution can carry it?
- Does ideology obscure the real interest?
- Are finance, administration, and intelligence requirements explicit?
- Where could necessity become excuse?

## Bismarck Check

- Is the aim limited?
- Does the policy isolate the correct adversary?
- Does it preserve optionality?
- Does it avoid frightening neutrals or overbinding allies?
- What restraint is required after success?

## Source Boundary

List the sources used and separate fact, interpretation, and operator synthesis.
