## Executive Read

March 2026 is the month in which **tactical decapitation success failed to become strategic control** — and the archive never stopped saying so from unlike functions.

Operation Epic Fury (from late February into March) delivered the precision-age performance Western planners expected: leadership strikes, synchronized waves, regime figures reported killed. Pape's March 1 newsletter names the trap immediately: tactical success is "exactly as expected," yet "tactical success vs Iran is slipping beyond control." By [Mar 3 day 4](../day/2026-03-03.md) the bench converges on **attrition/endurance** as the real war type — Iran wins if it **does not lose**; **Hormuz closes** (**J03-C1**); **Anchorage/Witkoff trust lane dies**; **3× F-15 friendly fire** (**J03-A1**) marks measurement error on day one of the attrition read. Fourteen days earlier, [Feb 17](../day/2026-02-17.md) had the same actors in **pre-kinetic** mode: **partial Hormuz drill** (**J17-P1**), **Trump boxed** (**J17-P5/P7**), **Geneva bifurcation** on Ukraine (**J17-G2**, Kartapolov **J17-M3**) — same week, two wars, **Anchorage still alive**. March's opening law: **Feb 17 maneuver → Mar 3 closure** is escalation arc, not contradiction.

By mid-month Crooke on Judging Freedom states the operative White House condition plainly: Trump's plan is to have no plan — ceasefire claims are "a lie… to deceive and to disrupt" while Iran treats the conflict as a long war only just started. By March 19 Crooke's Conflicts Forum essay titles the month object: "as the wheels come off the Iran conflict."

Two subordinate objects persist across all four weeks:

1. **Asymmetric mosaic war vs air-assault measurement** — Iran's dispersed command, silo-launched "air force," intercept-stock exhaustion, and Hormuz gating are not a hidden surprise; Crooke, Marandi, and Mercouris describe them as pre-planned counter-design to 2003-style decapitation. Western reporting keeps measuring tons of bombs; the bench keeps measuring logistics, psychology, and duration. [Mar 3 Crooke × Pape × Simplicius bench](../../notes/2026-03-03-crooke-pape-simplicius-air-power-survival-bench-weave.md) seeds this before [Mar 16 density](../../notes/2026-03-16-pape-vs-crooke-mosaic-trap.md).
2. **Dual-theater coupling** — Diesen–Mercouris (March 21) and Freeman (March 23) thread Iran escalation into Ukraine/Europe liability and economic shock; [Feb 17 Geneva week](../../notes/2026-02-17-geneva-day-weave-helmer-mercouris.md) already stacks **Ukraine guarantee poison** (**J17-M4–M6**) on **Iran Hormuz maneuver** same calendar day. March treats Washington as fighting more than one entrapment surface from week one.

**Primary lane: America** (hubris, gut-instinct command, victory narrative vs implementation gap, deal-theater lying). **Secondary: Persia** (mosaic phases, Hormuz gate, endurance nationalism). **Tertiary: Russia** (Ukraine war transformed by Iran shock). Europe is exposure surface, not month owner.

March **genesis law for June:** June's "sponsor cannot bind the client" and "recognition gate spent as hard power" presuppose March's earlier finding — **coercive opening never produced a closure grammar**.
