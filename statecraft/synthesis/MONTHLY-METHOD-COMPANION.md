# Monthly Method Supplement

> **Naming:** This file is the **monthly method supplement** for `statecraft/synthesis` — not a *statecraft note* (those are bounded mechanism/compare follow-ons under the same shelf). Legacy filename `MONTHLY-METHOD-COMPANION.md` retained for link stability.

Purpose: define the next architectural step for `statecraft/synthesis` month notes after the first May 2026 proof month.

Use this note when the active monthly method in [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) is already accepted and the real question is how month notes should deepen from here without outrunning the doctrine, the benchmarks, or the shelf's bounded function.

## What May 2026 Proved

The first month note, [2026-05.md](month/2026-05.md), proved five things:

- a month note can sit above a completed daily shelf without replaying chronology
- `Functional Convergence` can make cross-speaker compression visible
- a month note can remain object-first rather than speaker-first
- the five-volume CIV-STATE pass can deepen a month object without taking over the note
- selective re-entry days are more useful than a full month recap

That is enough to establish the monthly form as real, but not enough to let it grow carelessly.

## Week hinges and monthly re-entry

**Best Re-entry Days** in month notes should **not** replay scattered day links when month-aligned week hinges exist.

- Re-entry routing lives in **`YYYY-MM-weekN-start-here.md`** ([METHOD § Week Hinge](METHOD.md#week-hinge-contract)).
- Month notes list **which weekN** mattered (`week1` recognition gate, `week2` command fracture, etc.) and lift **object-migration lines** from finalized hinges into `Month Arcs`.
- Promotion from week hinge → month is **only** on explicit **`statecraft monthly synthesis`**—no dream auto-merge into partial month files.

## Next-Wave Monthly Law

Future month notes should preserve the current contract and deepen in three directions only:

### 1. Better object discrimination

The next gain should be sharper discrimination between:

- the primary month object
- subordinate month objects
- loud but non-owning objects

The month note should get better at saying what the month actually belonged to, not merely what happened a lot.

### 2. Better function-to-object compression

`Functional Convergence` should stay explicit, but it should become tighter over time.

The improvement target is:

- less speaker-credit feel
- more visible convergence law
- clearer proof that unlike functions landed on the same governing object

If a later month names many functions but cannot compress them into fewer real objects, the note has expanded without learning.

### 3. Better five-volume deepening

The monthly five-volume section should remain lighter than a daily note, but it should get more exact about which volume contributed the true deepening gain.

The useful question is not:

- did all five bullets appear?

The useful question is:

- which one or two volumes most changed the month-level read?

That preserves the five-volume audit structure while allowing the interpretation to remain alive.

## What Not To Add Yet

Do not add these to month notes yet:

- day-by-day early / middle / late month recap blocks
- full quote-heavy density matching the daily notes
- automatic month-score or month-grade language
- generated function counts
- validator-driven insight scoring
- extra monthly subtypes

Those additions would increase apparent sophistication faster than actual method quality.

## Ready Next Additions

These are the next legitimate month-level additions once another proof month exists:

- a second benchmark month with a different stress profile
- a sparse-month benchmark if the shelf later has one
- a small note in `Best Re-entry Days` distinguishing `entry`, `hinge`, and `decisive compression` days
- a validator that only checks section presence, function-label validity, and five-volume bullet order

Those are structure-preserving additions rather than doctrinal drift.

## Validator Boundary

The right automation boundary for month notes is narrow.

Good validator targets:

- required section presence and order
- `Functional Convergence` presence
- function labels limited to the fixed set
- five-volume section presence and bullet order
- `Best Re-entry Days` present with real links

Bad validator targets:

- judging whether convergence is intellectually good
- scoring the quality of cross-speaker compression
- deciding whether the five-volume gain is profound enough

Structure can be checked mechanically. Compression quality still needs human judgment.

## Proof Rule

No future monthly method change should be treated as successful until it preserves or improves [2026-05.md](month/2026-05.md) on three dimensions:

- object-first compression
- explicit `Functional Convergence`
- non-ornamental five-volume deepening

If a cleaner later month loses any of those, it is regression dressed as polish.
