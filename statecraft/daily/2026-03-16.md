## Executive Read

The dominant object on March 16 is **no-plan command meets mosaic war — center of gravity shifts to Hormuz and intercept exhaustion**.

Two weeks after opening decapitation, unlike functions agree the White House still performs victory and deal theater while Iran runs a different war. Crooke names the executive condition: Trump's plan is to have no plan — ceasefire claims are deception; Iran treats the fight as long war only just started. Pape (same day, newsletter tier) reframes the object economically: the president said the war could end "in two or three days" on day one; "That was 16 days ago" — center of gravity moves from skies over Iran to Hormuz and global energy markets.

The secondary object is **implementation impossibility as shared falsifier**: Ritter on Hormuz ("we'd lose every single ship we sent in there"), Johnson on intercept depletion (640 Iranian ballistic missiles vs 900 THAAD interceptors math), Escobar–Johnson on allied refusal to join Trump's flotilla, Baud on Trump begging partners after dominating-air rhetoric.
