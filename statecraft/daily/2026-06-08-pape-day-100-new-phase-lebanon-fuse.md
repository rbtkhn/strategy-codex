## Lebanon Fuse — Collision Geometry (Jun 4)

Pape is strongest when the issue is **why diplomacy fails while fighting continues**:

- **Triad exclusion:** Washington wants negotiations; Jerusalem wants security; Tehran wants Hezbollah preserved — objectives **exclude one another**.
- **Battlefield vs headline:** Ceasefire announced; operations continue — "diplomacy generates headlines, but the battlefield generates reality."
- **Hezbollah as deterrent pillar:** Durable deal for Tehran requires Hezbollah as meaningful force; Israeli buffer-zone logic pushes **permanent** weakening of Hezbollah.
- **Horizontal escalation preview:** Iran's "strongest card may not be Lebanon" — Red Sea / Yanbu / East-West pipeline bypass creates **dual-corridor** vulnerability if Houthis shift from disruption to systematic interference.

Lebanon Fuse therefore owns:

**Why Lebanon is not ancillary — it is the structural reason April ceasefire grammar cannot hold.**

## New Phase — Survival Converts to Hegemony (Jun 6)

Two days later Pape shifts altitude from **fuse mechanics** to **post-survival strategy**:

- **Sequence:** Survival → Leverage → Regional Power (Rezaei $24B demand as diplomacy-as-leverage, not relief-seeking).
- **Hegemony formula:** Threshold nuclear capability + energy-route influence + U.S.-alliance pressure = regional primacy (not territorial empire).
- **Target geometry:** Kuwait/Bahrain host-nation strikes → alliance friction → reduced U.S. presence — "America can no longer provide security."
- **Hormuz pillars:** energy flows, nuclear threshold, Gulf military predominance — Iran moving on all three, none complete.
- **Deep logic:** Military denial → economic pressure → diplomatic leverage.
- **Closing question:** Gulf states more secure **with American bases or without them?**

New Phase therefore owns:

**What Iran does after survival — convert Hormuz control and Gulf anxiety into political concessions.**

## Day 100 — Proof Event + Watch List (Jun 8)

Day 100 **closes the loop** Fuse opened and New Phase theorized:

### Proof chain (last 12–24 hours)

1. Israeli strikes on Hezbollah near **Beirut** (Lebanon fuse **fires**).
2. First direct Iranian missiles at Israel since April ceasefire — triggered by Lebanon, not prior Israel-on-Iran strike alone (**third-party rule** in Pape's vocabulary: theaters **linked**).
3. Israel strikes inside Iran; tit-for-tat resumes — April ceasefire **fragile / collapsing**.
4. Rezaei rhetoric: deadlock, $24B upfront, expand beyond Hormuz to Indian Ocean / Red Sea / Bab al-Mandab / Mediterranean (**New Phase** rhetoric at Day-100 tempo).
5. Gulf-host strikes (Kuwait, Bahrain, airport disruption) + inventory/SPR draw (**New Phase** pressure campaign + Day-100 **cushion** section).

Pape's Day-100 surprise: Iran **gaining** leverage, not losing it — explains ceasefire breakdown.

### Cushion / time inversion

- Opening campaign failed → regime survived → **success changes incentives**.
- ~500M bbl global stock draw Mar–May; IEA critical-level warning; U.S. commercial + SPR combined 16M bbl weekly draw (week ending May 29); eight consecutive weeks of U.S. crude decline.
- Trump blockade extended; concessions off table until Labor Day — Washington still assumes **time favors U.S.**; Tehran increasingly assumes **opposite**.
- **Escalation trap:** each side reads resistance as need for **more** pressure, not strategy failure.
- **Coercion falsifier:** Iraq 1990s, Trump max-pressure — punishment hurts society faster than it moves authoritarian governments; repression + nationalism, not mass uprising at Day 100.
