## Executive Read

March 2026 is the month in which **tactical decapitation success failed to become strategic control** — and the archive never stopped saying so from unlike functions.

Operation Epic Fury (from late February into March) delivered the precision-age performance Western planners expected: leadership strikes, synchronized waves, regime figures reported killed. Pape's March 1 newsletter names the trap immediately: tactical success is "exactly as expected," yet "tactical success vs Iran is slipping beyond control." By mid-month Crooke on Judging Freedom states the operative White House condition plainly: Trump's plan is to have no plan — ceasefire claims are "a lie… to deceive and to disrupt" while Iran treats the conflict as a long war only just started. By March 19 Crooke's Conflicts Forum essay titles the month object: "as the wheels come off the Iran conflict."

Two subordinate objects persist across all four weeks:

1. **Asymmetric mosaic war vs air-assault measurement** — Iran's dispersed command, silo-launched "air force," intercept-stock exhaustion, and Hormuz gating are not a hidden surprise; Crooke, Marandi, and Mercouris describe them as pre-planned counter-design to 2003-style decapitation. Western reporting keeps measuring tons of bombs; the bench keeps measuring logistics, psychology, and duration.
2. **Dual-theater coupling** — Diesen–Mercouris (March 21) and Freeman (March 23) thread Iran escalation into Ukraine/Europe liability and economic shock; March already treats Washington as fighting more than one entrapment surface.

**Primary lane: America** (hubris, gut-instinct command, victory narrative vs implementation gap, deal-theater lying). **Secondary: Persia** (mosaic phases, Hormuz gate, endurance nationalism). **Tertiary: Russia** (Ukraine war transformed by Iran shock). Europe is exposure surface, not month owner.

March **genesis law for June:** June's "sponsor cannot bind the client" and "recognition gate spent as hard power" presuppose March's earlier finding — **coercive opening never produced a closure grammar**.
