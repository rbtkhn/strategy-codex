# Protected Channel / Non-Regime-Change Framework - Sequence Brief

WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: convert the protected-channel framework into the smallest honest rollout sequence so the hardened clauses can be tested as a live instrument rather than admired as static text.

## Sequence logic

The order matters.

The object fails if it begins with Iranian restraint before America provides a dignity-bearing assurance and a visible reciprocal marker.
It also fails if America gives rhetoric without protected carriers, review, and breach procedure.

## Five-step rollout

### 1. Public assurance

The United States publicly states that the framework is **not** a regime-change or partition instrument and that its purpose is bounded de-escalation, civilian continuity, and restored bargaining authority.

Why first:

- this is the minimum asymmetry-correcting move
- without it, protected channels look like a trap

### 2. Named protected carriers

The United States, through State and the mediator, identifies in writing:

- protected diplomatic carriers
- mediator channels
- high-authority communication carriers
- the emergency contact chain for breach notice

Why second:

- the object is about preserving bargaining carriers after targeting
- without named carriers, there is no real protected channel

### 3. Reversible visible marker

Activation is paired with one visible but reversible reciprocal marker:

- narrow humanitarian or technical sanctions waiver
- temporary deconfliction assurance
- another explicitly named activation marker

Why third:

- this is the dignity-bearing proof that the framework is not process without reciprocity
- it gives the Iranian side something public enough to defend internally, but small enough for Washington to reverse if breach occurs

### 4. Review and reporting

Once activated:

- Congress receives automatic notification
- a short review clock begins
- Treasury and State produce the legal-basis and implementation record
- allies receive reassurance consultation

Why fourth:

- this converts executive activation into bounded command rather than drift
- it gives the America side a successor-stable carrier package

### 5. Breach ladder

If a protected carrier is attacked or degraded:

1. confidential mediator notice
2. urgent consultation
3. public attribution if unresolved
4. congressional reporting
5. only then sanctions snapback, suspension, or military response under the agreed override rules

Why fifth:

- the object fails if the first alleged breach immediately collapses back into accusation theater
- the ladder is what makes the framework look governable rather than sentimental

## Critical sequence warning

Do **not** start with:

- Iranian restraint first
- sanctions relief first without protected-carrier designation
- quiet talks first without public no-regime-change language
- public rhetoric first without review and breach mechanics

Those paths all recreate the same credibility trap in different forms.

## Bottleneck

The honest bottleneck is between steps `3` and `4`:

- the visible marker must be meaningful enough for Iran
- the review structure must be strong enough for America

That tension is the core test of whether the framework is real.
