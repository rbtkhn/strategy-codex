America cannot reach a viable settlement if every humanitarian acknowledgment is treated as total legal surrender.

## Protected Civilian-Harm Pattern

Minimum clause family:

> The parties shall preserve protected channels while creating an independent civilian-harm review mechanism for Minab-class incidents. Participation in the mechanism shall not constitute admission of liability, waiver of claims, recognition of regime-change aims, or acceptance that civilian casualties may be used as bargaining pressure. Humanitarian access, casualty documentation, and memorial dignity shall proceed before irreversible military or sanctions concessions are demanded.
