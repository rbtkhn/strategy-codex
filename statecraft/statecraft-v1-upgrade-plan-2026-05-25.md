## Next-Week Operating Discipline

1. reopen from [statecraft.md](statecraft.md) when continuity is not already live

3. ask for the narrowest honest output form before drafting mechanism

- bridge-as-router habits
- fresh frameworks for already mature objects

Protect instead:

- one kernel
- one ownership membrane
- one governed recursive-learning loop
