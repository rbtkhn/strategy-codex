# China Lane Spine And Support Ring

Purpose: name the China lane's actual main route and distinguish it from the strengthening surfaces that deepen the route without replacing it.

## Core Spine

The canonical China lane spine is:

```text
Helix -> current state carriers -> transaction drafting
```

Read it this way:

1. [China helix](helix.md) restores the integrated line of order, continuity, outward leverage, deformation, and restoration.
2. [China state](state/) decides who can quietly implement, finance, insure, warn, broker, or restrain the live move.
3. [China transactions](transactions/) converts the resolved lane object into spendable mechanism or clause language.

This is the honest main route. It should stay stable unless the lane itself is redesigned.

## Use Rule

Use the support ring to sharpen, test, or restore the main line.

Do not let support material displace the helix as the primary interpretive surface, and do not jump directly from support material into transaction drafting without resolving the carrier layer first.

## Authority Guard

- `helix.md` remains the primary China interpretive surface
- first-wave strand files remain supporting source-bearing lane surfaces
- transaction authority remains in the China lane
- bridge doctrine resolves posture ambiguity; it does not replace the lane
