# China Civilization Seed Patterns


These seeds translate CIV-MEM into China-lane civilization patterns. State rules should draw from these patterns and the China empire folder, not directly from CIV-MEM.

## Order Against Chaos

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/CIV–CORE–CHINA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/CIV–DOCTRINE–CHINA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–DYNASTY–XIA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–DYNASTY–SHANG.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–DYNASTY–ZHOU.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–DYNASTY–QIN.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–DYNASTY–HAN.md`.
- **Pattern:** Order is the legitimacy test; disorder is the warning sign that power has failed.
- **Statecraft use:** Frame proposals as anti-chaos mechanisms that stabilize energy, supply chains, and regional escalation.
- **Failure mode:** A visibly ambitious move creates the disorder China claims to prevent.

## Humiliation And Containment Memory

- **CIV-MEM pointers:** `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–RELEVANCE–CHINA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/CIV–STATE–CHINA.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–WAR–FIRST–OPIUM.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–WAR–TAIPING–REBELLION.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–WAR–BOXER–REBELLION.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–ROC.md`; `research/repos/civilization_memory/content/civilizations/CHINA/MEM–CHINA–TAIWAN.md`.
- **Pattern:** China resists terms that make it look managed, contained, or denied its restoration.
- **Statecraft use:** Give China stabilizer language, face-saving discretion, and material influence without public subordination.
- **Failure mode:** A useful deal becomes unacceptable because it appears to ratify U.S. primacy.
