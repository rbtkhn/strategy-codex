# Rome Secondary Sources - Medieval

WORK only; not Record.

## What This Secondary Shelf Is For

Use this shelf after the medieval Rome primary shelf when Christianization, Byzantine continuity, papal emergence, or transformed-carrier reading need stronger chronology or anti-decline correction.

## Core Clarifiers

- Peter Brown, for Christianization, late antique transition, and continuity through transformation.
- Judith Herrin, for Byzantine continuity and Eastern Roman carry.
- Anthony Kaldellis, for the Romanity of Byzantium and a strong correction against dead-empire misread.
- Runciman and related medieval Eastern Roman synthesis, used cautiously for transition readability rather than sole authority.

## Counterweights And Corrections

- Kaldellis and late-antique scholarship, as a correction against reading Byzantium as merely derivative or post-Roman.
- Papal-history scholarship, as a correction against turning medieval Rome into a pure church shelf without imperial and legal residue.

## Use Rule

Stay with the primary shelf when the issue is canon law, ecclesial wording, Byzantine self-description, or papal self-claim. Open this shelf when continuity through transformation is being misread as collapse, or when Byzantine and papal carriers are being wrongly separated from Romanity.
