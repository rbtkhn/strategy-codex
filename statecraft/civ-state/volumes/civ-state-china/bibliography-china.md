- *Shijing* / *Book of Odes*
- *Analects*
- *Mencius*
- *Daodejing*
- *Sunzi Bingfa* / *Art of War*

- Tang and Song legal-administrative compilations where they directly carry bureaucratic and imperial continuity

### Colonial

### Industrial

### Cybernetic

- Later PRC constitutional, party, and state-strategy texts bearing reform, restoration, and current carrier logic
