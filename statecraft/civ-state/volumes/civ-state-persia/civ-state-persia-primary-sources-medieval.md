Medieval retrieval priority:
- Islam as transformed continuity, not civilizational death
- Shahnameh and Persian literary transmission as major civilizational doors
- science, law, and administrative continuity as real carriers
- Safavid Shi'a state form as sovereign reconcentration after diffuse Persianate centuries
- Mongol rupture as major shock without displacing Persian reconstitution
