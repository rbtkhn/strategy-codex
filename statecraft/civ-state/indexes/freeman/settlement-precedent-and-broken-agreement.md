# Settlement Precedent And Broken Agreement

## Purpose

Provide a `civ-state` source scaffold for the Freeman theme in which durable settlements depend on architecture, trust, and implementation rather than on ceasefire optics alone.

## Backward Historical Question

How do durable settlements fail, hold, or get hollowed out over time when neutrality, recognition, minority protection, and postwar architecture are weak, violated, or politically unowned?

## Arc Pattern

Settlement durability depends on more than stopping fire. It requires recognizable carriers, acceptable status terms, minority or sovereignty protections where relevant, and an implementation logic that later actors can inherit without immediately repudiating.

Short form:

`truce or treaty form -> political carriers -> implementation burden -> trust or hollowing -> durable order or recycled conflict`

## Continuity / Transformation / Current Carrier / Failure Mode

- Continuity: major settlements recur around recognition, neutrality, borders, guarantees, and who enforces the peace
- Transformation: armistice can become stable order, frozen confrontation, or theater depending on whether the broader political architecture matures
- Current carrier: state negotiators, security guarantors, minority-rights institutions, and successor governments that must inherit the bargain
- Failure mode: ceasefire replaces peace, guarantees are not believed, one side pockets the gain, or the agreement is openly violated until trust collapses

## Statecraft Use

Use this scaffold when drafting or testing:

- neutrality formulas
- minority-rights guarantees
- implementation sequencing
- settlement versus ceasefire distinction
- review clauses for broken-agreement environments

## Counterweight

Historical settlement memory can become misleading when actors borrow prestige from old treaties without matching the institutional burden those settlements required.

Counterweight rule:

`do not cite durable-settlement prestige unless the modern draft has comparable carrier and implementation logic`

## Transaction Hooks

- ask whether the draft has carrier clarity or only conference rhetoric
- ask whether a ceasefire line is being mistaken for a political settlement
- ask what happens after first violation or first transfer of government
- ask whether trust has already been degraded by prior dishonored agreements
