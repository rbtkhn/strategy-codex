# Marandi routing

Purpose: make the Marandi speaker object fast to route without duplicating host chronology.

## Core rule

Marandi should usually be opened when a crisis needs to be translated into:

- Iranian legitimacy and red-line logic
- coercion resistance and failed intimidation
- selective Hormuz leverage and Gulf complicity
- sovereignty, endurance, and postwar regional-order claims

The default first-open lane is usually [Dialogue Works x Marandi](/C:/dev/strategy-codex/codex/speakers/nima/stream/nima-marandi-speaker-arc.md), but not every Marandi question is best opened there.

## When not to open Marandi first

Do not open Marandi first when the task is primarily:

- neutral technical verification
- U.S. constitutional or domestic-liability routing
- document-centered diplomatic readout comparison from the non-Iranian side
- purely operational ORBAT or engineering correction

Open another lane first in those cases, such as:

- [Barnes shelf](/C:/dev/strategy-codex/statecraft/civ-lens/barnes/README.md) for U.S. domestic-liability, executive-process, and war-powers exposure
- [Mercouris shelf](/C:/dev/strategy-codex/statecraft/civ-lens/mercouris/README.md) for document-centered diplomatic and escalation interpretation
- [Davis host shelf](/C:/dev/strategy-codex/statecraft/hosts/davis/README.md) for engineering-adjacent, operational-feasibility, and coercive-failure routing from the U.S. side

## Boundaries

- Use this note for routing speed, not for chronology ownership.
- Marandi is best when the question is `how Iran narrates legitimacy, red lines, complicity, and survival`.
- If the question is instead `who authorizes, who pays, or who politically survives on the U.S. side`, open the [Barnes shelf](/C:/dev/strategy-codex/statecraft/civ-lens/barnes/README.md) first.
- If the question is direct readout comparison or stream-native non-Iranian escalation interpretation, open the [Mercouris shelf](/C:/dev/strategy-codex/statecraft/civ-lens/mercouris/README.md) first.
- The May 26 file is a real mature-cluster Marandi entry point, not just a late appendix.
- The May 28 Judging Freedom capture is useful as support-tier U.S.-audience translation of the Marandi line, but it does not displace the three-host mature core or the Dialogue Works default first-open rule.
