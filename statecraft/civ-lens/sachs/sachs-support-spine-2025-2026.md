Purpose: explain why Sachs is canonical now, why the shelf is still intentionally lighter than a month ladder, and what would justify later extension.

## Why Sachs Is Canonical Now

Sachs is no longer just residue because three things are already true:

- there are direct transcript-bearing or transcript-rich 2026 archive anchors across more than one host context
- the same speaker object is visibly stable across `Diesen`, `Judging Freedom`, `Mercouris`, and `Tucker`
- live statecraft routing already needs him as a scale-changing complement to Barnes, Johnson, Marandi, and Postol

That is enough for a canonical shelf.

## Why The Shelf Stays Lighter

The shelf should still remain lighter than a Johnson-style month ladder because:

- host arcs still own most chronology more cleanly than a speaker-native month stack would
- the Napolitano branch is materially real but still reinforcing rather than co-equal with Diesen
- the Mercouris and Tucker branches are honest support but still compact and do not yet justify speaker-native monthly synthesis

So the right current shape is:

- canonical speaker identity
- routing discipline
- compact provenance bench
- explicit helix doctrine
- support spine that blocks symmetry pressure
