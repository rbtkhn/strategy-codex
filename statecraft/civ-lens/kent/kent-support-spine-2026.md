## Growth rule

Future Kent growth should follow this order:

1. thicken provenance and routing truthfully
2. add bounded month or cluster notes when one period becomes clearly reusable
3. add a helix or support-ladder only when cross-host repetition becomes dense enough to compare host-conditioned functions rather than merely list them

Do not reverse this order by inventing mature structure before the corpus earns it.
