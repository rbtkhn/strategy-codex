# Postol Support Spine 2025-2026

Purpose: explain why Postol is canonical now, why the shelf is still intentionally lighter than a month ladder, and what would justify later extension.

## Why Postol Is Canonical Now

Postol is no longer just residue because three things are already true:

- there are direct transcript-bearing 2026 archive anchors across more than one host context
- the same technical-correction object is visibly stable across `Davis`, `Diesen`, and `Dialogue Works`
- live statecraft routing already needs him as the engineering-truth complement to Barnes, Marandi, Johnson, and Pape

That is enough for a canonical shelf.

## Why The Shelf Stays Lighter

The shelf should still remain lighter than a Johnson-style month ladder because:

- host arcs still own most chronology more cleanly than a speaker-native month stack would
- the direct materialized archive run is real but still compact
- some of the broader density is still visible through inventory and support-tier evidence rather than a fully captured transcript body

So the right current shape is:

- canonical speaker identity
- routing discipline
- compact provenance bench
- explicit helix doctrine
- support spine that blocks symmetry pressure

## What Would Justify Extension

Extend beyond this lighter grammar only if one or more of the following becomes materially true:

- more direct transcript-bearing Postol captures are materialized across the spring 2025 to spring 2026 run
- `Diesen x Postol` becomes thick enough on disk to rival Davis as a first-open chronology lane
- `Dialogue Works x Postol` grows from a hot pressure branch into a denser recurring archive body
- the notebook starts needing a bounded Postol month ladder often enough that host-local entry is no longer cleaner

## Growth Rule

Grow this shelf by density, not by symmetry.

The next good extension would likely be one bounded support note or a thicker raw-input bench, not an automatic `2026-01` through `2026-05` ladder.
