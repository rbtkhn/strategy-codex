## Role In Current State Form

Putin functions in the Russia lane as the visible presidential carrier of state continuity, security depth, and recognition demands. Treat the profile as an authority-carrier check, not as biography.

## Civilizational Pattern Link

Putin is the current presidential / security-state carrier of post-Soviet [Russia state memory](../civilization/objects/state-memory.md): supreme authority as custodian of state continuity after collapse, encirclement, and recognition loss. Use this profile to test how that deeper pattern is carried now, where it is institutionally supported, and where a transaction would depend too heavily on Putin's personal tactical choice.

## Authority Base

- Formal authority: presidency, security command, diplomacy, appointment power.
- Informal authority: elite balancing, personal legitimacy, wartime symbolism, continuity narrative.
- Security / military authority: command relationship with defense, intelligence, and security institutions.
- Coalition authority: security elites, administrative state, regional governors, industrial and energy interests.
- Symbolic authority: sovereignty after collapse, resistance to Western coercion, restoration of recognition.

## Institutional Carrier

Durability depends on the presidency plus security institutions, Foreign Ministry, Defense Ministry, Security Council, energy state, and legal recognition formulas.

## Domestic Coalition

The leader must reassure security elites, nationalist publics, military institutions, economic managers, and partners who need Russia to remain predictable.

## Strategic Temperament

Russia-facing instruments should expect preference for ambiguity, phased recognition, linkage, and security formulas that avoid visible retreat.

## Red Lines

Concessions that look like acceptance of encirclement, betrayal of sacrifice, loss of strategic depth, or submission to Western deception can break carrying capacity.

## Settlement Credibility

- Can sign credibly: recognition formulas, security guarantees, mediator arrangements, sanctions workarounds, phased settlements.
- Needs institutional carrier: military implementation, intelligence channels, energy arrangements, Security Council consensus.

## Succession / Continuity Risk

Medium to high. A settlement must be framed as Russian state interest, not only Putin's tactical choice.

## Binding Instrument Test

Is the leader expressing the Russian state, capturing the state, or failing to carry a settlement that the state requires?
