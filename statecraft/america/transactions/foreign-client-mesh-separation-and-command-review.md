## Realism / Incentive Filter

- Does the alleged actor benefit more than it loses? Current carriers benefit from client mesh short-term (lobby continuity, executive flexibility) but lose sovereign comprehension, CI integrity, and Gulf/host credibility.
- Is the target something the actor still depends on? Washington still depends on basing geometry, alliance rhetoric, and executive war-initiation latitude — but June 6 bench says those dependencies are overstated relative to protection delivered.
- Is the act consistent with demonstrated behavior? Yes — NDAA integration proceeds while DIA elevates CI threat; deals route to Netanyahu after emissary rounds (Freeman); military obedience to law expected even when law degrades command (McGovern).
- Accident / negligence / private actor alternative: Not primary — capture is institutional and congressional, not a single rogue operator.
- Evidence needed: Senate passage of mesh clause; uniformed implementation memos; measurable base-use restraint in Kuwait/Bahrain; verifiable Lebanon gate movement before Hormuz normalization.

## Solvency / Legitimacy Filter

- Protected domestic obligation: constitutional war authority, counterintelligence integrity, public consent to foreign military entanglement.
- Creditor or patron with extraction leverage: foreign-client lobby bloc + executive patronage chain treating client preference as default veto.
- Sovereign asset at risk: unified command chain, CI compartmentalization, successor-stable war-powers grammar.
- Legitimacy-destroying reform risk: statutory merger of foreign client into U.S. command without reciprocal loyalty, borders, or advance-notification obligations (1973 treaty rejection logic).
- Minimum public-order floor: Congress retains separable authorization; intelligence community can name CI threat without being forced to share operational mesh.
- Face-saving assistance phrase: **command firewall**, **burden-separated cooperation**, **review before mesh** — not "abandon ally" or "retreat."

## Section 224 Statutory Mesh (Hoh Pass)

**Bench receipt (June 4):** NDAA **Section 224** creates a DoD coordination agent over R&D, industry, and procurement integration with a non-treaty client. Text is "incredibly short… incredibly vague" (Hoh). Netanyahu "wrote to the member of Congress to put this section 224 into the bill" (Khanna clip via Hoh). Effect: Israelis embedded "from start to finish" in Pentagon processes — "circulating in the bloodstream" — so assistance is "already baked into the Pentagon" and "Congress doesn't have to vote on sending weapons or sending money" (Hoh).

**Drafting implication:** Firewall must treat Section 224-class language as **successor-stable capture** unless sunset, NOFORN preservation, and vote floors are explicit. Performative Trump–Netanyahu rupture (Hoh: Axios daylight) does not unwind statutory mesh.

**Cross-lane:** Persia [Lebanon gate transaction](../../persia/transactions/lebanon-third-party-recognition-gate-transaction.md) Clause B requires **statutory/mediator carriers** for U.S. assurances — Section 224 is the inverse problem (assurances to client without vote).

## Instrument Text (Draft Clauses)

### Clause A — Section 224 moratorium and sunset

> No integrated military coordination agent with a non-treaty foreign state shall take effect under defense authorization law until a **180-day command-firewall review** reports on CI tiering, NOFORN compartment integrity, procurement embedding scope, and congressional vote requirements. Any such provision shall carry a **statutory sunset** not exceeding 24 months and require **affirmative reauthorization** by separate recorded vote in both chambers.

**Face-saving line:** **burden-separated cooperation review** — not alliance rupture, but pause before permanent Pentagon embedding.

**Hoh anchor:** Khanna — "should we do more for Israel than we already do or should we do less?" at the moment Netanyahu "arrogantly telling Donald Trump what we should do."

### Clause B — NOFORN and CI-tier gate

> While a foreign state remains designated a **critical counterintelligence threat** by the defense intelligence enterprise, no waiver shall permit that state's personnel or institutions **embedded integration** into U.S. procurement, R&D, or operational planning above **FMS/training-only** separation. **NOFORN** and compartment rules shall not be relaxed for mesh coordination absent treaty-equivalent loyalty, border, and advance-notification obligations (1973 mutual-defense rejection logic per Macgregor/McGovern).

**Face-saving line:** **tiered security cooperation** — assistance continues through bounded channels that preserve U.S. command identity.

**Hoh anchor:** integration "supersedes" NATO/Five Eyes patterns; would "take away" NOFORN protections.

### Clause C — Congressional vote floor (anti–Pentagon-bake)

> Assistance to a non-treaty client routed through Pentagon coordination, procurement co-development, or pre-integrated stockpiles shall require **annual recorded authorization** identifying type, value, and purpose. Appropriated NDAA totals alone shall not satisfy the vote requirement for new assistance categories obscured by coordination agents (Hoh: assistance hidden so "Congress doesn't have to vote").

**Face-saving line:** **transparent security cooperation ledger** — restores accountability without ending cooperation.

**Aguilar cross-pass:** executive NDAA bucket reallocation after client red-line breach requires **notification and 30-day hold** before obligation (see Executive levers unused below).

### Clause D — Client-originated clause disqualification

> Provisions in defense authorization originating at the written request of a foreign head of government or that foreign state's lobby channel shall be **flagged for enhanced review** and shall not proceed to floor vote without Foreign Affairs and Armed Services committee **separate majority** on record. Origination disclosure shall be public summary form.

**Face-saving line:** **sovereign legislative hygiene** — America calls the shots in its own statute process.

**Hoh anchor:** Netanyahu "wrote to the member of Congress to put this section 224 into the bill."

### Clause E — Performative daylight bar

> Executive–client public rupture communications shall not substitute for **Clause A–D** compliance. Emissary deals altering regional strike or basing use require **congressional notification within 72 hours** (Freeman referendum fix) regardless of concurrent media reports of presidential anger.

**Face-saving line:** **coordinated security messaging with review gates** — separates rhetoric from lawful carry.

**Hoh anchor:** Axios-style leaks give supporters "daylight" that "never was" (Biden/Trump parallel).
