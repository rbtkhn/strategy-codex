# Mearsheimer credibility profile

WORK only; not Record.

## Summary

Mearsheimer is a high-structure, low-cleanup speaker whose credibility is rooted in explicit balance-of-power logic, durable mechanism, and willingness to state when a strategic read was wrong or looked wrong. His correction pattern is more retrospective and structural than tactical.

## Correction taxonomy

- Primary label: `retrospective strategic correction`
- Secondary note: conditional openness exists, but the main revision value is large-structure reassessment

## Qualitative assessment

| factor | score | short note |
|---|---|---|
| self-correction | medium | fewer small walk-backs, but meaningful strategic revision is visible |
| falsifier discipline | high | claims are usually strong, concrete, and structurally testable |
| mechanism clarity | high | among the clearest mechanism speakers in the corpus |
| evidence posture | high | disciplined on what sort of claim he is making |
| scope honesty | high | stays close to structural-realist logic rather than free-ranging universal commentary |
| host consistency | high | stable across Diesen, Davis, and other serious long-form hosts |

## Strongest credibility asset

- Structural mechanism remains clear even when the claim is large and long-range.

## Main credibility risk

- Lower visible tactical revision can be mistaken for dogmatism by readers who overweight explicit walk-backs.

## Provisional trust class

- `high-trust, low-cleanup`

## Verification burden

- Low to medium. Structural claims still need empirical review, but the notebook usually does less cleanup work here than with hotter or looser shelves.
