# Ritter credibility profile

## Summary

Ritter is a mechanism-heavy escalation and war-feasibility speaker whose credibility comes from explicit causal language, operational consequence, and unusual willingness to admit direct wrongness. He is also host-sensitive: the same strengths that make him vivid can become evidentiary liabilities under hotter live-war pressure.

## Correction taxonomy

- Primary label: `collective wrongness, not personal correction`
- Secondary note: also shows `explicit factual correction` in smaller event-detail cases

## Qualitative assessment

| factor | score | short note |
|---|---|---|
| self-correction | high | includes both morally weighty collective admissions and smaller factual walk-backs |
| falsifier discipline | high | claims are usually strong enough to be genuinely disprovable |
| mechanism clarity | high | force, logistics, escalation, and consequence chains are explicit |
| evidence posture | medium | evidence quality varies sharply by host heat and event speed |
| scope honesty | medium | can drift from hard military logic into wider civilizational certainty |
| host consistency | medium | core mechanism survives, but closure quality drops on hotter hosts |

## Strongest credibility asset

- Strong mechanism plus direct admission of wrongness under consequential pressure.

## Main credibility risk

- Live-war heat can widen claims faster than the source base closes.

## Provisional trust class

- `medium-trust, mechanism-strong`

## Verification burden

- Medium. Use quickly for mechanism and warning structure, but recheck factual and magnitude claims before public reuse.

## Native adjustment notes

- Collective admission is a real credibility signal here because it is tied to an operational and moral error, not just abstract hindsight.
- Heat should only count against Ritter when it degrades evidence posture, not because the tone itself is sharp.

## Boundary

- This profile is not rewarding intensity for its own sake.
- It is rewarding explicit mechanism and visible revision, while keeping a clear verification burden attached.
