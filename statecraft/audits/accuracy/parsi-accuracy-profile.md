## Synthesis

- what this speaker tends to get right: coercion-failure logic, sanctions and leverage sequencing, and the structural requirements of a durable bargain
- what this speaker tends to miss or overstate: timing around quick walk-backs and how fast coherent architecture will force political adaptation
- whether the speaker revisits errors: not visibly enough in the current shelf to count as a strong revision habit
