# Speaker Credibility-Accuracy Bridge

WORK only; not Record.

This file exists to keep the notebook from collapsing two different judgments into one.

## Core Distinction

- **Credibility** asks: how much provisional trust should the notebook extend before every claim is independently closed?
- **Accuracy** asks: how well did a bounded claim survive later review?

Both matter. They are not interchangeable.

## Combined States

### High credibility / high accuracy

- strong mechanism
- real falsifiers
- visible revision
- claim ledger closes well

### High credibility / mixed accuracy

- trustworthy method
- clear mechanisms
- some misses or mistimed calls
- strong value for reuse despite imperfect hit rate

### High credibility / low visible accuracy closure

- method looks strong
- current claim window is still too open or too macro
- do not confuse slow closure with poor method

### Low credibility / occasional accuracy

- sometimes right
- weak justification, blurred evidence, or inflated scope
- notebook should not over-trust the next claim just because one landed

### Rhetorically strong / audit-weak

- memorable and forceful
- thin correction pattern
- weak falsifier structure or evidence posture

### Accurate but under-justified

- claim landed
- mechanism was thin, vague, or derivative
- future reuse still needs caution

### Credible but wrong

- method strong
- revision honest
- claim missed
- still often better for reuse than a lucky but sloppy speaker

### Provisionally credible, still underclosed

- mechanism good
- evidence decent but incomplete
- verification burden still visible
- do not over-promote or under-credit

## Anti-Confusions

- `admission` is not the same as `hedge`
- `conditionality` is not the same as revision
- one visible correction is not the same as a correction habit
- being right once is not the same as being reality-tracking
- agreement with the notebook is not credibility
- rhetorical heat is not discrediting unless it damages evidence, falsifiers, or scope

## Working Rule

When the two surfaces diverge, ask:

1. Is the speaker wrong for understandable, bounded, testable reasons?
2. Did the speaker revise honestly when disconfirmed?
3. Did the original claim have a real mechanism and a visible disproof path?
4. How much cleanup work does the notebook still have to do before reuse?

That is usually more important than raw hit count.

- open [speaker-credibility-matrix.md](speaker-credibility-matrix.md) when trust terms are the main question

## Boundary

This file is not a third scoring system. It is a translation aid between two separate audit layers.
