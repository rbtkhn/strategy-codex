# Doctrine-Hardening Law

WORK only; not Record.

Purpose: name how a corpus should harden into doctrine without letting elegant
interpretation outrun authority, surface truth, or falsification.

## When To Use

Use doctrine-hardening law when:

- a corpus is starting to produce method notes, hidden-doctrine notes, route
  notes, continuity notes, or interpretive commentary
- several candidate notes feel related, but they do not all carry the same
  authority
- coherent work may be landing on the wrong surface

## The Authority-Layer Distinction

Separate these before promotion:

- architecture
- routes
- continuity hypotheses
- interpretation

This solves: "the notes are individually strong, but together they are quietly
becoming a shadow constitution."

This is not: a ban on cross-reference or a demand that every note live alone.

## The Falsification Question

Before promotion, ask:

```text
what is the strongest claim here,
and what survives a direct falsification pass?
```

This solves: "the synthesis is compelling, but I do not yet know which part is
architecture-enacted and which part is extrapolation."

This is not: a requirement to attack every minor claim with equal intensity.

## Wrong-Surface Guardrail

Treat wrong-surface coherence as a real failure mode.

```text
coherent implementation
!= correct implementation surface
```

This solves: "the work is internally consistent, but it is hardening at the
doorway, router, or commentary layer instead of the layer that actually bears
the claim."

This is not: a reason to discard good work that only needs to move to the right
surface.

## What Counts As Misuse

The pattern is being misused when:

- topic proximity is treated as enough reason to commit different authority
  layers together
- route notes quietly begin functioning as architecture
- continuity hypotheses harden as if they were already settled doctrine
- interpretive commentary is allowed to outrank the public reader contract
- the "wrong surface" test is used as a vague taste objection instead of a
  concrete layer check

## Statecraft Reuse Rule

```text
When a corpus matures into doctrine,
separate architecture, routes, continuity hypotheses, and interpretation by
authority layer;
falsify the strongest claim before promotion;
and treat wrong-surface coherence as a real failure mode.
```
