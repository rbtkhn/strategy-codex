# Dual Structure Model

Narrative Systems assumes a dual decomposition:

## Structural Axis (EG)

Represents:

- spatial relationships in discourse
- alignment geometry
- cross-agent comparison

## Temporal Axis (PH)

Represents:

- evolution over time
- narrative drift
- retrospective reinterpretation

## Key Insight

Neither axis is reducible to the other.

NST exists in the interaction space between them.
