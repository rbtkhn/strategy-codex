## Assumptions

- Moonshots rhetoric is **synthesis material** — speculation stays marked; orbital compute, RSI pause, personhood law, and market-return claims need explicit confidence boundaries.
- No investment advice: market framing is analytic input, not recommendation.
- A valid helix pair requires dated IL signal **or** explicit archive cite — not thematic vibe alone.

## Open research questions

1. **Consequence topology** — Which episode threads recur across economy, agency, infrastructure, alignment, culture without collapsing into one "AI will change everything" bucket?
2. **Panel vs monologue** — How does multi-guest structure change what can be treated as load-bearing vs performative?
3. **Emergency vs scheduled** — Do unscheduled episodes (e.g. Anthropic pause framing) warrant different decay / pairing rules than numbered releases?
4. **Statecraft crossover** — When does export control / access governance belong in singularity-science vs statecraft lanes?
