## Assumptions

- IL issues are **evidence prompts**, not settled claims — vendor stats and release rhetoric stay bounded until verify.
- Research outputs are interpretive; they do not replace archive verbatim or loop YAML.
- Acceleration fronts may be tagged before Moonshots synthesis echo is found; absence of echo is a signal, not a failure.

## Open research questions

1. **Front clustering** — Which recurring fronts (compute, agents, robotics, biotech, markets, institutions, meaning) co-move across issues without collapsing into undifferentiated "AI news"?
2. **Cadence vs gravity** — When does high issue frequency on a front indicate structural shift vs publicity cycle?
3. **Signal decay** — How long does a dated IL item remain load-bearing for research models before it becomes background?
4. **Helix pairing** — For a tagged front, what counts as a valid Moonshots synthesis echo vs thematic adjacency? (See [../moonshots/synthesis-research-frame.md](../moonshots/synthesis-research-frame.md).)
